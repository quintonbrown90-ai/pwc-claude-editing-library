## The rule

If the first email is a pitch, you taught them to ignore you. Before doors open, send SETUP emails — weekly, no pitch — so opening Q's emails becomes a habit. When the sales email lands on Dec 7, a warmed list actually reads it.

Pipeline this plugs into: **FOUND** (exact-words cards) → **WARMED** (this sequence) → **CLOSED** (Dec 7 sales email).

## Every setup email has exactly four parts

1. **A subject line that makes them curious** — a moment, a number, a tension. Never "Newsletter #4", never the pitch.
2. **One story or one lesson** tied to what the launch teaches.
3. **One thing they can use TODAY** — a tip, a number, a quick fix. Real value, complete, not a teaser.
4. **One bottom line** reminding them when doors open. One line. That's the whole "pitch" until Dec 7.
