# OUTREACH TEMPLATES — Q's voice, their words, approval-gated

The technique worth copying from the source reel is structural: **quote the lead's own words back → name Paid With Content as the fix → invite to the waitlist → stop.** Being quoted reads as being heard; the message works because it is built from their sentence, not from a template with a name swapped in.

**Every message below is a DRAFT pattern. Nothing written from these templates is sent without Q's approval — drafts are prepared in batches, held, and only approved sends go out, paced and human-sent. No exceptions, no "quick batch" carve-outs.**

## Facts block (hard-code — never improvise around these)

- Doors are **LOCKED** — nothing opens before **Mon Dec 7, 2026** (founding cohort). No "join now," no early access, no waitlist-skipping.
- **Premium $97/mo ONLY** — the only tier and the only community price ever quoted.
- **Waitlisters get an extra month free** — the waitlist incentive, always stated.
- Founding window closes **Dec 13, 2026**.
- Route to the waitlist = **comment WAITLIST** on @yerrr__q (the ManyChat flow delivers). Outreach sends from @yerrr__q only.
- The **hidden UGC earnings app is never named** — not hinted at by name, not "the app I always talk about" with a name attached. It is revealed only inside the Premium community.
- Q's proof is his real record (Q + Kiya, ~1.5M followers combined, 125 brand campaigns, close to half a million earned from content) — never inflate it, never borrow another creator's numbers.

## Template 1 — DM opener (paced, personal, post-approval)

> Saw your post where you said "[THEIR PAIN, VERBATIM]".
>
> That's exactly what Paid With Content fixes — it's the community my wife Kiya and I built for small creators getting real UGC brand deals. Doors are locked right now — we reopen **Dec 7** for a founding cohort, Premium only ($97/mo), and **waitlisters get an extra month free**.
>
> Comment **WAITLIST** on my page and you're in line. No pitch beyond that — the waitlist is the move.

Rules on the opener: one message, their quote untouched inside the quotation marks, no second message chasing in the same sitting, no link unless the flow asks for one. If their card has no PAIN quote, there is no opener — a card without a verbatim pain is not outreach-ready.

## Template 2 — Follow-up (only to someone who replied or opted in)

> You told me "[THEIR WORDS FROM THE REPLY / ORIGINAL POST, VERBATIM]".
>
> That's the [dream — in their words] path we teach inside — [one concrete line on the mechanism, e.g. how brands actually pick small creators] is the part most creators under 10K never get shown.
>

Follow-ups exist only inside a real conversation (they replied) or an opt-in rail (they commented WAITLIST / bought the Calendar). Silence is never followed up from a cold card.

## What never appears in outreach

- "Join now" / doors-open / early-access language of any kind before Dec 7
- A Standard tier, a discount, or any price other than $97/mo Premium (community) / $35 (Calendar)
- The hidden app's name, or earnings claims beyond Q's real record
- Their words altered — if the quote doesn't fit the sentence, rewrite the sentence, never the quote
- Volume language ("I'm reaching out to a few creators…") — every approved send reads 1:1 because it is built 1:1
