# STAN COPY BLOCKS — voice-of-customer copy for stan.store/yerrr__q

The lead-card bank rewrites the storefront: the avatar's own sentences do the selling. Every quote used on a page comes from the bank — verbatim, sourced — the same honesty rule as the cards. Patterns below are structures; the words inside the quotation marks always come from a real card.

## 1. Pain-led Calendar headline (the flagship pattern — PWC Content Calendar, $35)

Structure: **their pain sentence as the hook → the Calendar named as the fix → the dream as the outcome line.**

> **"I post whenever I remember and my page is dying."**
> The PWC Content Calendar is the system that ends random posting — what to post, when, and why, built for creators who don't have hours to plan.
> Post like somebody headed somewhere: **[their dream sentence, e.g. "first $1K month from UGC"]** starts with a page that never goes quiet.

Rotate the pain quote from the bank (consistency / no-system / panic-filming variants); never write a fake "customer voice" line. If the bank has no quote for a slot, the slot uses Q's own words about his own experience — never an invented testimonial.

## 2. Objection block (under the price — the pattern)

Short block, their objection in their framing, answered in Q's voice. Rotate from the bank's OBJECTION fields for Calendar-fit objections:

- **"I don't have time to plan content."** → The Calendar is the plan. It's undated and built for people with jobs and kids — Q films with five kids asleep in the next room. Planning is the thing it replaces, not another task.
- **"I tried planning before and fell off."** → Falling off a system designed for full-time creators isn't a discipline problem. This one is for-dummies simple on purpose — if you can post, you can run it.
- **"I'm not consistent enough for this to work."** → Consistency is the output, not the entry fee. The Calendar carries the streak until it becomes habit.

Price-fact guardrail for every Stan block: **Calendar is $35.** Standing FOMO framing is fair game — most people charge double what Q charges ($35 Calendar / $97 Premium) with way less value and no action steps. No other price, discount, or tier may appear. **The hidden UGC app is never named on any Stan page.**

## 3. Waitlist bridge (inside the Calendar delivery / thank-you step)

Every Calendar delivery carries the next step — the download is the front door, the cohort is the house:

> **You're in. One more move:** Paid With Content — the community behind this Calendar — is locked right now. It reopens **Mon Dec 7** for a founding cohort, **Premium only ($97/mo)**, and **waitlisters get an extra month free**.
> Comment **WAITLIST** on @yerrr__q to hold your spot. Window closes Dec 13.

The bridge appears in the download/thank-you delivery and in the buyer follow-up (OUTREACH-TEMPLATES.md, Template 3) — same facts, same wording discipline. It never promises the Calendar buyer a seat, a discount on the community, or early entry: the waitlist is the offer, the extra month free is the incentive.

## 4. Rebuild rule

When the bank gains a stronger verbatim pain/dream/objection, the page blocks above are candidates for a refresh — drafted as a diff for Q's approval like any other copy change. Pages are never rewritten silently off the bank, and a quote pulled from the bank keeps its source noted in the working doc (not necessarily on the public page).
