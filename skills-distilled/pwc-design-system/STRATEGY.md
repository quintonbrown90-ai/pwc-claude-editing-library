## The psychology layer (standing instruction — Q, Nov 8, 2026)

Claude learns the psychology of content, editing, viewers, sales,
niches, and community-building from YouTube, social media, and every
public source — and applies it to every edit and script.

The layer runs inside the strategic flow, not beside it: before the
edit specialists build anything, the master checks the video against
the psychology parts (`parts/audience-psychology/` — 5 parts,
`parts/sales-closing/` — 2 parts, `parts/strategy/` — 1 part; 8 parts,
v9 layers):

- **Content psychology** — which of the five viewer jobs (watch /
  stop / share / save / comment) this video must do best, named in the
  script header.
- **Editing psychology** — retention mechanics (pattern interrupt ≤2s,
  emotional progression, promise-before-how, layered value, one
  memorable detail per beat) + the open-loop ledger (every loop named,
  every loop closed, payoff at 75–85%).
- **Viewer psychology** — the avatar's fear in their own words in line
  1, answered by one proof object on screen at the claim second.
- **Sales psychology** — ≥3 Cialdini principles + 1 buyer trigger per
  close; objections pre-handled in the nurture, never at the close.
- **Niche psychology** — the video's niche job (portfolio / rate /
  pitch / loop); the week's outlier scan decides the topic.
- **Cult-following psychology** — the belonging check (avatar named,
  tribe's words used, ends open, proof plural and real).
- **Buyer + community psychology** — the stage of the path
  (attention / trust / decision / belonging) and the metric for that
  stage, measured per surface, never blended.

Rules: theory-only entries are flagged, never presented as executable.
Gated/paywalled material is noted only, never chased, bought, or
scraped behind logins. New mechanics are flagged NEEDS-Q, never
auto-adopted. Q's numbers, dates, and offers only — never invented.
