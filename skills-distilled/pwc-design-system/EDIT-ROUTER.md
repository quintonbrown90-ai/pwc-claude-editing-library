# EDIT ROUTER — the best edit for whatever video it is

**Purpose:** given ANY video Q hands over (real take, avatar script, carousel,
UGC brief, series episode), this router picks the edit — style family,
devices, motion set, caption treatment, audio chain, QC gates — and says WHY
in three grounds (see §6 RECOMMENDATION). Load this FIRST on every edit job,
before any lane skill. It sits above all lane skills; lane skills execute,
this file decides.

**Hard rules (outrank everything in this file):** nothing over Q's face, ever.
Palette teal #14A886 / gold #FEE238 on navy #00214E, NO RED. CapCut-native
motion (renderer plates never substitute). Full self-watch before delivery.
Post the exact exported file, no re-encode. Real footage/photos/screens, never
AI-flat. No door device in any form. Words never clipped. Every claim gets its
proof on screen at the claim second. Keyword must have a live ManyChat flow
before the video posts.

### E. Psychology-aware device picks (which device for which viewer state)

The master reads the viewer's state first, then picks the device. A
device that lands on the wrong feeling is worse than no device.

| # | Viewer state (what the viewer feels right now) | Device pick | Why (psychology part) |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Skeptical** ("prove it") | Proof full-screen in first 2s; gold circle draw-on around the figure; zoom kick on the number | Proof closes the gap before doubt forms (`psychology-content-why-people-watch.md`) |
| E2 | **Curious but uncommitted** (leaning in) | Contrarian snapback line + open loop named on screen; payoff withheld to 75–85% | The loop is the debt that keeps them watching (`psychology-open-loops-payoff-timing.md`) |
| E3 | **Frustrated** ("posting for free, nothing to show") | Frustration beat first (their words), then the system as relief; emotional synchrony — the video's feeling matches theirs at that beat | Synchrony before solution (`psychology-viewer-avatar.md`) |
| E4 | **Aspirational** ("I want that") | Plural proof wall (other small creators) + future pacing ("Day 90: deal-ready") | Identity shift: they see themselves in the wall (`psychology-cult-following.md`) |
| E5 | **Ready to act** (commented, DM'd, waitlisted) | Value-stack end card ("YOU GET ✓…") + keyword restated in the video's own language + one action, one sentence | Reciprocity + commitment: small yes before big yes (`psychology-sales-closing.md`) |
| E6 | **Inside the room** (member, month 1–3) | Hot-seat framing (their page, worked on live) + visible progression marker (where they are on BUILD → CREATE → MONETIZE → SCALE) | Belonging + visible progression keep members (`psychology-buyers-community.md`) |

Rule: the emotional beat map (one feeling word per section) is written
BEFORE the device plan. No feeling word = no section. The beat map is
part of the RECOMMENDATION (§6) — the master states the viewer state it
is designing for, in one line, before naming any device.

## 2. The 11-question intake (when the type is ambiguous)

Ask these (of the video itself first — transcript, script, brief — then of Q
only if still ambiguous). The answers select the row.

1. Is Q on camera, an avatar, or neither (carousel/graphic-only)?
2. Does the video teach ONE named system/framework? If yes, what is its name (2–4 words)?
3. What is the single claim the hook makes, in one sentence?
4. What proof exists for that claim (real screen, figure, example, third-party reel, none)?
5. Is the power in a number, a story, a proof, or a breakdown?
6. Which lane: launch, proof-led, avatar, carousel, UGC brand, series, nurture?
7. What is the comment keyword, and is its ManyChat flow live?
8. What must the viewer DO at the end (comment, follow, join waitlist, buy)?
9. Is there a real document/screen/example the video must SHOW (not describe)?
10. What would make this video fail — what must NOT happen in the edit?
11. What does the viewer FEEL right now (skeptical / curious /
    frustrated / aspirational / ready to act / inside the room) — and
    which psychology part answers that state? The answer selects the
    §1.E row before any device is picked.

If Q answers "I don't know / just make it good": default to the row matching
the lane + proof type, run the Sky Form 2 / Adilet B1 house default, and say
so in the recommendation (§6).

## 4. Motion sets (named, never "animate it")

Every move in a build is named from the shared vocabulary (MOTION.md in each
skill): LINEAR, EASE IN, EASE OUT, EASE IN-OUT, OVERSHOOT, SPRING, BOUNCE,
STEPS — plus the family presets: Sky spring-overshoot pop (0.3s), Adilet
SPRING / WHIP PAN / ZOOM KICK 1.6× / STAMP SLAM / scramble count-up,
designed-captions overshoot+spring+glow, effects per-effect spec. A build
whose motion is described only as "animated" is not a build — name every move
and its trigger word.

## 5. The NEVER list (applies to every row)

- Never anything over Q's face — at any zoom, punch, or device scale.
- Never red. Never neon green. Rejects/dismissals are grey + strike/dim.
- Never AI-flat: no generic stock/AI photos for proof; real screens, real
  photos, real examples (illustrations only in the Adilet avatar grammar).
- Never the door device — no door card, animation, or transition, in any form.
- Never a clipped word — cuts land on sentence boundaries or device beats only.
- Never a bare talking head when a real example/screen/diagram exists for the claim.
- Never a fabricated number, fake analytics, or a redrawn fake screen.
- Never a caption group straddling a cut, overlapping a graphic, or overlapping another caption.
- Never a board/card fading or washing over the face; boards enter on hard cuts.
- Never music over Q's uploaded reels unless he asks for that specific post.
- Never post without a live ManyChat flow for the video's keyword.
- Never claim a style is "like Sky/Adilet" without the frame-sequence grade to prove it.

run as ordered steps, not background processes.

The recommendation is not a suggestion box — it is the decision record. If
the pick changes mid-build, the recommendation is re-issued with the new
grounds. If a ground is weak (e.g. no viral evidence for the style this
week), say so instead of inventing it.

## 8. QC gates (every build, on the exported file)

- Freezedetect (n=0.003, d=0.35): zero freezes ≥0.35s in footage spans.
- ±0.15s frame audits around every device/card/board entrance and exit.
- Last spoken word → end card ≤ ~1s.
- Face zone empty at every scale; safe zones x 70–930, y 280–1480.
- Per-word fit audit: no clipped words at any cut.
- Retention read: hook lands claim/curiosity ≤2s with face on screen; open
  loop early + second before midpoint; visual event ≤2s; every section ends
  on a mini-payoff or new question; ending chains to the next watch.
- Frame-SEQUENCE grade vs the model reel for the picked form (matched beats,
  in motion — never stills alone).
- Full self-watch at normal speed before delivery; delivery report states
  what was checked and what could not be.

### 8a. HUMAN-MADE CHECK (the final gate — Q's @rpn bar)

Q's standing rule: "don't make it look ai made at all, make me wanna know
how I did it." A render that reads "template" FAILS this gate even if it is
technically correct — every gate above can pass and the video still fails
here. Run it on the exported file, as a viewer, before any render ships:

- **No uniform rhythm:** sections do not run at the same beat in the same
  order. If every section is hook → diagram → stamp in identical timing,
  break the rhythm (vary a beat length, a device order, a pause).
- **Caption treatments vary:** the treatment changes across sections (the
  designed-captions lane switches per phrase; the Sky lane varies weight
  by era, never mixed in one video). One flat caption style for a whole
  video fails.
- **Transitions are motivated:** every cut, zoom, pan, and device entrance
  is motivated by the content (a gesture, a spoken word, a proof landing)
  — never by the template's schedule.
- **Examples are real:** every example is a real screen, a real creator, a
  real number. No generic stock, no AI-flat plate pretending to be proof,
  no invented figure.
- **Sound is dense but organic:** SFX on every in/out, varied (no single
  loop repeating), bed low and real. Silence where a device lands fails;
  a metronome-identical tick on every word fails.
- **Nothing frozen or stamped:** nothing sits still, nothing is a static
  plate held while the voice runs. Motion on everything; every device
  moves in AND out.

Verdict in one line: "A viewer would ask how I did it" — or FAIL, with
the exact fix named. A FAIL blocks delivery until fixed and re-checked.

## 9. BRAND DNA — the fingerprint that makes it HIS

Full spec: `design-system/BRAND-DNA.md`. Training set:
`examples/brand-dna/` (one worked before/after per major system).

**Fingerprint (6 markers, every video, checkable on the export):**
F1 PWC palette on screen (teal/gold/navy, zero red) · F2 PWC logo present,
unaltered · F3 Q's face clear + present in the hook (first 2s) · F4 Q's
caption voice for the lane · F5 PWC sound identity (SFX on every in/out,
cleared bed) · F6 PWC close (completed device + keyword CTA with live flow,
