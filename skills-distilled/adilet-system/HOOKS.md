## The meta-pattern [MEASURED]

His hooks are **claims with a number, a threat, or a secret** — never a
greeting, never "in this video." The first line names the viewer's pain or
the size of the prize, and the *second* line (also in the hook beat)
promises the mechanism ("here's exactly how"). Median video on the account
earns ~170 likes; every pattern below is ranked by what actually broke out.
