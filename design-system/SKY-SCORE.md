# SKY-SCORE — the Sky-beating feedback loop

**The benchmark:** Sky is the standard. Every new learning (new creator,
new kit, new video, new prompt, new playbook) gets APPLIED BACK to the Sky
edits and must answer: **does this make our Sky match closer, or BETTER
than Sky?** The goal is stated plainly: **match Sky perfectly, then beat
him** — and the same for every other style mastered: learn it, match it,
then exceed it with the PWC skin on top.

The weekly sweep updates these score cards when new material lands
(CONTINUOUS-LEARNING.md). A device's level moves only on measured evidence
(a frame-matched grade, a verified build, a live post's performance) — never
on a feeling.

## Levels
- **0 — NOT BUILT:** we have not built this device in Q's lane yet.
- **1 — APPROXIMATED:** built, but from the spec alone (parameters [EST]
  or [NEEDS-BROWSER-CONFIRM] in the Sky skill).
- **2 — MATCHED:** frame-matched against the model reel, in motion, on a
  shipped Q video.
- **3 — BEATING:** matched AND improved — faster, clearer, or more
  retentive than Sky's own version, with the PWC skin on top (measured
  where possible: retention, completion, or a frame-level comparison).

## Rules
- A level moves only on measured evidence. "Looks closer" is not evidence.
- When a new learning touches a device, its row gets an "Applied back"
  note with the date — even if the level does not move yet.
- Beating Sky never means breaking PWC law: palette, logo, face-clear,
  CapCut-native, human-made check — all still bind at level 3.
