## 0. THE BAR

- **Sky exact is the FLOOR. Ship better than Sky's own execution of his system.**
- Grade every edit against its named Sky model reel, frame-SEQUENCE side-by-side in motion
  (hook build, first device entrance, one mid-video state-change, one verdict/count-up
  landing, the close). The question is one sentence: **does this read as their style or as
  ours?** Any matched pair that reads as ours FAILS and gets rebuilt — never explained away.
- PASS requires BOTH: at-or-above the reference on fidelity AND above it on execution
  quality (timing / sound / motion polish). Nothing ships ungraded.

## 1. BEFORE YOU BUILD

1. Pick the FORM and FAMILY first. One form per video (desk-framework / autopsy /
   split-screen reaction / enumerated types / live workflow demo / chart hybrid / POV /
   founder vlog). One family per video: GREEN→teal (data, numbers, systems, charts,
   checklists) OR ORANGE→gold (beliefs, identity, story, funnels). Never mix accents.
2. Coin Q's framework name (2–4 words, ownable) + list its parts (3–6; 4–5 is the sweet
   spot). Pre-select each part's example asset. A part without an asset gets merged or cut.
3. State the plan header: `family`, `devices=[...]` with the Sky source reel for each
   device, and every event's beat time. Vary the device set + rhythm per video — the same
   components in the same rhythm as the last video is a FAIL. Each device ≤2 uses per video.
4. Confirm the CTA keyword's ManyChat flow is LIVE before building the close. No live flow
   = the video is held. Current facts: doors LOCKED until Mon Dec 7, 2026; Premium $97/mo
   only; keyword WAITLIST (waitlisters get an extra month free).

## 2. A-ROLL + FRAMING

- Canvas 1080×1920, 30 fps. Medium shot, face upper-middle, empty space above the head +
  chest band reserved for devices. Dim, quiet background.
- Silence-compress the take FIRST (inter-word gaps ≈0.15 s; dramatic pauses rare; performed
  visual intro/outro bookends stay whole). Build every graphic timing on the FINAL cut.
- Cuts are rare and structural. Zoom, where the form uses it, is mild: 105–110% MAX,
  keyframed at definition/verdict/close lines, back to 100% at the section boundary. Never
  punch in on a graphic state-change frame (one motion at a time). Some Sky forms are one
  locked-off take with zero zoom (the origin form) — match the model reel, never assume.
- NOTHING ever covers Q's face, at any zoom/punch scale. Safe zone x 70–930, y 280–1480.
  Behind-the-head depth is allowed: back graphics behind Q's cut-out, front graphics +
  captions in front.

## 3. CAPTIONS (D1) — NON-NEGOTIABLE

- Transcribe the take's ACTUAL audio (word timings from the transcript). Captions follow
  the SPEECH. Never write captions/hooks from on-screen frame text.
- Default (Df-era): **1–3 words per group**, never a full sentence, groups change every
  **~0.5 s**. White #FCFCFC, bare text — no box, no pill, no stroke. ONE weight per video
  (bold for doctrine/teaching, light/regular for process/casual). Cap height ≈50–62 px.
- Position: centre-lower chest band, chin + ≥40 px clearance, never over the face, inside
  the safe zone. (Measured anchor: face box ≈ x345–735, chin ~y1170 → captions ~y1230–1245
  on a Sky-framed take; recompute from Q's actual face box per take.)
- Motion: **IN = hard cut to full size in ≤1 frame (≤0.033 s). No scale-in ramp, no typing
  effect, no letter-by-letter — ever.** One group at a time; the previous group is fully
  gone before the next lands. No group straddles a device state-change — re-segment so the
  boundary and the device beat coincide. No word ever clipped: one clipped word = FAIL.
- NO current-word highlight on captions. The accent word is a separate headline device
  (D2), not a caption colour. Captions stay all-white.
- Dcc-era variant ONLY when the video names template beats/figures the origin way: 2–5
  words/group (median ~0.6 s), accent word INSIDE the caption in gold (hook accent /
  current beat name / key figures). Never mix the two variants in one video.

## 5. TIMING — BEAT-LOCK (binding)

- **Everything on beat:** captions, motions, visuals, visual motions, sounds, transitions —
  every planned event sits on a beat grid derived from the take's own audio (word stresses
  from word timings + detected music beats where a bed exists).
- **Target: ≤1 frame (0.033 s) tolerance.** An event outside tolerance FAILS validation
  unless the plan carries an explicit `off_beat_reason`. Report the on-beat percentage per
  build. (Sky's own best instances are frame-exact: donut pop 1 f on the figure, figures
  1 f on the numbers, caption cuts ≤1 f — build to his best, not his average.)
- Walk the hero device part by part: at each part's FIRST spoken word, fire its
  state-change (opacity 40→100% over 0.15 s + its device animation) and land its example
  asset at the exact claim word. Numbers land ON the spoken digit, never before it.
- Mid-video reset at 45–55% of runtime (pivot headline / chart's second act / document
  reveal / branch).
- Text handoff: next top-zone text starts ≥0.5 s after the previous one's effective end;
  hero held until last word + 0.9 s.
- Pacing: caption rhythm ~0.5 s never stops; a visual event every ≤2 s via caption groups +
  real state-changes (never fake devices); graphic-free time only on punch-in / B&W beats.
- Dead air is banned: cut silences to the flow standard; **last spoken word → end moment
  ≤ ~1 s**. Sky's one shipped dead tail (1.37 s) is a defect to beat, not a style.

## 6. MOTION + TRANSITIONS

- Entrances: pop/scale ~0.3 s with slight overshoot (glass cards); panel in 7 f scale
  .88→1 no blur; child builds 6 f on the spoken word, stagger ≥3 f; active panel line
  pulses (glow ~2.5×, ~0.3 s). Connectors draw AFTER their nodes land.
- Exits: **hard cut.** Inserts exit with motion-blur slides where measured (D34 heavy blur
  is mandatory there). Fades exist in exactly three measured spots (D2 Dcc-variant 5 f
  exit, D31 10 f exit, D17 fade-out) — never generalise them into a fade/dissolve grammar.
- **No idle float** on landed panels/boards/charts. Only small floating elements (head
  cutouts, D10 inserts) bob gently.
- Transitions: silent hard cuts are the norm; most reels barely cut. Whip/blur-frame
  transitions exist (measured, silent) — use only where the model form does. NEVER put a
  whoosh on a cut and call it Sky.
- Exact easing curves were never frame-resolved — do not claim a curve is "Sky's". Polish
  comes from frame-exact timing, not from template easing.

## 7. HOOKS, PROOF, ENDINGS

- SAY IT, SHOW IT: proof on screen at the claim second, by second 3 — view count, profile

- Close: complete the hero device ON the closing line; hold the completed state 0.8–1.5 s;
  spoken door over the completed graphic; Q's logo/keyword end moment ≤1 s after the last
  word. Rotate Q's ending forms (`card` / `comments` / `notifs`). **No door device in any
  form** — no door card, animation, or transition. PWC logo (transparent cutout) in every
  video. Finishing lift on every final: `eq=gamma=1.10:brightness=0.015` once, whole frame.

## 8. SOUND

**Sky's measured grammar (know it before you diverge):** dry voice base; NO music bed in
any talking-head reel; cuts are near-silent (no whoosh/pop on any cut, anywhere); ordinary
device landings are silent EXCEPT a faint gated **tick-tone family** synced to animated
counters — 5,926 Hz pulse trains (~13–17 pulses/s) on timers/count-ups/chart draws, gated
~9.15 kHz tones on two opening builds, ~11.1+11.85 kHz two-tones on two graphic landings —
plus rare isolated SFX (a UI blip on a stats-card swap is the type case). No dead air:
zero silences ≥0.25 s is his norm.

**Q's build law (the divergence — apply it, label it):**
- Voice: static gain only, to −14.5 LUFS. Built videos: cleared bed ducked ≈ −28 dB under
  speech, local licensed library only. Uploaded reels: original audio only.
- **SFX on 100% of device entrances AND exits** (Sky lands some devices silent — beat him
  here): tick/pop on landings + exits, stamp on verdicts, riser + ding on counters/charts,
  and reproduce the tick-tone itself on counters/timers/count-ups/chart draws. Vary volume
  + pitch per event; `sfx_gain` ≈ .6. Source sounds from the licensed local pools (§9) —
  never invent a sound Sky's grammar wouldn't carry, and never ship a generic tick as "the"
  tick-tone without an ear match (no verified local match exists yet — flag it if you
  substitute).

## 12. QC GATES (all must pass on the EXPORTED file)

1. **Motion grade:** frame-sequence side-by-side vs the named model reel (§0). One failing
   beat = the grade fails.
2. **Freezes:** freezedetect (n=0.003, d=0.35) — zero freezes ≥0.35 s in footage spans.
3. **Entrances/exits:** audit ±0.15 s around EVERY device/card/board entrance + exit —
   hard cuts only for boards over footage; plate + text land together; nothing crosses the face.
4. **Ending:** last spoken word → end moment ≤ ~1 s.
5. **Lip-sync:** ffprobe container offsets (decisive) + eye-check strips at quiet→loud
   onsets. Never shift audio on a correlation score alone.
6. **Beat-lock report:** on-beat percentage at ≤1 frame tolerance; every off-beat event
   carries an `off_beat_reason` or fails.
7. **Per-word fit + no-overlap:** every caption/graphic word fully visible at every cut;
   no text over a graphic, no pop over a pop, no card over a card; text-handoff ≥0.5 s.
8. **System checks:** framework name ≤10% of runtime; hero device visibly unfinished until
   the close; every planned device present in the export (a missing device fails even when
   technical checks pass); one accent only; logo present (cutout); blur law applied;
   keyword flow live.
9. **Retention read:** claim/curiosity within 2 s with Q's face on screen; open loop early
   + a second before the midpoint; visual event every ≤2 s; every section ends on a
   mini-payoff or a new question; ending chains to the next watch; no sagging middle.
10. **Full self-watch:** watch the ENTIRE export at normal speed before delivery.
11. **Delivery report, per file:** CapCut project/draft name · motion-grade result per
    beat · freeze count + longest freeze · entrances/exits audited count · last-word→end
    gap · per-word fit result · beat-lock percentage · any `[EST]` parameters relied on.
    A cut with any gate unrun is reported as UNVERIFIED, never as verified.
