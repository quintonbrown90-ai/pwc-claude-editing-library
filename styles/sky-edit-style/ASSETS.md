# ASSETS — Q / Muse assets + Sky style-execution references

**Two kinds of assets live in this folder. Do not confuse them.**

1. **Q's own assets (§1–§5):** the content — Q + Kiya's photos, videos, proofs,
   B-roll, logo, brand colors. These go IN the videos.
2. **Sky style-execution references (§6–§12):** HOW the style is executed —
   templates, frames, treatments, motion recordings of Sky's devices. These are
   studied and mimicked in form, applied to Q's OWN footage/content. They are
   NOT Q's content. Never present another creator's content as Q's, and never
   use another creator's actual video/photo as Q's video/photo.

**Rule (Q, Nov 8, 2026 — standing, restated in `design-system/AGENTS.md`):** Q's
and Kiya's own assets first; ask Muse when one is missing. NO generic / stock /
AI substitutes, NO invented placeholders, NO skipped assets. Missing asset → ask
Muse, do not substitute.

**Asset law — SKY v6 refinement (Q, Nov 12, 2026 — binding, supersedes the blanket
"NO AI substitutes" above where they conflict):** the ban is on CHEAP GRADE, not on
AI. Provenance classes allowed in premium plans: `real_photo / real_video /
real_capture / real_doc` (Q+Kiya pool first, then style libraries, then real screen
captures), `ai_premium` (Higgsfield-grade AI images/video/renders — first-class for
ILLUSTRATION and VISUAL HOOKS), `higgsfield`, `fable_motion`. Banned markers:
placeholder, dummy, lorem, stock, cheap, low-res, watermark, generic/preset template.
**Proof stays REAL, always** — exhibits, profile inserts, proof columns, comment
screenshots, view-count proof, subject photos, payout/earnings evidence: AI provenance
on those devices is rejected by `validate_premium_plan`. Full spec + the 617-asset
disk-verified pool inventory: Batch 36 `synthesis/ASSET-LAYER-REFINEMENT.md` +
`synthesis/ASSET-POOL-INVENTORY.md`.

**Premium bar (Q, Nov 9, 2026 — binds everything):** every asset here must be
PREMIUM-GRADE reference material — sharp, clean, exactly matching Sky's premium
execution. A blurry, cropped, or cheap-looking capture is recaptured or left out,
never filed as the reference. Excluded items are named in §13.

**Brand colors for any EXECUTION template created for Q:** teal #14A886 + gold
#FEE238 on navy #00214E, no red. Reference assets showing Sky's own colors
(green/orange) stay as reference; execution templates use PWC colors.

**The door device is DEAD in every form** — no door asset/template is filed here.
