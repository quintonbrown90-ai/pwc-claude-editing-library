## The form — 6 beats over 12 slides

1. **CLAIM HOOK (slide 0).** The invention claim + the money result + the offer, in huge type over a CGI spectacle visual (glowing figures, falling money). The deck opens on the biggest possible promise, visually loud.
2. **THE STUCK CLIENT (slides 1–2).** Who the format was built for and their plateau (~5K views despite doing the work): a testimonial endorsement + a grid of their low-view thumbnails. The grid IS the proof of stuck — evidence, not adjectives.
3. **THE INVENTION PROCESS (slides 3–5).** Criteria before creation: the format had to be (a) something people care about, (b) visual, (c) progress-showing. What got rejected and why (scripts, CTAs, descriptions = boring). The spark: one existing "POV"-style reel that showed the strength to build on.
4. **THE PACKAGING TEARDOWN (slides 6–9).** One real video dissected layer by layer — the designing, the mid-action shot, the environment (clothing rack). Inspiration credited (a GaryVee podcast). Behind-the-scenes of directing it live, with the revenue dashboard climbing as proof-in-motion.
5. **THE RESULT (slides 10–11).** Directing POV (hand gestures, storyboard sketches), then the payoff grid: the three videos with their view counts (66.7K / 56.1K / 10.4K), and the opening claim repeated verbatim as the close.

## Design grammar (what makes it a type, not a one-off)

- **The claim bookends the deck** — slide 0 promises it, the final slide repeats it over results. Everything between is the evidence chain.
- **Every slide is a different evidence type:** CGI spectacle → testimonial → thumbnail grid → criteria text → spark artifact → teardown layers → BTS photo → revenue dashboard → sketches → results grid. No two slides ask the eye to do the same job.
- **Teaching lives in the middle** (criteria + teardown), so a services pitch reads as a case study. The sell is the frame; the value is the filling.
- **Process is shown, not summarized** — sketches, mid-action frames, live directing. The unfinished artifacts are the credibility.
