# B-ROLL — the B-roll types Sky uses

**Sky's B-roll rule (verified across the 15):** B-roll is *evidence*, not decoration.
Every cutaway proves the spoken point in the moment it is spoken, then hard-cuts
back. Exhibit/demo clips do NOT freeze while he talks over them (D9). B-roll is
rare — most reels have ONE cutaway, some have none — which is why each one lands.

## B-roll grammar

- Entry: HARD CUT (never a fade). Exit: hard cut back to Sky (D9 exhibit inserts
  exit with motion-blur slides in some cases).
- Duration: 0.6s (tennis) to ~4.5s (burger, dice). Short = punch; the long ones
  are the satisfying payoffs.
- Text ON the B-roll: one big line, accent color, center-left (tennis) or center
  (burger "Zero Value"). Captions keep running over B-roll.
- No B-roll in most reels: DfX2 has exactly one cutaway; DfQ4 has one; DfU49's
  "B-roll" IS the subject's footage. If a Q build has B-roll every 2 seconds it
  is not Sky — Sky's density comes from *devices*, not cutaways.
- **Q lane:** B-roll is Q's own footage/proofs/B-roll first (standing rule — ask
  Muse for the asset pool). Sky's B-roll above is the *type* reference, never the
  footage to reuse.
