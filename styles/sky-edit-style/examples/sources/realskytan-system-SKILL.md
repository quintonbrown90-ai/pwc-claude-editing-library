## 1. Identity — when this lane applies

- **Name:** SKY-15 SYSTEM (Q adaptation: PWC SKY LANE).
- **Use when:** Q films a teaching, doctrine, proof-led, breakdown, or launch talking-head and the video teaches ONE named system.
- **Do not use when:** couples content, pure story/vlog with no system to teach, or any video whose power is a single emotional beat (use Form 12's confessional *pacing* only).

## 2. The common recipe — executable rules

1. **One named framework per video.** The name is coined before the shoot, appears on screen within the first 10% of runtime, and the video is that framework *filling in*. No name = no Sky lane.
2. **The hero graphic is visibly unfinished until the payoff.** A board, stack, index, chart, or timeline stays on screen (or returns at every section) with its missing parts obvious. The unfinished visual is the open loop — the viewer can *see* what is still missing. Completion = the visual payoff, timed with the closing line.
3. **A graphic state-change at every section boundary, and an example at every claim.** No claim is spoken over a bare talking head when a real screen, example clip, or diagram state exists for it. Proof lands at the moment of claim, never as a later montage.
4. **Two text layers, never merged:** (a) the caption stream (D1) — speech only; (b) the graphic layer — framework names, devices, figures. Captions never carry the framework; graphics never transcribe speech.
5. **Proof at the moment of claim.** A view count, a profile grid, a retention graph, a calendar screenshot, or an example reel appears on screen in the same beat the claim is spoken.
6. **Other people's footage is the B-roll library in breakdowns.** Authority comes from dissecting a real third-party reel on screen (its view badge visible), not from claiming wins.
7. **CTA is structural, not shouted.** The close is a door built into the last labelled section: the framework offered as a take-home via a comment keyword (door 1) and/or a follow for the series (door 2). Never an unrelated end-card blast. See §7.
8. **Series logic.** The same framework grammar repeats across episodes; formats are deliberately re-runnable. Consistency of the device templates across episodes is what makes it read as a system. Build devices once as CapCut draft templates and duplicate per episode.
9. **Dark, quiet visual world.** Dim set, dark wardrobe; the set never competes with the graphic layer. Q translation: keep the near-black/navy world, swap Sky's accent dialect per §5.
10. **The framework name or the result figure is on screen inside the first 10%**, face present from frame one, no greeting in-video.

2. **Face is sacred:** nothing over Q's face at any zoom or punch scale. Devices live in the chest band, face-clear side zones, and the top stack (see rule 3). Verify the face zone empty at every scale used.
3. **IG safe zone:** all text/graphics inside **x 70–930, y 280–1480 on the 1080×1920 canvas**. Sky's top title stack (D3) sits at y 6–15% in his lane — in Q's lane it **shifts DOWN: title baseline starts at y ≥ 300**, pill below it at y ≈ 360–440. Nothing in Q's Sky lane may sit above y = 280.

Captions never overlap graphics or each other; one caption group at a time, fully gone before the next lands.
5. **PWC logo** appears in every video, unaltered (`~/workspace/pwc_course_video/assets/pwc_logo.jpg`).
6. **Words never clipped:** cuts land on sentence boundaries or device beats, only in silence with the full word envelope surviving; caption groups never straddle a cut mid-word. One clipped word = internal fail, blocks delivery.
7. **Real screens over drawings:** profiles, grids, analytics, and documents are real screen recordings (D10) — never redrawn fakes, never fake analytics. Drawn diagrams (D4–D7, D12, D13) are built from CapCut native shapes/text — never ffmpeg/PIL plates.

10. **No door device:** no door card, door animation, or door transition in any form — even though "doors" language appears in launch speech and the CTA is described as a "door" structurally.
11. **Sound — VERIFIED DIVERGENCE (Q override):** Sky's audio is **dry voice ONLY in every reel heard (VERIFIED, browser, Nov 6)** — no music bed, no SFX on device landings, no named track on any reel page. ### D1 — Speech caption stream (the constant)
- **What:** White sans-serif caption groups, centred, at chin/chest height. 1–3 words per group, never a full sentence (observed groups: 1 word ×2, 2 words ×4, 3 words ×2 across caption-visible thumbnails).
- **Size:** Cap height ≈ 2.6–3.2% of frame height ≈ 50–62 px at 1920 (CapCut font ≈ 54–64 on a 1080-wide canvas). A 3-word group spans ≈ 18–24% of frame width.
- **Placement:** Sky's baseline band y ≈ 44–58% of frame height (y ≈ 850–1110/1920). Q: chest band, under the chin with ≥ 40 px clearance, always inside y 280–1480, never on the face.

- **Colour:** #FFFFFF, no background box, no highlight pill. **Current-word highlight: NONE on Sky's own captions in any reel watched — VERIFIED (browser, Nov 6).** Highlight fires only inside *embedded* content: client captions get a green phrase highlight (DfSf5v8j73o); rewrite lists highlight the new word green / old word orange (DfLaEFNDKkv — colours remapped in Q's lane, see D8). **Q lane: caption groups stay all-white.** Do not import Q's karaoke-gold caption system into this lane.

## 8. Production recipe — pre-production → shoot → edit (E1–E10) → QC

### Pre-production
- **P1.** Coin the framework name (2–4 words, ownable: a Filter, a Fit, a Stack, a Split, an Anatomy). Write it on the shoot brief.
- **P2.** List the framework's parts: 3–6 (4–5 is the sweet spot — the 15 use 3, 4, 5, 6, 7; above 6 only for the caption-system meta format). One part = one section = one device state. Budget the mid-video reset (E7) here.
- **P3.** For each part, pre-select its **example asset** (real screen, clip, figure, or rewrite pair). A part without an asset gets merged or cut.
- **P4.** Write the hook from one of the four proven formulas (§8.1). Write the close as the framework's completion line + door.
- **P5.** Draft the post caption's two-door version (§7) *before* the edit, and confirm the keyword's ManyChat flow is live or scheduled to be live before posting.

### Shoot
- **S1.** Medium shot, face in the upper-middle, **empty space above the head and at the chest band** reserved for D3/D1. Dark/quiet background; the set never competes with the graphic layer.
- **S2.** Deliver part-by-part with a breath at each boundary — the editor's state-changes need clean sentence edges.

### Edit (CapCut, native only)
- **E1.** A-roll on Track 0. Silence-compress to Q's flow standard (inter-word gaps ≈ 0.15 s, dramatic pauses rare) *before* graphics, so every device timing is built on the final cut.
- **E2.** Captions: Auto Captions → re-segment to **1–3 words per group** (D1 spec) → apply the locked Q Sky-lane caption style (one weight per video — bold era A for doctrine/teaching, light era B for process/casual; bare text + shadow/stroke per §5.4) → position chest band, face-clear.
- **E3.** Hook headline: D2 lockup (teaching/statement videos) or D3 title stack (numbered/framework videos) lands **with the first spoken line**, ≤ 0.3 s in-animation.
- **E4.** Build the hero device — pick ONE primary: D4 board / D5 index / D6 chart / D12 timeline / D13 strip. Place it in the top stack (y ≥ 300) or a face-clear side zone. Set every part to its pending state (40% opacity).
- **E5.** Walk the timeline part by part: at each part's first spoken word, fire its state-change (opacity keyframe 40→100% over 0.15 s + its device animation from §6) and insert its example asset on the overlay track at the exact claim word.
- **E6.** Punch-ins: keyframed A-roll scale at the definition line, each part's verdict line, and the close — **capped at 105–110%**, returning to 100% at the next section boundary. **Sky's own zoom is mild, ≈ 5–10% MAX — VERIFIED (browser, Nov 6); most of his reels are ONE continuous take, and the rhythm comes from graphic swaps ≈ every ~10 s per section, NOT camera cuts.** Hard cuts in Q's Sky lane stay rare and structural. Never punch in on a graphic state-change frame (one motion at a time). Face-clear rule holds at every punch scale.
- **E7.** Mid-video reset: at 45–55% of runtime, one larger event — the pivot headline (DeYKkAIgVfL, DfDuMnCjNdE), the chart's second act (DfPxqFPAY4_), the document reveal (DexJ-qWiWdY), or the branch (Dez4fPJCR74).
- **E8.** Figures: every number that is *argued* (not just mentioned) gets a figure state (D6/D7 count-up or landing). Numbers land on the spoken digit, never before it.
- **E9.** Close: complete the hero device (final state-change) *on* the closing line; hold the completed state 0.8–1.5 s; spoken door (keyword/follow) over the completed graphic; Q's logo/keyword end moment ≤ 1 s after the last word.
- **E10.** Sound (Q override, §5.11): cleared bed ducked ≈ −28 dB; SFX tick/pop on every device landing and exit; stamp sound on verdicts. No device appears or leaves silently.

## 9. Motion QC checklist (run on the exported file, in motion — all must pass)

**Sky-system checks**
- [ ] Framework name on screen ≤ 10% of runtime; hero device visibly unfinished until the close.
- [ ] Every part: state-change fires on its first spoken word; example asset present at its claim; no claim spoken over a bare talking head when an asset exists.
- [ ] Captions: every group 1–3 words, one weight all video, none over the face, none straddling a cut, none overlapping a graphic or another caption.
- [ ] Side-by-side frame-**sequence** grade vs the model reel for this video's form (matched beats, sequences not stills): reads as Sky's *system* in Q's colours — or it doesn't ship. Rebuild the failing section, don't explain the difference.
- [ ] Per-word fit audit: every caption and graphic word fully visible, none clipped at any cut. One clipped word blocks delivery.
- [ ] Every required device from the build plan present in the export; a missing device is a fail even when technical checks pass.
- [ ] Carousel twin exported from the same device states (framework reels).

**Q's standing gates (on the exported file itself)**
- [ ] Full decode clean; sampled frames across the whole length — no black, frozen, or missing-picture sections.
- [ ] **Freezedetect (noise 0.003, duration 0.35): zero freezes ≥ 0.35 s** inside footage spans.
- [ ] **±0.15 s frame audits around every device/card/board entrance and exit:** no board fading or washing over the face, no title crossing the face, no blank plate landing before its text, plate + text land together. Boards over footage enter on hard cuts.
- [ ] **Ending dead-air: last spoken word → end card ≤ ~1 s**, measured on the export.
- [ ] Face framing matches the source take's proportions (no squeeze/stretch); face zone verified empty of graphics at every zoom/punch scale used.
- [ ] Framing/picture check confirms the intended take (background, clothing, lighting match the take Q sent).
- [ ] **Full self-watch:** the editor watches the entire export at normal speed before delivery. Speed never waives this.
- [ ] Delivery report states, per file: CapCut project/draft name, motion-sequence grade result per beat, freeze count + longest freeze, number of entrances/exits audited, last-word→end-card gap, per-word fit result. A cut with any gate unrun is reported as unverified, never as verified.
