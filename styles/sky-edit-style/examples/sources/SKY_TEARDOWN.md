## 2. The cross-cutting Sky system (present in almost every form)

Before the individual forms — these are the constants that make a cut read as "Sky" regardless of form:

1. **One named framework per video.** Every teaching post is built around a single coined, named system (a filter, a loop, an anatomy, a method, a numbered taxonomy). The name appears on screen early and the video is the framework filling in. Structure is the retention device; editing executes it.
2. **The diagram builds as he teaches.** Lists resolve item-by-item, meters fill (a "TRUST" meter going 0/4 → 4/4), charts gain rows, a spine gains vertebrae. The unfinished visual is the open loop — the viewer can *see* what is still missing.
3. **Proof at the moment of claim.** A view count, a client's profile grid, a retention graph, a calendar screenshot or an example reel appears on screen in the same beat the claim is spoken — never as a later montage.
4. **Other people's footage is the B-roll library.** Breakdowns and reactions dissect a real third-party reel on screen (with its view badge visible). Authority comes from dissecting winners, not from claiming wins.
5. **Dark, low-key visual world.** Dim office / night-desk settings, black hoodie/sweatshirt, glasses; graphics in a glowing **neon-green on near-black** dialect (carousels especially), white bold text with a single accent colour per concept. Set never competes with graphics.
6. **Two text layers.** (a) A *headline/graphic layer* — big, designed, statement-level text and diagrams. (b) A *caption layer* — subordinate, keeping up with speech. In carousels the graphic layer does 100% of the work (no face, no voice).
7. **The caption under the post is a second piece of content.** A fixed 7-part storytelling caption structure (Form 13) runs under nearly every reel in the survey, ending in a keyword CTA — overwhelmingly the same keyword (CONTENT) across ~40 surveyed posts, with topic-specific keywords on framework posts (e.g. SPINE, MUJI, AIOS, Crowd, format).
8. **CTA is structural, not shouted.** Link-in-bio for DFY services, a comment keyword for the free asset that *is* the video's framework in take-home form, or a follow close built into the last labelled section.
9. **Series/volume logic.** The same desk setup and framework grammar repeat daily; at least one carousel series visualises a volume method (7 formats × 4 variations). Formats are deliberately re-runnable — the style is a production system, not a one-off edit.

**Q-adaptation overrides that apply to EVERY form below** (Q's standing hard rules outrank Sky's dialect): highlights in **teal + yellow/gold, no red**; **nothing over Q's face** at any zoom; all text/graphics inside IG safe zones (x 70–930, y 280–1480 on 1080×1920); PWC logo present; words never clipped at cuts; captions never overlap graphics or each other; hidden-method proof screenshots blurred per Q's per-asset spec; earnings framed second-person/collective, never as Q's solo claim; community link is always skool.com/paid-with-content.
