## THE UNIVERSAL DNA (true of all 9 posts)
1. **Every slide is a VIDEO** (4–22s). Nothing is static. Text reveals line by line, diagrams draw themselves, bars fill, numbers land, cards populate. On Instagram these autoplay/loop as you swipe — that's the "live" feel. Slides are exported as short MP4s, 1080×1350 (4:5).
2. **One accent color per series** — either neon GREEN or burnt ORANGE, never both. Everything else is black/off-white/cream.
3. **Headline system:** a small plain kicker line, then a HUGE display headline with the one keyword in the accent color. The keyword is the framework's name or the payoff word.
4. **Every claim gets a DIAGRAM, not decoration.** A funnel, a meter, a scatter of dots, a phone mockup, a checklist — one visual system per slide that builds while you watch.
5. **Named, numbered frameworks.** Parts have names (THE HOOK, FILTER 1 OF 4, LOOP 2 OF 3). "X of Y" counters and slide counters ("02 / 10") everywhere — they create completion pressure to keep swiping.
6. **Handle watermark on every slide** (@realskytan, small, bottom). For Q: @yerrr__q + the PWC logo where the format has a badge slot.
7. **Save-bait + CTA closes.** Teaching carousels end with a "steal the skeleton" fill-in template slide (drives saves), then a CTA slide: giant quote-mark keyword ("Comment 'CAPTION'") + a mockup of the free product card + a comment-box mockup with the keyword typing itself in. Authority carousels (D, some E) skip the CTA and end on the checklist/proof visual.
8. **Slide rhythm:** cover 7–8s, teaching slides ~4s (B/C) or ~7s (D), Breakdown slides 7–22s (as long as the section's transcript needs).
