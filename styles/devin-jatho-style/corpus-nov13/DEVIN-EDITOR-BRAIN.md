## 1. The one-sentence system

Devin makes abstract business systems VISIBLE: every claim gets a screen, a grid, a list, a moving number, or a performance of the claim itself — one thing at a time, and the number and the picture are always the same event. **[36v + 6/6]**

## 3. Caption system

- **Font:** Montserrat ExtraBold (glyph-matched against Anton/Poppins across the studio corpus). Anton appears on a separate gate-reel specimen only. **[38]**
- **Body construction:** white→light-grey vertical gradient (white top → ~rgb(198,204,214) bottom), soft radial shadow. Emphasis words: 4-colour gradient + light sweep + Deep Glow ~0.2 alpha. The source stroke is heavier than stock Montserrat ExtraBold (~0.22× cap height vs ~0.17×) — a known residual gap. **[38]**
- **Shape:** two-line PHRASE CHUNKS swapped as a unit on the phrase. No karaoke word-highlighting in the studio grammar. **[6/6 priority + 38]**
- **Size:** body cap height ~57px, hook headline ~82px at 1080×1920. **[measured, batch-11]**
- **Timing:** chunks LEAD the spoken word by 0.1–0.4s, landing in the pause before the word — captions anticipate, they do not chase. **[38]**
- **Emphasis entry:** the emphasis word enters horizontally STRETCHED and motion-smeared, settling crisp in ~0.15s — a scale-X settle, never a bounce. **[motion-study, Dfc + others]**
- **Colour semantics (source):** red = warning/problem/verdict · blue = next action (pills) · yellow/gold = payoff · purple/magenta = system/tool names and revenue chips (DfV6GWMyqs0's "$194,613 → $567,123" chips are purple, frame-verified) · green = system working. **Q mapping is binding in §10.**
- **Exceptions (do not flatten):** profile-audit reels may run ZERO speech captions (the UI carries the words); comment-hub has no speech captions; performed/vlog sections use ONE-WORD hard-swapped captions (DLpgNYQMZlm apartment section, 37–43s); Dd3x4cMStzs uses true phrase captions ONLY in its final act — acts 1–2 are headline/UI text, and its three text-ups are typed/glow headline cards (the warning card reads "WARNING / NEVER POST ON INSTAGRAM" in red glow, frame-verified at 16.0s — and that text-up is literally DSh8cgtjfGX's hook: his text-ups are his own covers). **[6/6 show at least one exception or the rule]**
- **Q placement law:** baseline ~y1560 at 1080×1920, lines ≤ ~900px wide, ≥43px margins, never over the face envelope.

## 4. Board & device grammar

- **One device + one caption idea at a time.** A panel and its content land as ONE event — a panel never sits empty. **[6/6]**
- **Full-screen boards** are for: hook slams, screen recordings, matrices, fixed/final frames, proof/results interfaces. **Split layout** for walkthroughs: screen top ~55% / face bottom ~45%, instruction pills ON the seam. **[6/6]**
- **Count-ups (the signature):** accelerate-then-settle over ~1.5s while the grid/wall DENSIFIES in sync — number and picture are one event. Measured 25→625 sequence: 25, 27, 36, 50, 81, 133, 224, 343, 557, 623, 625. Revenue variant: $0→$241,069 in ~1.2s with the chart drawing as the number ticks. Eye/view count-DOWN: 60,981→175 in ~11 frames (0.46s), landing ~0.1s BEFORE the word "views". DSh8cgtjfGX extends the family: counts climb ON phone cards and profile tiles (52K→596K; followers 23,519→26,137; 61K→607K; 0→672K) — the number changes on the artefact, not in a caption. **[4 distinct count devices measured]**
- **Cursor grammar:** target travel 10–16 frames → 2-frame pause → button compresses to 97% for 2 frames → click ring expands 70%→140% over 8 frames. DSh's dashboard walk uses glowing cursor RINGS as the pointer. **[measured]**
- **Three-card comparison:** centres at x≈0.24/0.50/0.76 of frame width, each ~0.22 width; selected card scales to ~112%, unselected dim to 40–60%. **[measured]**
- **Section cards (Q adaptation):** HOOK / DELIVERY / PAYOFF stamp gold at structural breaks. (System-level reconstruction — no single decoded reel isolates this entrance; flagged honestly in the library GAPS.)
- **The fixed frame (C format):** the "after" is shown as a finished, postable frame — subject low in frame, headline pill + numbers in the empty space above; a fix can be a pure TEXT SWAP inside the held frame (old headline wipes to blank ~40.83s, new wipes in from ~41.4s, ~0.6s total, chips never move). **[DfV, frame-measured]**
- **Subtraction as a device:** the losing version is DELETED on screen (Dd3, ~3–6s); the strawman is lit RED (DSh, 23–27s); clutter is annotated then removed (DfV, 24–26s → 26–39.5s). The edit argues by removing. **[3/6]**

## 5. Motion system

- **Standard card entrance:** opacity 0, y +60px, scale 94%→100%, 8–12 frames, cubic ease-out, optional 102% overshoot. **[38]**
- **Gesture-synced cards:** FADE over ~3 frames (0.1s) — no slide, no pop. The finger LEADS: finger up ~0.1s before the word, card ~0.2s after the finger (Dff structure card: finger 34.10s, word 34.20s, card 34.27s — frame-measured). **[measured]**
- **Verdict X:** 1-frame pop, ~120%→100% settle over 4 frames (0.17s); can land ~0.28–0.47s BEFORE/AFTER the verdict word — in DLpgNYQMZlm X1 it lands 0.47s after "26." in the pause; all three DLpg X stamps are SILENT (RMS 0.02–0.04). **[measured]**
- **Split-comparison wipe:** divider holds ~46%, slides to ~62% over 3–4 frames landing ON "vs." — the strong panel eats the weak one; the wipe IS the verdict. **[measured]**
- **Other measured transitions:** wedge fly-in (tilted + motion-blurred ~6 frames/0.25s, settles upright, holds ~1.2s, hard cut out, silent) · comments sheet slides up ~17 frames/0.71s · money-bill wipe ~5 frames · RGB-split glitch ~4–5 frames · white flash (1 frame + ~0.9s fade-down; DSh uses a glow-flash variant ~4.5–5.5s) · circular iris · category-ring zoom-blur ~5 frames · hook-wheel scale-up ~5 frames · letter-badge collapse ~6 frames · countdown-timer compression (Dd3: 24:00:00 morph) · fly-to-profile-grid landing (Dd3 winner tile). **[38 + 6/6]**
- **Returns to face** often use a ~3-frame CROSS-DISSOLVE, not a hard cut (Dff, 34.07s measured). Hard cuts are for hook and device changes. **[measured]**
- **Camera:** locked tripod A-roll; movement is editorial — jump-cut reframes at phrase boundaries, divider wipes, a slow zoom INTO the mosaic wall closer (Dfc, 64–67s). There is no handheld drift in the studio grammar. **[6/6]**
- **Lighting as punctuation:** the studio background re-lights between sections (DSh: red-lit hook/strawman vs blue-lit teaching) — colour of the ROOM marks the argument's side. **[DSh frame-verified; consistent with DLpg's red room / window room / B&W restatement grades]**

## 6. Sound system — TWO families, never collapse them

**Family A — the dry studio (the majority):** voice-led, NO continuous bed; phrase gaps fall to RMS 0.01–0.04; ONE SFX per device, never stacked; no per-word ticks; a hook slam is NOT universal (DLpg's hook starts quiet, first-bin RMS 0.061; Dff's first 0.1s hits 0.44 — probable impact, RMS-only). Verdict Xs in DLpg are silent. The Dfc 25→625 count-up has NO tick track. **[Dff, Dfc, DfV, DLpg measured]**

**Family B — the bedded device reel (Dd3x4cMStzs):** a CONTINUOUS music bed under all 39.8s (overall RMS −16.0 dBFS, peak −0.87, ZERO silences at −35dB/0.15s or −40dB/0.25s); designed sounds are plausible at the Share-button shatter, Trial toggle, countdown swap, DM banner and final glitch, but they were NOT spectrally isolated in the source soundmap — treat those labels as probable, not measured. **[1 reel, fully measured profile]**

**This pass's machine profiles (0.1s RMS, see each BREAKDOWN's grid):** onset candidates (RMS jumps >2× and ≥0.09) run 33–68 per reel (Dff 33, DLpg 34, Dd3 39, Dfc 49, DfV 58, DSh 68) — these include word onsets, so they are an UPPER bound on designed SFX, never an SFX count. Near-silence spans (<−38dBFS, ≥0.25s): DLpg 4, DSh 1, all others 0 at that strict threshold — the dry reels breathe between phrases without hitting true digital silence. **[6/6 measured this pass]**

**Placement rules that survive both families:** sound follows the DEVICE, not the word (one event = one sound); subtractions and verdicts can be silent on purpose; the CTA is performed (typing/send/banner) and its sounds belong to the performance; beds, when used, live under SCREEN/UI stretches, not under face A-roll.

## 7. Structure & pacing

- **Spine:** hook slam (comparison or problem shown) → old way / diagnosis → the system built on screen → payoff shown as a finished artefact → CTA performed as a mock (comment types → sends → DM banner lands) OR a cliffhanger cut mid-sentence (DfV "but this…", DSh "you shouldn't…") — Devin often withholds the ending to loop or sequel. **[6/6]**
- **Visual-state cadence by format:** M every 1.5–3s · S every 3–5s · C every 5–8s · performed/D about every 8s · M-hook ~3.4s (15 states/51s). Retention comes from the performed idea or the building system, not raw cut density. Screen-record stretches hold longer ONLY while the cursor/UI keeps moving. **[measured across the 6 + 36v]**
- **Face presence:** S/C/M keep him on footage or in split for most of the reel; the UI-theatre format removes him for ~22s (Dd3, 9.5–32s) — the exception, and it works because the UI performs. Q's face law (§10) constrains how far Q's cuts may copy that stretch.
- **Endings:** no end card is a Devin habit (glitch-out, phone-check frame, mid-sentence cut, mosaic wall). Q's ending law overrides (§10).

## 8. B-ROLL SYSTEM (new chapter — logged per-reel in every priority BREAKDOWN)

Devin's B-roll is never decoration. Every insert is the claim PERFORMED or the evidence SHOWN. Six recurring types, from the 6 priority reels (counts = reels showing the type; instances counted in the BREAKDOWN logs):

**B1 — Performed scenario (he acts the situation).** DLpg: Zoom-call mock, ~6s full-screen inside a real Zoom UI, lands on "you're looking at yourself on the screen, what should you do?". Dff: mock Zoom call gallery, ~3s, lands on "…let them in the meeting". *Framing:* full-screen cutaway. *In/out:* hard cuts. *Sound:* his voice continues over/inside; the scenario itself is silent. **[2 reels, 2 instances]**

**B2 — Performed example (he delivers someone else's hook himself, in costume sets).** DLpg is built from these: books hook in a red room with a red book (~4.9–9.1s) then a window room with book stacks (~9.1–16.5s); apartment-smell hook in an apartment set with word-level captions (~33–43s) + peace-sign quote beat (~43–44.5s) + B&W restatement (~46.5–49.5s). *Durations:* 4–11s per example. *Framing:* full-screen, new set per example. *Sound:* voice only; verdict Xs silent. **[1 reel, 4+ instances — the entire M-hook format is B2]**

**B3 — Subject footage as evidence (the other creator's own video).** DfV: subject's reel playing inside the phone in his hands (~0–8.4s, PiP-by-prop); subject's frame full-screen annotated (~18–26s); the FIXED frame full-screen held ~9s (39.5–50.8s) and v2 ~4.4s (67.6–72s); subject's hook AUDIO replayed under diagnosis (~59–64s). *Rule:* the subject's footage is shown at the exact line it proves, and the "after" version is held longest. **[1 reel, 5 instances]**

**B4 — Embedded example reels (other creators inside his graphics).** Dfc matrix cells: a workout-plans CLONE-format video and a healthy-food-swaps WHITEBOARD video play as cells/cards (~28–44s) while he points at them. *Framing:* matrix cell / side card, never full-screen. *Sound:* muted — his voice carries. **[1 reel, 2 instances]**

**B5 — Content B-roll (his own past content as the artefact).** DSh: his reel cards compared full-screen with counts changing (11–18s, ~7s); his profile grid with follower counts climbing (34–38s); two of his cards chained by the link glyph with counts climbing (65–71s). Dd3: his tiles inside the results grid + the winner landing in his real profile (27–32s). *Rule:* when the claim is about content performance, his own grid/cards perform it. **[2 reels, 4 instances]**

**B6 — Hand/prop insert (no face).** DLpg phone-check divider: a hand holding a phone showing his own post with a glowing cyan check — ~1–2s, recurs 3× (section divider AND closer). DfV phone-in-hands open (also B3). DLpg diffuser-bottles product insert (~44.5–46.5s, shown never named). *Rule:* the hand/prop insert is punctuation — it divides sections and stamps verdicts, it never carries teaching. **[2 reels, 5 instances]**

**Cross-type laws:** (1) B-roll lands ON the line it proves — never as wallpaper under unrelated speech. (2) In/out is a hard cut except returns-to-face dissolves (~3 frames) — B-roll never gets a fancy transition of its own. (3) B-roll audio is his voice (or silence); the insert's own audio is heard only when the audio IS the evidence (DfV replay). (4) Performed B-roll uses a NEW set/grade per idea (red room, window room, apartment, B&W) — the set change is the section change. (5) Durations: punctuation inserts 1–2s; scenarios 3–6s; performed examples 4–11s; evidence holds up to ~9s when the artefact is the payoff.

## 14. Q GRASS-WALL KEYING — Q's turf wall as a selective keying surface

**The asset (frame-verified Nov 13):** Q records seated in front of an artificial-turf (green grass) wall. **What keying the turf enables (mapped to Devin's background moves):**
1. **Boards/screens BEHIND Q while he stays foreground (Bg4):** the paid-brand wall, a matrix/grid build, or a proof wall staged behind his shoulders on section beats while he keeps talking — the DTvM3ZvDegP flowchart-backdrop move, in PWC colours.
2. **Background plate swaps on section changes (Bg1-by-key):** the turf swaps to a navy plate / brand plate / calendar plate at HOOK→DELIVERY→PAYOFF breaks while framing and Q stay locked — his lighting-change punctuation, done with plates.
3. **Cutout PiP of Q over full-screen boards/UI (Bg6/Bg2):** where a screen must be full-bleed (Skool post, calendar, profile audit), Q's cutout rides bottom-corner or bottom-panel over it instead of a hard split — the DdWZp6OyyqV inside-the-player composite, PWC-styled.
4. **Graphics sliding BEHIND him (Bg4 variant):** count-up walls and tile clusters densify behind his shoulders (the Dfc mosaic move) instead of covering the frame's front — depth without touching his face envelope.

**The honest constraints (why this is a gate, not a default):**
- **Turf is not chroma fabric.** It is textured (visible blade noise and shadow pockets) and unevenly lit (brighter upper-left, falloff at the edges) — a global key will chatter and crawl on the boundary unless the key is tuned per shot and the plate is forgiving (busy plates like walls/grids hide edges; flat plates show them).
- **Hair is the hard edge.** Q's twists hang against the turf: thin strands, gaps that show green, and green spill risk onto skin and the red shirt. The hairline/twist edge decides pass/fail on every shot — check it at 100% on a frame strip before any keyed beat ships.
- **The chair is not green.** The gaming-chair wings sit behind his shoulders on both sides; a plate swap that only keys the turf leaves the chair floating on the new background. Either the mask includes the chair as foreground (harder roto), the crop excludes the wings, or the beat uses a panel layout instead.
- **Above the turf line** the pale wall + logo decal are not keyable green — keyed plates must crop below that strip or mask it too.
- **Motion breaks keys.** Beats where Q leans, points wide, or holds props up against the turf change the edge geometry; a key that holds on the locked talking beats may fail on the gesture beats.

**The gate (binding on the trio builds):** keying is auditioned per take AND per beat — extract the beat, key it, and run the edge check (hair/twists strip at 100%, spill check on skin/shirt, chair handling decided). Where the edge holds, use the keyed move (it is the closest Q can get to Devin's Bg4/Bg6 staging). Where it does not hold, fall back WITHOUT forcing it to Devin's own defaults: **panel/split layout (Bg2), full-screen board with Q in split (Bg3), or graphics over the footage in empty space (Bg5)** — all of which are equally Devin. Never ship a crawling edge to imitate a background move Devin himself usually makes without a key — and remember his own verdict on obvious keying: he stamps it FAKE (DNDfsANOqJc). Check each take's backdrop at build time before assuming the turf is available in frame for a given beat.
