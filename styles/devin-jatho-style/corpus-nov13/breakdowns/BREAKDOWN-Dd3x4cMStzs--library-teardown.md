## 3. WHAT THIS REEL DOES DIFFERENTLY (vs the first telling / standing Devin decode)
1. **The presenter vanishes for ~22 of 40 seconds.** Standing screen-demo grammar keeps the split (screen top ~55% / face bottom ~45%) with the face always present. Here Act 2 is full-screen UI theatre with voiceover only. The face is a bookend device, not a constant.
2. **A music bed runs the whole reel.** Batch-11 measured "no bed" across 13+ decodes (gaps → near-silence); the only exception was the songs reel, where songs were the topic. Here the bed is structural — it fills every sentence gap and carries the faceless act. Zero silences at −35dB/0.15s in 39.83s (SOUNDMAP.md).
3. **The three versions are floating video CARDS, not clones of Devin.** First telling (De0KOkxyGXd): three standing Devins, WARNING/RESULT/CURIOUS labels fading in beneath them over ~0.33s. Here: three portrait cards on a black reflective void; the camera pushes into each solo while its **complete text-up types in** — kicker included, mid-type states visible ("RES LT", "CURIO S") — in verdict colours (red / green / yellow glow-boxes). The text-ups are shown as the actual post covers, not as category labels.
4. **The posting screen itself is the stage — first time in the decode set.** The IG "New reel" screen is performed end-to-end: cursor-orb click → Share **shatters into pixels and turns red** (the mistake, in-UI) → Trial toggle flips → **Share becomes a "24:00:00" countdown that ticks down**. Timers existed before (72h ring, 23:58:14 on a Share button in De0KOkxyGXd) but never as the posting flow's own button, and the shatter-warning is new.
5. **The hook comparison resolves by subtraction.** The M-hook split (DLpgNYQMZlm) keeps both sides and wipes between them. Here the losing version is **deleted from the panel**, the winner keeps playing as an inset, and a handwritten annotation + arrow labels the result. The panel edits itself down to the answer.
6. **Proof is a ticking results grid + his real profile, not a number card.** All three trial counts climb across cuts; the winner is neon-outlined in the grid; then the winner tile lands in his actual profile grid (486K, "42.8M accounts reached" dashboard visible). No count-up card, no wall — the interface is the receipt.
7. **Pointing is two-handed and sustained; framing is wider.** The hook presenter is waist-up (light-grey jacket) with **both index fingers up for ~4s**, pointing at the panel above him — versus the ZOOM grammar's single-finger card-pointing in chest-up framing. He needs both arms in frame; the shot is composed for the gesture.
8. **The ending is a performed funnel + glitch-out.** The keyword types itself into a comment pill at his chest (he air-quotes it), the DM receipt banner drops, and a full-frame RGB glitch — previously only a mid-video transition (De0KXUgyj77 settings tear, DNqDAY7t264 ad-break entry) — becomes the **exit**: the file ends mid-glitch, no end card, no spoken CTA beyond the performed comment.
9. **Same system, second grammar.** This reel and De0KOkxyGXd teach the identical three-text-ups system with almost no shared devices. Confirms at the edit level what batch 11 found at the distribution level: Devin's profile is a small set of systems, each re-cut multiple ways.
