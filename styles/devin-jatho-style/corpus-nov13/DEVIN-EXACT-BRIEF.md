## 1. The measured spec sheet (non-negotiables)

**Captions**
- Montserrat ExtraBold; white→light-grey gradient (top white → ~rgb(198,204,214)); soft radial shadow; emphasis = 4-colour gradient mapped to PWC teal/gold + light sweep + Deep Glow ~0.2 alpha. Source stroke ~0.22× cap height (heavier than stock font — compensate in the render, see brain §11 residual).
- TWO-LINE PHRASE CHUNKS, swapped as a unit. No karaoke. (One-word hard-swap mode exists in Devin ONLY for a performed punch line — one line per video, max.)
- Size at 1080×1920: body cap ~57px, hook headline ~82px. Position: baseline ~y1560, ≤~900px wide, ≥43px margins, NEVER over Q's face envelope.
- Timing: chunks LEAD the spoken word by 0.1–0.4s (land in the pause before the word). The Nov 12 pass used a uniform 0.15s lead — keep leads in the 0.1–0.4 window but VARY per chunk the way the source does (phrase-boundary pauses), do not metronome them.
- Emphasis entry: horizontal stretch + motion smear → crisp in ~0.15s (scale-X settle, no bounce).

**Boards & devices**
- One device + one caption idea at a time; a panel and its content land as ONE event (never an empty panel).
- Full-screen boards: hook slams, screen recordings, matrices, fixed/final frames, proof interfaces. Split: screen top ~55% / face bottom ~45%, instruction pills ON the seam (teal).
- Count-ups: accelerate-then-settle ~1.5s, grid/wall densifies in sync (measured steps 25→27→36→50→81→133→224→343→557→623→625). Counts may also live ON artefacts (cards/tiles) — DSh pattern.
- Cursor: travel 10–16f → 2f pause → press to 97% for 2f → click ring 70%→140% over 8f.
- Card entrance: opacity 0, y+60, scale 94→100%, 8–12f, cubic ease-out, optional 102% overshoot.
- Gesture-synced cards: FADE ~3f; finger (or gaze, for clone beats) leads the word by ~0.1s, card lands ~0.2s after the gesture.
- Three-card comparison: centres x≈0.24/0.50/0.76, ~0.22 width each; selected 112%, others dim 40–60%.
- Section cards HOOK / DELIVERY / PAYOFF in gold at structural breaks.

**Motion & transitions**
- Hard cuts for hook/device changes; ~3-frame CROSS-DISSOLVE on returns to face.
- Verdict X (gold for Q): 1-frame pop, 120%→100% settle over 4f, SILENT.
- Split-comparison wipe: divider ~46% → ~62% over 3–4f landing ON "vs.".
- Wedge fly-in (tilted/blurred ~6f, hold ~1.2s, hard cut out, silent) · comments sheet up ~17f · RGB glitch ~4–5f · white flash 1f + ~0.9s fade · iris · zoom-blur ~5f · timer compression · fly-to-grid.
- Camera: locked; movement is editorial (jump-cut reframes, divider wipes, slow zoom into a wall closer). Room lighting can mark sections (red-lit = the wrong way in Devin; for Q use grade/lighting shifts only within his light-grade law).

**Pacing (visual-state changes)**
- M beats every 1.5–3s · S beats every 3–5s · C beats every 5–8s · performed beats ~8s. Screen stretches hold only while cursor/UI moves.

**Sound — read carefully, this is where the last pass went wrong**
