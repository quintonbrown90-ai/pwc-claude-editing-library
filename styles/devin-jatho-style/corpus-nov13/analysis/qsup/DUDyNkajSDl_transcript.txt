This is how the algorithm works for Instagram stories.
Once you learn the algorithm, you know the rules of the game,
and then you know how to win the game.
When you first publish your story,
the algorithm shows it to your followers
to interact with your content the most,
and then measures their engagement.
If they don't open your story,
or if they do open it, but swipe away,
the algorithm will vary your stories
and the rest of your followers never see it.
But if they do open your story,
stick with it till the end,
and interact with the sticker,
leave a like, comment, share, or reply,
the algorithm will push it to the front of the story
for all of your followers to see.
