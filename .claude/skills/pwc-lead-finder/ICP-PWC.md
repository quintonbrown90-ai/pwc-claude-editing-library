# ICP — who a PWC lead is (and who a Stan Calendar lead is)

The targeting comes from Q's offer, the same way the source reel reads the ICP off a website — except Q's is written down here, not inferred per run. Two funnels share one avatar at different temperatures.

## The avatar (both funnels)

Creators **under ~10K followers** who want **UGC / brand deals** and are not getting them yet — or not consistently. They post (or want to post) content, they watch creator-economy content, and they say the quiet parts out loud in public posts and comments. Q is half of Q and Kiya (~1.5M followers combined, 125 brand campaigns, partners like Walmart/ESPN/NFL/NBA Finals) — the lead's dream is the life Q already lives; the lead card's job is to hold the lead's own words for the gap.

## ICP A — Paid With Content waitlist (the Dec 7 funnel)

**Follower band:** under ~10K (the avatar band; note the exact count on the card and band it: nano <1K / micro 1K–10K).

**They signal fit by posting or commenting things like:**
- Brands ghosting them after they send rates, or never replying at all
- Rate confusion — not knowing what to charge, undercharging, "is $X too much?"
- No brand deal yet / first-deal milestones ("still haven't landed my first paid deal")
- Posting consistently with nothing to show for it; growth stalled
- Buying courses/programs that didn't deliver ("every course I bought was recycled YouTube videos" — objection gold)
- Wanting UGC income to cut shifts at a job, support a family, or go full-time

**Not a fit:** brands and agencies themselves; established creators well above the band selling their own competing coaching; accounts with no public posting history to quote (no posts = no verbatim quotes = no card); anyone whose public content is outside the creator-economy conversation.

## ICP B — Stan Content Calendar buyer (the $35 front door)

Micro-creators posting about **planning / consistency struggles**: "I post whenever I remember," no content system, filming in panic, page dying from inconsistency. They are Calendar prospects **today** and cohort prospects on Dec 7 — every Calendar push doubles as waitlist building, and the Calendar delivery carries the waitlist bridge (see STAN-COPY-BLOCKS.md).

## Surfaces — where they already talk in public

Run through the connectors Q already has (Instagram search/read), small batches, human pace:

1. **Commenters on creator-economy / UGC posts** — the avatar self-identifies in comment sections under UGC, brand-deal, and creator-coach content.
2. **Q's daily engagement-rotation communities** (e.g. Skoolers, Synthesizer) — members posting their struggles where Q already shows up.
3. **Creators posting struggle content** — brand-deal struggles, ghosting, rate questions, no-deal-yet milestones, consistency spirals (search the phrases, not just the niches).
4. **Q's own inbound surface** — people who comment WAITLIST or engage @yerrr__q content are already opted in; cards built from their public posts sharpen follow-ups, they are never "found leads" to cold-touch.

**Promotion-side rule:** any outreach or promotion runs only on **@yerrr__q** — never the couples pages (@sheszakiya / @qandkiya). The couples audiences are not an ICP surface for PWC.

## Pacing (binding)

- Small batches, human pace, inside normal engagement work — a handful of cards per batch, not a harvest.
- No bulk scraping of any platform, no automation signatures. If a surface can't be read through a legitimate connector at a human pace, it is not a surface.
- The reel this adapts claims "thousands of profiles at a time" — that is the creator's marketing. This skill makes no scale promise; quality of the verbatim card beats list size every time.
