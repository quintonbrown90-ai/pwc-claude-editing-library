# HOOKS — Jev hook bank: judge every hook six ways, rank by real views (@liamjohnston.ai)

Source: @liamjohnston.ai reel De6xXw-Aldw (INFERRED reconstruction; the Jev
setup is GATED, never chased). An agent (Opus 5.5, per the video) reads every
video on the account and judges every hook on six dimensions (PUBLISHED):
Hookstyle, Lever, Emotion, Curiosity, Credibility, Reusable. Output: a Hook
Bank table — every hook ranked by REAL views with a 0–100 score (MEASURED
rows from the video: 81.4 / 81.2 / 77.8 / 74.4 / 73.6; top hook "Your Claude
Code runs one agent. This runs hundreds for 75% less." at 497,358 views).
Every answer carries a confidence note.

## The rule
Rank by real views, diagnose the tired hook. A shape whose last 16 uses
never broke its median is tired, however good it once was.

## Executable steps
1. Build Q's Hook Bank from his own posted hooks (P1/RC1/P2/P3 + trial reels
   + launch pack): hook line, views, score on the six dimensions, rank.
2. The diagnosis: point Jev at what's NOT working — "The hook you just heard
   comes from one that did almost 600,000 views. But the style's been used so
   much since that the last 16 never broke 100,000. So it's not a bad hook,
   it's just a tired one." Counter-signal in the same video: "The free tool
   hooks broke 100,000 on three out of eight."
3. Retire hook shapes on evidence: any shape with 3+ consecutive
   below-median uses is flagged TIRED and rested.
4. After every 30 posts, Claude builds/updates Q's Hook Bank (hook, views,
   6-dimension scores, rank, confidence) → top 3 formulas feed the next
   month's hooks.
5. Point it at other creators and learn which hook styles are carrying them
   right now — "borrow what's already winning" (their words; the borrowing
   is the hook SHAPE, never their script — same rule as the library's
   copy-the-mechanics line).
6. The self-referential open is a device: open a video with Q's own best
   hook line, then show the table that proves it.

## Worked example (PWC)
Q's Hook Bank: "BRANDS ARE PAYING SMALL CREATORS LIKE YOU" — claim type,
score 81.4, top rank. The question-hook shape ("Did you know…") — last 3
uses all below median → flagged TIRED, rested for the month. Next month's
hooks: all claims. Keyword WAITLIST.

## FLAG
"Claude sets the whole thing up and connects it to your Instagram for you"
is an OAuth/connector grant — never connect without Q's explicit word
(GAP-1 in the Liam drafts). Cost ("one cent" / under $0.01) and speed
("reads every video in about a minute") are the creator's claims,
UNVERIFIED.

## Human-made check
Pairs with the Nate hookscore part (hookscore.py's five checks vs Jev's six
dimensions — run both; where they disagree, the retention graph decides).
No view promises, ever.

## PWC skin
Q's hooks, Q's views, Q's bank. Tired shapes rest on evidence, not on
taste.
