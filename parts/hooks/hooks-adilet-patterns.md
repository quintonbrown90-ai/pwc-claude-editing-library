## The rule
Hooks are **claims with a number, a threat, or a secret** — never a greeting,
never "in this video." Line 1 names the viewer's pain or the size of the
prize; line 2 (same hook beat) promises the mechanism ("here's exactly how").
Median video on the account earns ~170 likes; every pattern below is ranked
by what actually broke out.

## The patterns (use verbatim formulas, fill with TRUE PWC claims)
1. **THE LAWSUIT** (fear + specific dollar figure) — "Your vibe-coded app
   can get sued for $100K before it makes a single sale." Ran 4x in 10 days
   (Sep 18–27) with micro-variations; winners re-shot. Formula: Your [thing
   you built] can [specific financial/legal disaster] before it [earns
   anything]. The number is round, large and exact.
2. **THE GATE** ("don't start until…") — "Don't start vibe coding with
   Claude Code until these 4 plugins are in." Formula: Don't start
   [activity] until [N specific things] are in. Withholds progress; the
   number promises a finite, finishable list.
3. **THE METER** (you're running at X/Y) — "Your Claude Code is running at
   0/4 power until you plug these in." Formula: Your [tool] is running at
   [low fraction] until you [fix]. A dashboard reading makes the deficiency
   feel measured, not opinionated.
4. **EVERYONE'S DOING IT** (meta / how-it's-made) — "Everyone's making
   AI-animated reels with Claude Code. Here's exactly how, step by step."

## Executable steps
1. Pick ONE pattern. Write line 1 from its formula with a TRUE PWC claim
   (collective framing for earnings: "creators like my wife and I, and
   other creators…"). No invented numbers.
2. Write line 2 as the mechanism promise ("here's exactly how").
3. Test hooks like ad variants: same hook, new wording, new video —
   winners re-shot, losers dropped.
