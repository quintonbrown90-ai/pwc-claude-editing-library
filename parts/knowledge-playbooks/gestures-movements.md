# KNOWLEDGE — Gestures + Movements vocabulary (OBSERVED + INFERRED)

Source: `research/knowledge/GESTURES-MOVEMENTS.md` — the visual-language
vocabulary. OBSERVED = seen in a decoded video on disk. INFERRED =
working rule from the motion/camera playbooks.

## The rule
Claude must RECOGNIZE what's in a video: every point, gesture, look,
movement, and cut motivation — then respond to it in the edit.

## OBSERVED (from decoded videos)
- Look to camera, held (David DfdAvbfRQY5, frames f_002–f_030): eyes
  never break for the screen demo — demo plays in a split panel. EDIT:
  never cut away from the face on a look-to-camera claim; proof goes in
  a split panel.
- Face still, world moves (David f_010/f_020): same take, devices cycle
  around him. EDIT: lock the framing; move the device, not Q.
- Word passes BEHIND the head (Viashaan, 35.66s): EDIT: HERO_BEHIND on
  1–4 words/video; front captions yield during hero windows.
- Lying-down scene (Viashaan, 27.5–31.4s): a posture change is a visual
  reset. EDIT: once per video max, only where the line is a state change.
- Point at the stack (Sky Form 2 / P1 v9): EDIT: pulse the pointed-at
  tile on the exact frame the finger extends.
- Whip pan / zoom kick on the payoff word (Adilet, measured 222 wpm):
  EDIT: the move IS the punctuation — never a move with no word behind it.

## INFERRED vocabulary (gesture → edit response)
- Point at screen → cut/zoom to the thing on the point frame.
- Look down at phone → cut to the real phone screen; cut back on look-up.
- Look off camera → cut to the thing looked at.
- Lean in / eyes widen → punch-in 1.05–1.15× on the lean.
- Hand count 1-2-3 → number chip lands per finger; completed row = payoff.
- Head nod → no cut (the nod is the punctuation). Head shake → cut to the
  wrong thing crossed out.
- Turn head → camera switch on the turn (two-cam rule: never mid-turn).
- Step toward camera → slow push-in; CTA lands as the step lands.
- Hands open, palms up → bring the full map on screen.
- Hands chop → hard cut on the chop; the chopped thing exits frame.
- Stillness after motion → hold 0.4–0.7s (the only sanctioned pause).
- Walking while talking → the walk IS the transition.

## Cut motivations (log one per cut in every decode)
WORD · POINT · PROOF · BEAT · REACTION · DEVICE · TEMPLATE. A decode
with unmotivated cuts is incomplete.

## Worked example
Q points at tile 3 while saying "this one made $2,800": finger extends
on "this" → tile 3 pulses gold on the same frame → 0.5s hold → back to Q.
