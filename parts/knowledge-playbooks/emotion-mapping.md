# KNOWLEDGE — Emotion mapping: feeling through the cut (OBSERVED + GAP-FILL)

Source: `research/knowledge/EMOTION.md` (first draft, Nov 8) — 16
OBSERVED mappings cited to video + timestamp, 5 [GAP-FILL]. The layer
that makes edits feel human, not AI.

## The rule
Every device must carry a feeling. A device with no emotion attached is
decoration — cut it or give it a job.

## The map (emotion → device → sound → visual)
- **AWE / PROOF** — count-up under the claim (Jayant 0–5.7s: 432→38,000);
  sound: rising tick + landing thud; visual: giant Anton number.
- **TRUST / DIRECT ADDRESS** — look to camera held, proof in split panel
  or PiP, face never leaves (Viashaan + JLee whole reels); sound: dry
  voice, no bed swell; visual: eyes on lens.
- **CURIOSITY / OPEN LOOP** — name the payoff, withhold it, tag the
  withheld item (Jayant 15.4–19.1s, "5th is most effective"); sound: a
  held note that never resolves; visual: the tagged item glows.
- **RELIEF** — winner filter (Jayant trial-reel A–E: losers fall away,
  winner stays); sound: exhale / soft resolve; visual: the field clears.
- **SCALE / OVERWHELM** — 17-agent grid (Nate Gold); sound: layered
  ticks building; visual: the grid fills the frame.
- **TENSION** — editor wall / flat-data warning (Viashaan); sound:
  low drone, no melody; visual: tight framing, dark grade.
- **WARMTH** — cooking line diagrams (Viashaan taste section); sound:
  warm bed, soft pops; visual: gold light, slow moves.
- **DISARM** — honest aside held on face (Viashaan "no, I don't sell any
  courses", 1.6s); sound: silence — no SFX on the aside; visual: hold
  the face, do not cut.
- **URGENCY** — flat data warning; sound: ticking; visual: the number
  in gold, no decoration.
- **HYPNOSIS** — cover push-in (Kallaway); sound: slow swell; visual:
  slow push toward the figure.
- **SNAPBACK SURPRISE** — contrarian jerk (Kallaway Step 3); sound:
  whip + snap; visual: the frame jerks back.
- **ANTICIPATION** — orbit (Liam); sound: rising shimmer; visual:
  objects circle, never settle.
- **SATISFACTION** — Notion board payoff (CCC Idea 1–10); sound:
  check ticks in a row; visual: the completed list holds.
- **BELONGING** — roster (JLee 5-agent team); sound: warm unison;
  visual: the team card, all five present.
- **AUTHORITY** — correction→rule card (JLee); sound: stamp; visual:
  the rule card locks in.

## GAP-FILL entries (labeled — Q didn't ask, mastermind added)
Joy, fear, frustration, sadness, playfulness — mapped the same way, with
the NO RED constraint applied (anger = gold strike-through, dread =
darkness + stillness, never red).

## Executable steps
1. Name the feeling of each beat BEFORE choosing its device. A beat
   with no feeling gets no device.
2. Check the map: the device, sound, and visual must agree on ONE
   emotion. Mixed signals read as AI.
3. The HUMAN-MADE CHECK now includes an emotion read: does the video
   FEEL like something, or only show things?

## Worked example
Hook beat "Five emails, $92,000": feeling = AWE/PROOF → device =
count-up card → sound = rising tick + landing thud → visual = giant
gold number. Then TRUST: Q's face, eyes on lens, proof in split panel.
