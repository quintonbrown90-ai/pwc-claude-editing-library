## The rule
People do not buy a product. They buy the end of a pain, at a price that
feels safe, from someone they trust, before a deadline that is real.
Every close must pass four gates in order: safety ("this person knows
the path"), value ("the cost of doing nothing is bigger than the
price"), trust ("people like me did this"), and timing ("the door really
closes"). Skip safety and the buyer freezes. Skip value and the buyer
haggles. Skip trust and the buyer waits. Skip timing and the buyer
waits forever. Urgency is always a real deadline — never manufactured.

## The 7 principles (Cialdini — activate ≥3 at every conversion moment)
1. **Reciprocity** — give first. The keyword flow delivers its promise
   BEFORE any ask. The 3-Wins PDF, the rate sheet, the free teaching:
   value first is not generosity, it is the mechanism.
2. **Commitment / consistency** — small yes before big yes. Comment one
   keyword → drop an email → join the waitlist → join Dec 7. Each step
   is small enough to say yes to, and each yes makes the next one
   easier. Never ask for the big yes first.
3. **Social proof** — plural and real. The wall of other small creators'
   paid videos. "People like you are already getting paid" beats any
   single receipt.
4. **Authority** — borrow it, don't claim it. Real numbers, real
   creators, real brand names Q & Kiya actually worked with (Walmart,
   ESPN, NBA, NFL — the couples credentials). Authority in tone: calm
   pacing, confidence, no hype. "These bypass fear and tell the brain,
   'You're safe, this person knows the path.'"
5. **Liking** — the founder's story, told as the avatar's story in
   advance (Baltimore → Houston on faith, 4 kids, no village, started
   at 32). People buy from people they recognise themselves in.
6. **Scarcity** — real only. The founding window closes Dec 13, 11:59 PM
   CT; next opening not promised. That is the ONLY urgency line the
   library permits. A fake countdown is a lie the buyer remembers.
7. **Unity** — shared identity. "Creators like us." The keyword badges,
   the inside language, the shared enemy (posting for free). Unity is
   why the close feels like joining, not buying.

## The buyer triggers (stack 3 per close)
- **Loss aversion (2–3×):** frame the cost of inaction, not just the
  dream. "Every month you post for free is a month of videos a brand
  would have paid $150–$400 each for." The brain weighs the loss
  heavier than the gain — sell the leak, not just the bucket.
- **Anchoring:** $97/mo feels expensive until anchored against the
  upside (one $150 video pays for the month; one $400 video pays for
  four) or against the alternative (an agency, a $10K program, another
  year of guessing). Q's numbers only — never invent an anchor.
- **Future pacing:** vividly imagine the reward. "Day 90: deal-ready,
  rates set, pitches out weekly." Dopamine fires on the imagined
  future, not on the feature list. The 90-day path IS the future-pacing
  device — use it by name.
- **Framing:** "the 90-day path from posting for free to deal-ready"
  (a system, a room, a cohort) — never "a course I'm teaching."
  Framing is the whole sale: same offer, different frame, different
  decision.

## Objection handling (psychology first, lines second)
The reframe loop (Buyer's Brain): **Acknowledge** ("Totally fair, I
hear you" — safety) → **Reframe** (shift perspective with psychology)
→ **Redirect** (pull back to vision/decision). Top performers pause 3+
seconds before responding — thoughtful beats reactive. The VALUE
framework (Sybill): Verify the real objection (most pricing objections
are value objections in disguise) → Anchor to a business outcome the
buyer already confirmed → Link price to quantified payback → Uncover
budget/approval constraints → Enable the champion (a forwardable
summary with ROI math — for Q: the 3-Wins PDF + the waitlist page).
Pre-handle objections BEFORE the offer (Martell rule): the spouse
objection, the "another course?" objection, and the "prove it"
objection are answered in the nurture, not at the close. A no is a no
— never chase it twice in one thread. The exact lines live in
`research/knowledge/SALES-CLOSING.md` (objection table); this part
decides WHICH trigger each line runs on — it never rewrites the lines.

## The value-stack end card (itsflxkzy steal)
Sell the BONUS, not the free content. The end card names what they GET:
"YOU GET ✓ the rate sheet ✓ the pitch template ✓ the 3-Wins PDF ✓ the
first 7 days mapped." The checklist sells the bonus items, not the thing
already shown free. Restate the comment keyword in the video's own
on-screen language (keyword-as-command CTA). The face is the closer,
not the opener — in carousels the human appears last; in videos the
face is in the hook (Q's rule) and the value-stack card closes.

## Executable steps
1. Before any close (DM, caption, end card, live), name the 3
   principles + 1 trigger it activates. Fewer than 3 principles = the
   close is thin — add proof, reciprocity, or unity before shipping.
2. Anchor every price mention against a real upside or a real
   alternative, in Q's numbers only. A bare price with no anchor is a
   haggle invitation.
3. Pre-handle the top 3 objections in the nurture (value → waitlist →
   join): affordability, "another course?", "prove it." The close
   itself answers nothing new — it collects a decision already made.
4. Use the reframe loop on every live objection: acknowledge (pause,
   safety) → reframe → redirect to the vision. Never argue, never
   discount first. Two options, never a discount-first response
   (Option A: best fit for what they said they want; Option B: best
   fit for a lower price — "which feels more comfortable?").
5. End cards: "YOU GET ✓…" checklist + keyword restated in the video's
   own language + one action, one sentence.

## Worked example (PWC)
Waitlist DM after a WAITLIST comment: deliver the 3-Wins PDF
(reciprocity) → "Your first win is in there — page 1" (commitment: a
small yes, reading page 1) → "Doors open Dec 7, you're first in line,
extra month free" (scarcity: real deadline + unity: first in line).
Triggers: loss aversion ("every month posting for free…") in the
nurture, anchoring ("one $150 video covers the month") in the close
week, future pacing ("Day 90: deal-ready") on Dec 7. No pitch, no
pressure, one next step.
