## The rule
A follower becomes a buyer in four psychological moves, and each move
needs a different thing: ATTENTION (reels — proof, identity, reach) →
TRUST (Stories + nurture — the face, the daily presence, the free
teaching) → DECISION (the door opens — a real deadline, a small first
step, the risk removed) → BELONGING (inside — rituals, hot seats,
visible progress, wins posted by members). Most creators try to sell
at the attention stage. That is why their reels get views and their
communities stay empty. Reels build authority; Stories close; the room
keeps.

## The four stages (with the numbers)
### Stage 1 — Attention (reels: proof-led, identity-first)
- Job: reach strangers + name the avatar. >50% of Reel views come from
  non-followers; reels are the discovery engine. One announcement reel
  can do the work of a month of posting (Ron: 5,000 followers + 10,000
  saves from ONE reel).
- Measure: views vs the creator's median (outlier multiple), saves,
  shares into DMs. Likes are the weakest signal — ignore them as a
  decision input.
- Q's shape: proof-led P/RC packs, Authority-30, YAP30. Proof
  full-screen in the first 2 seconds; hook = title words spoken up
  front; close = Comment KEYWORD (flow must exist first).

### Stage 2 — Trust (Stories + nurture: the conversion surface)
- Job: turn a viewer into someone who would pay. "Reels don't convert.
  Stories do." Ron's conversion: 5–10 new members per story post, with
  low-pressure CTAs, 1–2 story posts a week. The 3-story daily
  (greet → promote → personal) is the ritual form — NEEDS-Q before
  adoption as a daily rule.
- Why it works: Stories are intimate (face, voice, daily presence),
  ephemeral (a reason to check today), and conversational (polls,
  replies, DMs). A reel is a broadcast; a Story is a room with the
  door open.
- Q's shape: ManyChat DM broadcasts + free teaching avatar videos as
  warm-up content (Nov 2–Dec 6); the 3-Wins PDF as the initiation
  gift; waitlist nurture that teaches before it asks.
- Measure: qualified DMs started per story, not follower count
  ("a 3,000-follower account where forty people a month start a
  conversation beats a 30,000-follower account where four do" —
  SetLuca, via AUDIENCE-PSYCHOLOGY.md §12).

### Stage 3 — Decision (Dec 7 open → Dec 13 close)
- Job: collect a decision already made. The door opens Dec 7; the
  join link goes to waitlisters' DMs first; Day 1 live 12 PM CT.
  Founding window closes Dec 13, 11:59 PM CT — the ONLY urgency line.
- Psychology: commitment/consistency (small yeses already made:
  comment → email → waitlist), loss aversion (the window really
  closes; next opening not promised), risk removal ("you're not buying
  anything today — judge the free teaching first"; monthly only, no
  annual; extra month free for waitlisters).
- Q's shape: the close playbook (Dec 7 open → Dec 13 close, day by
  day) owned by the Closer Specialist. A no is a no — never chase it
  twice in one thread. A declined card may mean order, not rejection
  — ask before assuming.

### Stage 4 — Belonging (inside: retention is the product)
- Job: make month 2 easier than month 1. Ron's churn lesson: month 1
  churns because early signups didn't all convert; month 2+ stabilises
  at 80–85%+ retention when three things improve: clearer CTAs filter
  better-fit members upfront, more content exists in the community,
  and onboarding tells members what they're getting.
- Retention drivers, ranked: (1) owner presence — 20 minutes a day in
  the room, comments and replies, no substitute; (2) onboarding —
  structured welcome + first-7-days map (Q's 3-Wins PDF is the seed);
  (3) rituals — 2 lives/week, same times; (4) member wins posted as
  proof — "the wins members post in month three are the raw material
  for the content that fills month four"; (5) challenges — 70–80%
  completion vs 3–5% for courses, 1:6 conversion from challenge to
  higher offer (a future Q ruling — challenges are not yet a PWC
  mechanic).
- Realistic churn: 5–10%/mo on a well-run community; 20–30%/mo at
  first while fit is found; 10–15% after 90 days of consistent
  delivery. Churn is arithmetic against lifetime value, not a moral
  failure — measure it, fix the structural cause, do not hide it.
- Graduation is the retention device: Day 90 = Sat Mar 6, 2027,
  Graduation Sun Mar 7, 2027. A visible end makes the middle worth
  staying for. Every member must be able to say where they are on the
  path (BUILD → CREATE → MONETIZE → SCALE).

## The binge effect (why the avatar lane matters)
Viewers who watch 3–5 videos in one session are 15–25% more likely to
join a paid community — binge-watching mimics habit formation; they are
building a relationship in real time. YouTube members retain 50%
longer than Instagram/TikTok members (LTV $300–500 vs $150–250).
Implication for Q: the avatar explainer lane and the proof packs are
not just reach — they are the binge surface. Series structure (P1 → P2
→ P3, proof packs P1–P35) is a binge mechanic: each video names the
next one. The retention loop IS the conversion funnel.

## Executable steps
1. Split conversion measurement by surface: reels (reach + saves +
   shares), Stories (qualified DMs + joins per story post), nurture
   (waitlist growth + email captures), close (joins per day Dec 7–13),
   inside (weekly actives, churn, wins posted). One blended number
   hides where the funnel actually breaks.
2. Before Dec 7, the nurture runs value-first: every DM broadcast
   teaches something usable before any ask. The ask is one action, one
   sentence.
3. Onboarding is built before doors open: the 3-Wins PDF + first-7-days
   map + Day 1 live. A member who does not know what they're getting
   in week 1 is a churn stat in month 2.
4. Owner presence is scheduled, not improvised: 20 minutes a day in
   the room, every day, protected like a live slot.
5. Weekly inactivity sweep: members quiet for 7+ days get a personal
   message (not a broadcast). Cancel-save flow matched to the reason
   given. Wins posted by members are collected weekly and fed back
   into the next month's proof content.
