# SALES / CLOSING — Closer Specialist (mastermind #14)

Source: `research/knowledge/SALES-CLOSING.md` — Q's actual offers only:
Premium $97/mo monthly-only; Dec 7 cohort, Day 90 Mar 6, 2027,
Graduation Mar 7, 2027; founding window closes Dec 13, 11:59 PM CT;
waitlisters get an extra month free + 3-Wins PDF; CALENDAR $35.

## The rule
Value → waitlist → join. Urgency is always a real deadline (Dec 13),
never manufactured. A no is a no — never chase it twice in one thread.

## Executable steps
1. Value first: the keyword flow delivers its promise BEFORE any ask.
2. The ask is one action, one sentence: "Drop your email and you're on
   the waitlist — extra month free when doors open Dec 7."
3. Frame the offer as the system (90-day path, two lives a week, hot
   seats), never "a course I'm teaching."
4. Objections get the true answer from the table (affordability =
   monthly-only + free month; "another course?" = the cohort is the
   room, not a recording; "prove it" = judge the free teaching before
   paying a dollar).
5. Own the nurture playbook (DM broadcasts + avatar warm-ups Nov 2–
   Dec 6) and the close playbook (Dec 7 open → Dec 13 close, day by day).

## Worked example
DM after a WAITLIST comment: deliver the 3-Wins PDF → "Your first win is
in there — page 1. Doors open Dec 7, you're first in line, extra month
free." No pitch, no pressure, one next step.
