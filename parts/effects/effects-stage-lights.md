## The rule
The room becomes a stage: a grid of warm lights appears behind the speaker,
face clear, and the talk is suddenly a performance. The lights are the stage
— they never touch the speaker, they frame him.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Build the light grid behind the speaker (segmented background layer):
   2 rows × 4 columns of small warm lights, evenly spaced, in the empty
   space behind the shoulders and head — never overlapping the head box.
2. Entrance: lights switch on one at a time, left to right, 0.1s apart, on
   the typed command + a soft click per light. Exit: lights switch off in
   reverse on the next command.
3. The lights glow (soft halo, no hard flare) and stay lit for the beat —
   they are a backdrop, not a strobe. No flashing, no pulsing faster than
   1Hz; the grid is calm.

## Face-clearance check
Grid sits behind the speaker, face clear. No light ever overlaps the head
box. PASS in the source video.

## Worked example (PWC)
Reveal beat: Q delivers the payoff line and the gold light grid switches on

## Not the spotlight
Distinct from effects-spotlight (the background darkens while the subject
stays lit — a pool of light ON the speaker). Stage-lights ADDS lights behind
him; the background does not dim.
