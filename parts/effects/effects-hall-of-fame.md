# EFFECT — hall of fame [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @stocktonwalbeck / @coursecreator360 reel DfcnEEMgsNf (~13–15s,
effect 06) — spoken in the list run. The demo build is gated behind
EDITOR -> DM — NOT chased. Rebuild from visible spec only.

## The rule
On a proof moment, the wall of receipts: the speaker's best
reels/proof tiles fill the frame as a wall — the claim, proved by
volume.

## Look (measured from frames)
Wall of his best reels/proof tiles ("241 REELS · 2019–2025",
"TRIPLED THEIR BUSINESS", "ABSOLUTE").

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Wall tiles are REAL videos only — Q's own proof tiles (payouts,
   results, best reels), each tile a real screen with its real figure.
   Never a generic tile, never a redrawn fake. Blur per the complete
   blur law where other people getting paid appear (brand, face,
   profile picture, profile name blurred; amounts + follower counts
   sharp).
2. Build: tiles pop in staggered (SPRING, 0.2s each, row by row) with a
   soft tick SFX per row; a header strip carries the aggregate claim
   ("241 REELS · 2019–2025" shape — Q's real aggregate, real dates).
3. The wall fills the frame BETWEEN Q's beats, or as a dimmed bed
   behind a face-clear zone. It holds 2–3s while the proof line lands.
4. PWC skin: navy wall bed, gold header figures, teal tile edges on the
   tiles being spoken about. The spoken figure lands in gold, large,
   over the wall's top edge.
5. Trigger: a proof line ("my best", "the receipts", "241 reels",
   "tripled their business"). No proof in the words = no wall.

## Numbering
Blue `NN` chip top-left + name in Stockton's original. PWC remap:
GOLD chip text on NAVY (teal border), never blue.

## Face-clearance check
The wall never covers Q's face. Full-frame wall = between beats; on
screen with Q = dimmed bed behind a face-clear zone, or a side wall in
empty space.

## Variants
- **P1 wall device** (Q's own launch P1 v9 — the canonical PWC wall):
  "EVERY TILE = A REAL VIDEO" — every tile a real video, no big
  creators, no Q & Kiya couples grid in the opening wall (the opening
  wall is OTHER creators' paid-video walls; Q & Kiya's own wall appears
  only at beats where the script is actually about them). Stockton's
  Hall of Fame is the same device on his own catalog; P1 is the PWC
  house version with the stricter tile rule. When in doubt, build the
  P1 wall.
- **Mosaic Drop** (`parts/effects/effects-mosaic-drop.md`): same mosaic
  grammar, different job — catalog/list, not proof.

## Worked example
Proof beat: the wall of Q's best proof tiles fills the frame as he
says "241 reels" — header strip "241 REELS · 2019–2025" in gold, tiles
ticking in row by row, then hard cut back to Q.
