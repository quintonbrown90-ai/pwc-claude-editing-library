# EFFECTS — comment pile: stacked real comments as social proof behind the speaker (@patlaw, Tier S)

Source: @patlaw reel DfKciU1o6zV — "/edit catalog" (60.2s, Format B silent
showcase). Commands per search summary + frames viewed directly. The skill
behind /edit is NOT named on screen (INFERRED: same effects-skill family as
Vibha's — unconfirmed). Distinct from effects-comment-bubble.md (a single
bubble): the pile is a STACK of real comments, the proof is the quantity.

## The rule
Real comments, stacked, behind the speaker — the pile is the proof. One
comment is a quote; a pile is a crowd. The pile builds while Q talks, one
comment at a time, and holds behind him like a wall of receipts.

## Executable steps
1. Build: a stack of real comments (screenshots, real handles visible) lands
   one at a time behind Q — beside/above, never over his face — each with a
   soft pop + tick.
2. Sequence: comments land on the spoken beat they prove (the claim first,
   the pile second). 4–8 comments max — a pile, not a landfill. Every
   comment traces to a real person and a real post, never a fabricated quote.
3. Face rule: the pile is BEHIND Q — his head and shoulders stay in front of
   it, face clear at all times. No comment ever crosses his face.
4. Motion: the pile settles, then one comment pulses gold on the payoff
   line. The pile never scrolls during the talk — a scrolling pile reads as
   a feed, not proof.
5. Sourcing rule: comments come from Q's own posts / DMs / community
   (waitlist comments, student wins). Never invent a comment, never borrow
   another creator's comments as Q's proof.

## Worked example (PWC)
"Creators are asking for this every day" — the pile builds behind Q as he
names the question, real waitlist comments stacking (handles visible), one
gold pulse on "every day". Keyword WAITLIST.

## Human-made check
Every comment in the pile is a real comment Q can open and show. The pile
builds on his words, not on a timer — and it never covers his face.

## PWC skin
Navy pile background, gold pulse on the payoff comment, teal comment edges.
NO RED. Real handles in white; the pile sits in empty space, never over Q.
