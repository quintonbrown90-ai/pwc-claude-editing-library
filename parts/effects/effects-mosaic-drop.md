# EFFECT — mosaic drop [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @stocktonwalbeck / @coursecreator360 reel DfcnEEMgsNf (~6–8s,
effect 02) — spoken right after the teardown opener. The demo build is
gated behind EDITOR -> DM — NOT chased. Rebuild from visible spec only.

## The rule
On a list/count moment, the whole catalog drops in at once as a dense
mosaic grid of tiles — the list made visible, all of it, in one beat.

## Look (measured from frames)
Dense mosaic grid of dozens of his video cards/tiles (CONVERSION,
4 MAIN REASONS, BECAUSE, etc.) dropping in as one wall.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Build the mosaic from REAL tiles only — Q's own video cards/posts,
   never stock, never generic placeholders. 12–30 tiles in a tight grid.
2. Drop: tiles fall in staggered rows (top row first, 2–3 frames apart,
   EASE IN settle) with a soft thud SFX per row, not per tile.
3. The mosaic fills the frame BEHIND the speaker or full-frame between
   his beats — never over his face. It holds 1–2s, then hard cut out.
4. PWC skin: tiles carry the PWC badge; grid lines navy; the spoken
   count word (e.g. "17") lands in gold over the grid's top edge.
5. Trigger: a list/count line in the transcript ("here are 17…", "four
   systems", "three wins"). No list in the words = no mosaic.

## Numbering
Blue `NN` chip top-left + name in Stockton's original. PWC remap:
GOLD chip text on NAVY (teal border), never blue.

## Face-clearance check
Full-frame mosaic plays BETWEEN Q's beats, not over him. If he is on
screen, the mosaic is a dimmed bed behind a face-clear zone, or it
drops in the empty space around him.

## Variants
- **P1 wall device** (Q's own launch P1 v9): a wall of REAL videos,
  one tile per proof — same mosaic grammar, slower job (proof, not
  catalog). See `parts/effects/effects-hall-of-fame.md` for the proof
  version; this part is the catalog/list version.

## Worked example
"Four systems" beat: 16 tiles (4 per system) drop in as a mosaic as Q
says "four systems" — the grid holds while he names them, then cuts
to the first system.
