# EFFECT — billboard [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @stocktonwalbeck / @coursecreator360 reel DfcnEEMgsNf (~16–18s,
effect 08) — spoken in the list run. The demo build is gated behind
EDITOR -> DM — NOT chased. Rebuild from visible spec only.

## The rule
On a statement line, the word becomes a billboard behind the speaker —
giant text, split AROUND the head, face always clear.

## Look (measured from frames)
Giant "BILLBOARD" text behind him, split around his head (face clear).

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Pick the statement word(s) — one or two words max, the thesis of the
   line. Set them in Anton, giant (fills the frame width), in PWC gold.
2. Split: the text is cut into two halves that sit LEFT and RIGHT of
   the head, or above/below with the head in the gap. The split follows
   the tracked head box with >=40px clearance on every side.
3. Entrance: the two halves slam in from the sides (EASE OUT, 8 frames)
   ON the spoken word, with a slam SFX; they hold for the line, then
   hard cut out.
4. PWC skin: gold text on the dimmed room; a thin teal underline under
   the word. NO RED — nothing in the billboard is red.
5. Trigger: a statement line — a claim stated flat ("ten years as a
   video editor", "this is the system"). If the line is a question or a
   story beat, use Headline or a caption instead.

## Numbering
Blue `NN` chip top-left + name in Stockton's original. PWC remap:
GOLD chip text on NAVY (teal border), never blue.

## Face-clearance check
THE rule of this effect: the text splits AROUND the head. If the head
moves, the split follows the track. A billboard that crosses the face
is a failed render — quarantine and re-cut.

## Variants
- **Sky-style giant text behind speaker:** same device in Sky's lane
  (giant thesis words behind the speaker, face clear) — Stockton's
  Billboard is the named, numbered version of it.
- **Kienobifilms titles-behind-head** (`parts/imagery/
  imagery-titles-behind-head.md`): the title passes BEHIND the head
  (one layer, head occludes the middle) — Billboard splits the text
  around the head instead of hiding it behind; pick Billboard when the
  whole word must stay readable.

## Worked example
Statement beat: "TEN YEARS" slams in split around Q's head as he says
"ten years as a video editor" — the word holds for the line, then cuts
to the proof.
