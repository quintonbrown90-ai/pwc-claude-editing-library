# EFFECT — doodle boil [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: Marc Cleroux / @tenfoldmarc reel Dew3PAQTMCs, canonical decode.
Effect 11 of 16 in his on-screen index. GATED pack — rebuild from
visible specs only.

## The rule
A wobbly hand-drawn outline traces the speaker's silhouette and BOILS
— redrawn with jitter every couple of frames so the line never sits
still — with small doodles on the spoken nouns.

## Look (MEASURED from frames)
- f_012 (montage + decode): a wobbly yellow outline traces his head and
  shoulders, with small doodle marks (circles, squiggles, arrows) around
  him. The outline is visibly re-drawn / wobbling between frames — that
  re-draw is the boil.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Cut out the subject (MediaPipe/YuNet) and trace the silhouette as a
   rough hand-drawn stroke (2–3px, uneven weight).
2. Boil: re-draw the stroke with jitter every 2 frames (12fps boil) —
   same shape, slightly different wobble each redraw. A still outline
   is a failed doodle boil.
3. Add 2–3 doodle marks on the spoken nouns (circle the word's object,
   arrow to the proof, star on the payoff) — each pops in ON its word.
4. Sound: pencil-scratch tick on each redraw cycle, soft pop per doodle.

## Trigger rule
A playful / self-aware / messy line: "here's the messy part", a sketch
of the idea, a hand-drawn diagram moment. Never on a proof beat — the
doodle is charm, not evidence.

## Face-clearance check
The outline traces AROUND the silhouette — it never crosses the face,
and no doodle sits on the face. The face stays photographic inside the
drawn line.
