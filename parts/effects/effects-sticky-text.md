# EFFECT — sticky text [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: Marc Cleroux / @tenfoldmarc reel Dew3PAQTMCs, canonical decode.
Effect 12 of 16 in his on-screen index. GATED pack — rebuild from
visible specs only.

## The rule
A label sticks to the frame like a note — slapped in on the word it
names, stuck for the line, peeled off after. A definition made physical.

## Look (MEASURED from frames)
- f_013: a yellow chip top-right reading "12 sticky text" — a
  sticky-note label in a face-clear corner, slight tilt as a physical
  object, drop shadow. The chip also carries the effect's index number,
  Marc's numbering convention (gold chip in PWC remap).

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Note card (~200x90px, gold bg, navy text, 1–4 words): enters with a
   small slap (scale 1.2 -> 1.0, 0.15s EASE OUT) + tilt as an OBJECT
   (rotation -3° to +3°), drop shadow, slap SFX.
2. The note sticks for exactly its line, then peels (corner lift +
   0.2s EASE IN out). One note per line, never two.
3. Text inside the note stays LEVEL — the note tilts, the words don't
   (no-slanted-text rule).

## Trigger rule
A label / definition / name line: "this is called X", a term the viewer
must remember, a step name in a list. The note IS the term.

## Face-clearance check
The note lives in a face-clear corner (top-right or side zone, inside
safe zones x 70–930, y 280–1480). Never over the face, never over the
chest-band caption zone.
