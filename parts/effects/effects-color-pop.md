# EFFECT — color pop [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @stocktonwalbeck / @coursecreator360 reel DfcnEEMgsNf (~28–30s,
effect 15) — spoken in the list run. The demo build is gated behind
EDITOR -> DM — NOT chased. Rebuild from visible spec only.

## The rule
On a number/claim moment, the background goes gray and the claim pops
— desaturate everything except the number.

## Look (measured from frames)
Desaturated background, giant "10 YEARS" white text, "AS A VIDEO
EDITOR" small above.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Desaturate the background to ~10% saturation (the room goes gray);
   the speaker stays at full colour — segment the subject (MediaPipe
   cutout) and hold him sharp and saturated.
2. Claim text: the number/claim in giant Anton, white with a gold
   figure — Stockton's original is giant white "10 YEARS"; PWC remap:
   the NUMBER goes GOLD, the rest white. Small qualifier line above
   ("AS A VIDEO EDITOR" shape) in white, smaller.
3. Entrance: the gray closes in over 8–12 frames (EASE OUT) ON the
   spoken number, with a low whoosh SFX; the claim text pops (SPRING,
   0.25s) on the digit. Exit: colour returns over 5–6 frames.
4. PWC skin: gold number, white qualifier, teal rim on the subject's
   edge. NO RED.
5. Trigger: a number/claim line ("10 years", "$92,000", "241 reels").
   The number in the text IS the spoken number — never a different one.

## Numbering
Blue `NN` chip top-left + name in Stockton's original. PWC remap:
GOLD chip text on NAVY (teal border), never blue.

## Face-clearance check
Nothing is placed over the face — the effect is a grade + text. The
claim text sits in a face-clear zone (top stack y >= 300 or lower
band), never over the mouth.

## Variants
- **David's spotlight** (`parts/effects/effects-spotlight.md`): same
  isolate-the-subject family — Spotlight darkens the background,
  Color Pop desaturates it. Darken vs gray; pick by the claim (a
  number wants Color Pop; a confessional line wants Spotlight).
- **Sky count-ups:** the number lands with a count-up instead of a
  grade change — same trigger (a number line), different device.

## Worked example
Number beat: the room goes gray as Q says "ten years" — giant gold
"10 YEARS" pops with "AS A VIDEO EDITOR" small above, Q himself still
in full colour, then the colour returns.
