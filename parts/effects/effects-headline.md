## The rule
On the thesis moment, the script becomes the newspaper: a fake
newspaper whose headline IS the thesis and whose article body IS the
video's script.

## Look (measured from frames)
Fake newspaper "The Daily Cut" (Tue Nov 3, 2026, AI EDITION; sections
Reels / Long-form / VSLs / Courses / Tools); headline "Ten years as a
video editor." with a red circle on "video editor" and yellow
highlights on "Headline" + "17 visual effects"; the article body IS
the video's script.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Newspaper masthead: Q's paper name ("The Daily Cut" shape — PWC
   masthead for Q: "PAID WITH CONTENT" / the lane's paper name), date
   (the real publish date), edition line, section tabs (Reels /
   Long-form / VSLs / Courses / Tools — Q's lanes).
2. Headline: the thesis line, verbatim from the script, in a serif
   headline face. The key phrase gets a GOLD circle drawn on (draw-on,
   0.4s) — Stockton's original uses a RED circle; PWC remap is GOLD.
   Yellow highlights on the device name + effect count map to GOLD
   highlight (teal for the second highlight if two are needed).
3. Article body: the video's ACTUAL script, set as newspaper columns
   in small type — the script is the article. The body is legible at
   phone size for the first 2–3 lines; the rest is texture.
4. Entrance: the paper slams in (hard cut, full-frame between beats)
   ON the thesis line, with a paper-slam SFX; the circle draws on as
   the key phrase is spoken.
5. PWC skin: navy paper bed, gold masthead + headline, teal section
   tabs, PWC badge in the masthead corner. NO RED anywhere.

## Numbering

GOLD chip text on NAVY (teal border), never blue.

## Face-clearance check

on screen, the paper is a dimmed bed behind a face-clear zone.
