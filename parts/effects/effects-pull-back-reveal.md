# EFFECT — pull-back reveal [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: Marc Cleroux / @tenfoldmarc reel Dew3PAQTMCs, canonical decode.
Effect 14 of 16 in his on-screen index. GATED pack — rebuild from
visible specs only.

## The rule
The camera pulls back from a tight shot to reveal the speaker was
inside a frame all along — a picture-in-picture of the edit itself.
The wider context IS the reveal.

## Look (MEASURED from frames)
- f_016 (the timeline frame): a small PiP of himself inside the editor
  timeline, with the checklist (cut pauses / add effects / export) and
  the audio waveform around it — the pull-back lands on the edit UI.
- f_030 (decode): PiP of himself mid-reel, the before+after / pull-back
  device in action.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Start tight (face ~80% of frame) on the setup line.
2. Pull back over 0.6s EASE OUT: the shot shrinks into a PiP frame and
   the surrounding context appears (timeline, checklist, waveform, or
   the real workspace). Hold ~1s.
3. Punch back in on the next line (hard cut, no slow push — the return
   is a cut, not a zoom). Sound: whoosh out on the pull, click on the
   landing.
4. The revealed context must be REAL (the actual timeline, the real
   checklist) — never a fake UI.

## Trigger rule
A reveal-the-process line: "here's what this looks like inside the
edit", "and this is the frame it lives in", a before/after setup. One
pull-back per video — twice reads gimmicky.

## Face-clearance check
The face is never covered during the pull — it shrinks INTO the PiP,
staying fully visible. The PiP frame edge never crosses the face.

## Variants / cross-links
- **Rabbit hole** (`parts/effects/effects-rabbit-hole.md`): PiP of the
  video inside the video — the nested-video version. Pull-back reveal
  is the CAMERA-MOVE version (tight -> wide), rabbit hole is the
  nested-play version.
- **Timeline checklist** (`parts/render-pipelines/
  pipeline-repurposing-one-to-ten.md` §3): the checklist that usually
  sits in the revealed frame.

## Worked example (PWC)
"This video took one prompt" — tight on Q, then the pull-back reveals
him inside the editor timeline with the checklist ticking; punch back
in as he says "and here's the proof it worked".
