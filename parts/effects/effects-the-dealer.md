# EFFECT — the dealer [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @stocktonwalbeck / @coursecreator360 reel DfcnEEMgsNf (~12s,
effect 05) — spoken in the list run. The demo build is gated behind
EDITOR -> DM — NOT chased. Rebuild from visible spec only.

## The rule
On a rules/steps moment, deal the steps like playing cards — fanned in
front of the speaker, one card per step, dealt on the spoken word.

## Look (measured from frames)
Playing cards fanned in front of him: "Rules" (01) + black cards
02/03/04.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Card set: 3–5 cards, one per step/rule. Card 01 names the set
   ("Rules", "Steps", "The 4 moves"); the rest carry one step each.
2. Fan: cards spread in a hand-fan in the chest band / lower graphic
   zone, in empty space, inside safe zones (x 70–930, y 280–1480).
3. Deal motion: each card slides in from the deck edge (EASE OUT, 6–8
   frames) ON its spoken step, with a card-flick SFX; the dealt card
   lifts slightly (SPRING pop) as its step is named.
4. PWC skin: navy cards, gold borders, gold numbers, white step text;
   the active card's border goes teal. NO RED — Stockton's black cards
   stay black/navy; any red suit or red accent maps to gold.
5. Trigger: a rules/steps line ("the rules are…", "three steps",
   "here's how it works"). The cards persist after dealing — the
   completed fan is the payoff.

## Numbering
Blue `NN` chip top-left + name in Stockton's original. PWC remap:
GOLD chip text on NAVY (teal border), never blue.

## Face-clearance check
The fan sits in front of the CHEST, never over the face and never over
the mouth. Q presents with an open palm (the present gesture) — the
cards arrive as he opens his hand.
