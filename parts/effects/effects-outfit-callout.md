# EFFECTS — outfit / gear callout + comment pile (@patlaw)

Source: @patlaw reel DfKciU1o6zV (Nov 2026) — INFERRED reconstruction; the
densest single-video command catalog in the sweep. The /edit catalog itself
is a variant on zachdoesai-command-catalog (already in parts).

## The rule
Name the fit, don't just wear it. Outfit callouts ('HAT / Brixton', 'FRAMES /
Tart Optical', 'KNIT TOP / Anon') sit beside the body as small label + brand
lines — a wardrobe/gear callout Q can lift for his own fits. The pegboard
'BOOM' moment is the punctuation: one loud label that lands on the payoff
word.

## Executable steps
1. **Callout:** label + brand, two lines, small caps, placed beside the body
   part it names (hat → beside the head, never over the face; frames → beside
   the glasses line; top → beside the torso). Each callout pops in on the
   spoken mention with a micro-tick, holds 0.8s, exits with a tick.
2. **Pegboard BOOM:** one big label ('BOOM' or Q's payoff word) on a pegboard-
   style full-screen card, hard cut in on the payoff word, hold 0.6s, hard cut
   out. Boards over footage cut in hard — no cross-fade.
3. **Comment pile:** real comments as social proof behind the speaker — a
   stack of real comment cards, slightly fanned, behind Q's shoulder. Only
   real comments, verbatim, with handles visible; never fabricated.
4. **Catalog:** the /edit command catalog on one clip — each named operation
   demonstrated on the same take, one per beat.

## Worked example (PWC)
Q's fit callouts on a talking-head take: 'JERSEY / FUBU 05' beside the torso,
'GOLDS' beside the smile line — then the pegboard card 'PAID WITH CONTENT'
on the payoff. Real waitlist comments piled behind him for the close.

## Human-made check
Every callout names something Q actually wears / owns; every comment in the
pile is a real comment, copied verbatim.

## PWC skin
Teal label text, gold brand line, navy backing chip. NO RED. Callouts never
over the face. CapCut-native motion.
