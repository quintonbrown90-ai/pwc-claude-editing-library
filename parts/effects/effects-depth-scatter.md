# EFFECT — depth scatter [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: Marc Cleroux / @tenfoldmarc reel Dew3PAQTMCs, canonical decode.
Effect 15 of 16 in his on-screen index. GATED pack — rebuild from
visible specs only.

## The rule
The phrase's letters explode along the z-axis — near letters huge, far
letters small, staggered a few frames apart — over a wall of tiles.
Depth, not flatness.

## Look (MEASURED from frames)
- f_017: giant "depth scatter" type across the frame — "depth" in
  white, "scatter" in yellow/gold — letters at different depths and
  scales, scattered over a wall of video tiles behind him.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Split the phrase into letters. Assign each a depth: near letters
   1.6–2.0x scale with a soft shadow, far letters 0.6–0.8x, mid letters
   1.0x. Stagger entrances 2–3 frames apart, EASE OUT, on the spoken
   syllables.
2. Bed: a wall of real tiles (Q's own video stills / real creator
   tiles — P1 wall family) at low opacity behind the type.
3. Settle: all letters land and hold for the payoff word (~0.5s), then
   hard cut out — never a slow fade.
4. Sound: a tick per landing letter, layered landing on the payoff word.

## Trigger rule
A list / scatter moment: "all of these", "scattered everywhere", a
catalog landing. The phrase must be short (2 words) — long phrases
scatter into mush.

## Face-clearance check
The type scatters in the graphic zones (top band + sides); Q's face
stays clear in the centre. If the take is a close-up with no side
space, the scatter plays full-frame BETWEEN beats, not over him.

## Variants / cross-links
- **Mosaic drop** (`parts/effects/effects-mosaic-drop.md`): the catalog
  drops as a mosaic — scatter is the DEPTH version (letters at
  different z), mosaic is the GRID version.
- **Headline wall** (`parts/effects/effects-headline-wall.md`): the tile
  wall behind the scatter is the same wall family.

## Worked example (PWC)
"All of these — every one a real video" — "EVERY TILE" scatters in
depth over the P1 wall as he says it, landing on "real".
