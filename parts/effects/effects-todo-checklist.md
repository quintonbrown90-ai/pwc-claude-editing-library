# EFFECT — to-do checklist [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @david_knowsai reel DfdAvbfRQY5 (12.6–16.6s) — his words: "adds a
checklist beside you that ticks off as you talk." Command: `/effects
to do`. Skill gated, NOT public — rebuild from this visible spec only.

## The rule
A checklist sits BESIDE the speaker (in empty space) and ticks items off
one by one as he talks — each tick lands on the spoken item.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Checklist panel: glassmorphic card (black 65% opacity, rounded),
   placed in a face-clear side zone, inside safe zones.
2. Rows: 3–5 items, pending at 40% opacity. Each row activates to 100%
   on its spoken item name, then gets its verdict: teal check + tick SFX
   on landing (Q remap — never red/green verdict colours).
3. Tick motion: Pop scale 1.6→1.0, 0.18s EASE OUT. Rows persist after
   judgement — the board fills; the unfinished board is the open loop
   (same grammar as Sky D4).
4. PWC skin: panel navy/teal/gold; checks teal; item text white.

## Face-clearance check
The panel sits BESIDE the speaker, never over the face and never over the
chest-band caption zone. If the take is a close-up with no side space,
the panel goes top-stack (y ≥ 300) instead.

## Worked example
"Four systems" beat: the checklist lists the 4 systems; each ticks off
as Q names it; the completed board holds into the CTA.
