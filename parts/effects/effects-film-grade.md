## The rule
The mood changes in one tap: the whole picture shifts to a warm film grade
on command, the speaker never moves, the grade IS the moment. The grade is
the mood — it colours the frame, it never recolours the brand.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. On the typed command, cross-dissolve the picture to the warm film grade
   over 8–12 frames (EASE OUT) — lifted blacks, warm highlights, gentle
   contrast curve, slight grain. The speaker stays still, face forward, for
   the grade beat — the grade is the whole picture, so the picture must hold
   still to show it.
2. The grade holds for the beat (2–4s), then dissolves back to the house
   grade on the next command (8–12 frames EASE IN). One grade per video
   section, never a grade per cut.
3. The PWC palette binds inside the grade: teal stays teal, gold stays
   gold — the warm shift lifts the shadows, it never pushes the brand
   colours toward orange or red.

## Face-clearance check
A colour shift only — nothing is placed over the face. The face stays at
full sharpness under the grade. PASS in the source video.
