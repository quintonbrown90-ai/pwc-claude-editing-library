# EFFECT — frame break [INFERRED reconstruction — geometry NOT frame-measured]

Source: Marc Cleroux / @tenfoldmarc reel Dew3PAQTMCs, canonical decode.
Effect 10 of 16 in his on-screen index. GATED pack — rebuild from
visible specs only.

## The rule
The speaker (or a device) breaks THROUGH the frame border — a hand or
a card pushes past the edge, the border cracks/slides, then re-forms.
Breaking the box is the point.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Frame border: a thin gold rule around the video frame, visible but
   quiet (2px).
2. On the trigger word, the subject's hand (or a card) pushes past the
   border with a crack SFX + 2–3 frame overshoot past the edge; the
   border segment slides/cracks where it is crossed.
3. The border re-forms (0.3s EASE OUT) as the next line starts. One
   break per video — the box breaks once and means something.
4. Sound: crack on the break, soft reset tick on the re-form.

## Trigger rule
A rule-breaking / out-of-the-box line: "nobody does it like this",
"break the format", a contrarian claim. The break lands ON the
contrarian word.

## Face-clearance check
The break happens at the frame EDGE in the graphic zone — the face
stays inside the safe frame, fully visible, never crossed by the
border or the breaking element.
