## The rule
An editing timeline sits in the lower graphic band with a glowing
playhead that moves as he talks — the video's own progress, made visible.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Timeline bar: thin track (navy, 6px) with clip blocks for each beat,
   placed in the face-clear graphic zone — inside y 280–1480 and ABOVE
   the bottom 440px caption zone (never in the IG caption area).
2. Playhead: vertical gold line + glow, moving LINEAR with the talk
   (machine motion — allowed on LINEAR). Clip blocks light up (teal) as
   the playhead passes them.
3. The timeline IS the progress device: it completes at the keyword CTA
   (same job as Q's 'Water Line' — slim teal progress line under the top
   block; this effect is its timeline-shaped cousin).
4. Sound: soft tick as the playhead crosses each clip boundary.

## Face-clearance check
The timeline lives in the lower band, clear of the face and clear of the
caption zone. Never over the chin, never in the bottom 440px.

## Worked example

playhead crosses each as Q lands the beat; the final block (CTA) glows
gold as the keyword lands.
