# EFFECT — glass widgets [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @david_knowsai reel DfdAvbfRQY5 (0.0–3.6s) — his words: "adds
floating glass panels around you." Command: `/effects glass widgets`.
Skill gated, NOT public — rebuild from this visible spec only.

## The rule
Floating glass panels (frosted, translucent, rounded) hover in the empty
space around the speaker — never over the face. They drift slowly and
catch light; they are atmosphere, not content.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Face-track the speaker (YuNet/MediaPipe cutout — same engine as the
   effects skill, v1 already in Claude).
2. Place 2–3 glass panels in the empty space above the head / at the sides,
   inside safe zones (x 70–930, y 280–1480). Panels are frosted
   (blur + white 15% fill + 1px light border), rounded corners.
3. Drift: slow EASE IN-OUT float (±8px, 3–4s cycle), staggered per panel.
   Sound: soft glass tick on entrance, nothing on the drift.
4. PWC skin: panels tinted navy/teal at low opacity; any text inside a
   panel is PWC gold, never red.

## Face-clearance check
Panels sit in EMPTY space only. If a panel drifts toward the face, it is
parked — the face zone is never covered, even by translucent glass.

## Worked example
Q's hook beat: two glass panels float at the upper-left and upper-right
while he says the hook; a third panel slides in with the proof number.
All three clear the face by ≥40px.
