# EFFECT — match cut [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @david_knowsai reel DfdAvbfRQY5 (22.2–26.7s) — his words:
"creates fast cuts between news pages with the same word staying locked
in place." Command: `/effects match cut`. Skill gated, NOT public —
rebuild from this visible spec only.

## The rule
Fast hard cuts between pages/screens, with ONE word locked in the same
screen position across every cut — the word is the anchor, the pages
change around it.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Pick the anchor word (the claim word). Place it at a fixed position
   inside safe zones, in PWC gold, Anton.
2. Cut hard between 3–5 real screens (news pages, dashboards, posts —
   real, never fake) at ~0.5s intervals. The anchor word does NOT move,
   resize, or restyle across cuts.
3. Hard cuts only — no cross-fade, no dissolve. Sound: a sharp cut tick
   on each cut.
4. PWC skin: anchor word gold; screens real (Q's own screens or real
   public pages, blurred per the blur law where people get paid).

## Face-clearance check
The anchor word sits in a face-clear zone (top stack y ≥ 300 or lower
band). The cuts never put a screen over Q's face — if Q is on screen,
the match-cut plays full-frame between his beats, not over him.

## Worked example
"Five emails" beat: the anchor word "$92,000" stays locked centre-top
while the cuts flash the 5 email screens behind it, one per email.
