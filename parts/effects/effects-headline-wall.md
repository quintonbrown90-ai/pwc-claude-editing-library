# EFFECT — headline wall [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: Marc Cleroux / @tenfoldmarc reel Dew3PAQTMCs, canonical decode.

visible specs only. (Transcript hears "headline law"; the on-screen
index reads "headline wall" — the index is canonical.)

## The rule
A wall of headline tiles fills the frame and the hero headline IS the
thesis. The wall is the proof: every tile a real headline, a real video,
a real claim.

## Look (MEASURED from frames)
- f_001: he holds a fake newspaper, headline "claude can now edit your
  videos" — the headline as a physical object, held up to camera. The
  wall form (tiles filling the frame) is the same device at full scale:
  the reel's later frames (f_017–f_021, f_027) show the tile wall
  behind him as the depth-scatter / system beats play.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Wall: a grid of real headline tiles (Q's proof tiles, real creator
   tiles — the P1 wall family). Each tile: one real headline + one real
   number or still. No invented tiles, no stock.
2. Hero headline: the thesis line in huge type across the wall (top
   band), landing ON the spoken thesis with a slam + tick SFX.
3. Tiles fill in staggered (2–3 frames apart) as the hook runs; the
   completed wall holds into the CTA — the completed wall IS the payoff
   (same grammar as the loadout bar).
4. Q's face stays present in the hook (PiP or split composite with the
   wall — never a faceless full-screen board open).

## Trigger rule
The thesis / claim beat that opens a proof-led video. The wall opens
the video (proof in the first 2s) or lands on the claim second.

## Face-clearance check
Wall plays behind him in the top band / full-frame BETWEEN beats, never
over his face. In the hook, Q's face is present (PiP / split) per the
standing rule.
