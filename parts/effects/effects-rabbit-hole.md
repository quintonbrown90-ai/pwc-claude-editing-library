# EFFECT — rabbit hole [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @stocktonwalbeck / @coursecreator360 reel DfcnEEMgsNf (~15s,
effect 07) — spoken in the list run. The demo build is gated behind
EDITOR -> DM — NOT chased. Rebuild from visible spec only.

## The rule
On a "go deeper" moment, screen-within-screen: the speaker's own video
plays on a monitor behind him — the video inside the video.

## Look (measured from frames)
Screen-within-screen: his own video playing on a monitor behind him.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. The inner screen plays a REAL Q video (the thing being gone deeper
   into), never a placeholder. It sits on a monitor/device frame in the
   empty space behind or beside him.
2. Entrance: the monitor scales in (SPRING pop, 0.3s) ON the "go
   deeper" line, with a soft whoosh SFX; the inner video is already
   playing when it lands (no black first frame).
3. The inner video plays 2–4s at low volume under Q's voice (his voice
   stays forward; the inner audio is a bed, ducked). Hard cut out — no
   fade.
4. PWC skin: monitor frame navy with a thin gold edge; PWC badge on the
   monitor's corner.
5. Trigger: a depth line ("go deeper", "inside that", "let me show you
   what's in there"). One rabbit hole per video max — a second one
   reads as a gimmick.

## Numbering
Blue `NN` chip top-left + name in Stockton's original. PWC remap:
GOLD chip text on NAVY (teal border), never blue.

## Face-clearance check
The monitor sits BEHIND or BESIDE the speaker, never over the face.
If the take is tight, the monitor goes full-frame between beats
instead of over him.

## Variants
- **Sky Form 4 reaction split:** same screen-within-screen grammar in
  Sky's breakdown lane (target reel + reaction). Rabbit Hole = the
  speaker's OWN video inside; Form 4 = someone else's reel under
  autopsy.

## Worked example
"Go deeper" beat: a monitor scales in behind Q playing his own
breakdown video as he says "inside that video is the whole system" —
it plays 3s under his voice, then cuts away.
