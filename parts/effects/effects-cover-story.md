## The rule
The video ends as a magazine cover — the speaker's best frame, a cover
headline, a cover line, a price-bar-style CTA strip. The end card IS the
cover story: the video was the article, the cover is what you remember.

## Executable steps
1. Build: Q's best frame full-bleed (real frame from the video, never an
   AI still), a huge headline across the top band (Q's exact words), one
   gold cover line, and a bottom CTA strip ("COMMENT WAITLIST").
2. Sequence: the last spoken word lands → hard cut to the cover (no
   cross-fade — boards over footage cut in hard) → cover holds 1.5s →
   CTA strip slides in with a tick.
3. Ending gate: the cover hits ≤~1s after the last word (Q's ending
   dead-air gate). No bed-only tail before the cover.
4. Face rule: the headline sits in the top band above Q's head, never over
   his face; the CTA strip sits in the bottom safe zone.
