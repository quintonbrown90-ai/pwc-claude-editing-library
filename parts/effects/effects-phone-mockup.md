## The rule
A phone floats beside the speaker showing the REAL thing — the offer
page, the DM thread, the community — so the viewer sees the destination,
not a description of it.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Phone frame (real device outline) floating in a face-clear side
   zone; screen shows the REAL page (waitlist page, Stan product, Skool
   page, DM thread) — never a redrawn fake.
2. Entrance: tilt in 5° -> 0° SPRING overshoot (0.4s) + soft pop SFX.
   The relevant row on the screen highlights (gold row) as it is spoken.
3. The screen content may scroll slowly to the spoken row — the scroll
   is the motion; the phone itself stays put once landed.
4. Exit: hard cut with the beat; never a slow drift over the face.

## Trigger rule
A "look at this" line: "here's the page", "here's the offer", "check
your phone", a DM / community / product moment. The phone shows the
destination the CTA points to.

## Face-clearance check
The phone lives in a side zone, never over the face, never over the
chest-band captions. On a close-up take, the phone goes top-stack
(y ≥ 300) or the beat plays full-frame between takes.
