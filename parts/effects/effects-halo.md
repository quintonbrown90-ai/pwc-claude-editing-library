# EFFECT — halo [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @stocktonwalbeck / @coursecreator360 reel DfcnEEMgsNf (~8–10s,
effect 03) — spoken in the list run. The demo build is gated behind
EDITOR -> DM — NOT chased. Rebuild from visible spec only.

## The rule
On a showcase moment, screens orbit ABOVE the head like a halo — a
ring of vertical phone-screen cards arcing over the speaker, never
over the face.

## Look (measured from frames)
A ring of vertical phone-screen cards arcing over his head like a halo.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Face-track the head centre; the arc is centred on the head, radius
   head width x 1.6, in the face-clear zone ABOVE the head.
2. 5–7 vertical phone-screen cards (REAL screens — Q's own videos/posts)
   on the arc, each upright (counter-rotate if the arc moves).
3. Motion: cards rise into the arc staggered (SPRING, 0.25s each) with a
   soft chime SFX; the arc holds while the showcase line lands, then
   cards lift away. Slow drift only — no spin.
4. PWC skin: card frames navy with a thin gold edge; the active card
   (the one being spoken about) gets a teal edge.
5. Trigger: a showcase line ("look at these", "all of them", a
   catalog/show-off moment). Q points UP at the arc on the gesture —
   the point IS the cue (see gestures vocabulary).

## Numbering
Blue `NN` chip top-left + name in Stockton's original. PWC remap:
GOLD chip text on NAVY (teal border), never blue.

## Face-clearance check
The arc goes ABOVE the head. No card ever crosses the face, even at the
arc's lowest point — radius is set from the tracked head box with
>=40px clearance. If the head moves, the arc follows the track.

## Variants
- **David's orbit** (`parts/effects/effects-orbit.md`): thumbnails
  circle AROUND the head in a full ring; Halo is the top-half arc only
  — same family (screens around the head, face clear), different shape.
  Orbit = full ring, slow rotation; Halo = top arc, static hold.

## Worked example
Showcase beat: 6 phone cards arc over Q's head as he says "every one
of these is real" — each card is a real Q video; the arc lifts away as
the proof line lands.
