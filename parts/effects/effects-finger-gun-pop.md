# EFFECTS — finger gun: point, and the pop-up fires (DRAFT — v9 bulk-b)

Source: @kingketo "15 video effects" speed-run (DfDCEKrAa5z), effect #1 "finger gun", 0.0–0.6s. INFERRED reconstruction from the name + timing; his effect pack is GATED — never chased.

## The rule
The speaker points — finger gun at empty space — and the pop-up fires exactly on the point. The gesture IS the trigger; the pop-up lands where the finger points, on the frame the finger extends.

## Executable steps
1. Find the point: the frame where Q's finger extends (pointing, moving, suggesting — see the body-language scanner rule: scan motion and intention, not just words).
2. Fire on the point frame: the pop-up (proof, example, number, card) lands at the fingertip position, with a pop sound, on that frame — not a beat early, not a beat late.
3. The pop-up sits in empty space beside/below the point — never over Q's face, never covering the pointing hand. Teal/gold on navy, no red.
4. Hold while the point holds; exit with a sound when the hand drops.

## Worked example (PWC)
Q points to his right on "a tile like this" — the Adley 2.8M tile pops in at his fingertip, gold pulse on the payoff. The point is the proof's cue; the tile is the proof.

## Human-made check
Every finger-gun pop traces to a real thing Q can point to (a real video, a real number, a real example) — never a stock tile fired by a generic point.

## PWC skin
Navy plate, gold border, teal accents. The pop sound is from the local licensed library. The gesture is Q's own pointing, never an animated hand.
