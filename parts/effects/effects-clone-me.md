## The rule
Two clones of the speaker flank him while he talks — one left, one right —
same clothes, same framing, same moment. The clones are the joke and the
proof: one person, three of him, no editing app. The face stays clear on all
three figures; the clones never cross the centre figure.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Segment the speaker (face-track + cutout) and duplicate the figure twice.
2. Place the clones left and right in the face-clear side zones, slightly
   smaller than the centre figure (scale ~0.9), same eye line. The centre
   figure is the speaker; the clones mirror his pose with a 2–4 frame delay
   so the trio reads as live, not pasted.
3. Entrance: clones pop in on the typed command (spring overshoot 0.25s) +
   pop SFX, one at a time (left, then right). Exit: clones peel off in
   reverse on the next command.
4. The speaker's look never breaks for the effect — eyes stay on the lens
   while the clones appear around him (same law as the David reel: the
   subject stays put, the devices move).

## Face-clearance check
Clones flank the speaker, face clear on all three. No clone ever crosses the
centre face, even at the closest point. If the head moves, all three tracks
follow it. PASS in both source videos.

holding up one finger — three figures, three deals, the count IS the visual.
