# EFFECT — zoom-through cut [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: Marc Cleroux / @tenfoldmarc reel Dew3PAQTMCs, canonical decode.
Effect 02 of 16 in his on-screen index. GATED pack — rebuild from
visible specs only.

## The rule
Fly THROUGH the frame into the next shot: a radial zoom-blur burst
ramps up on the cut point and you land on the new shot. The cut happens
INSIDE the zoom.

## Look (MEASURED from frames)
- f_002: a radial-blur zoom burst — streaks radiating from the centre,
  bright yellow-white core, the subject blurred outward. The burst IS
  the transition; there is no clean frame mid-burst.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. On the cut point, ramp a radial zoom-blur 1.0x -> 2.5x+ over 6–8
   frames (EASE IN), whoosh SFX rising with the ramp.
2. Cut to the new shot at the burst peak; land at 1.0x with a 2-frame
   settle (EASE OUT). Total ~0.35s — a punch, not a linger.
3. The new shot must be a NEW angle / new scene — a zoom-through into
   the same framing is a fake move.
4. Cap the blur so the landing frame is tack-sharp on the first clean
   frame; a soft landing fails QC.

## Trigger rule
A scene-change / "let's go inside this" line: moving from the claim to
the proof, from the room to the screen, from one example to the next.
One zoom-through per section, max.

## Face-clearance check
The burst radiates FROM the frame centre — never hold the burst on
Q's face. The landing frame has his face sharp, centred, and fully
visible in the first clean frame.
