## The rule
Split-screen stage: two angles/takes side by side on one stage — the
same line from two takes, or two takes in conversation with each other.

## Look (stated, NOT frame-measured — see flag)
Two takes side by side, split down the middle of the frame.

## Executable steps (rebuild — confirm geometry on re-sample)
1. Two takes, side by side, 50/50 vertical split (confirm on
   re-sample; if the real split is 60/40 or a stage with a gap, match
   the real geometry and update this part).
2. Both takes play in sync (same master audio under both — one unbroken
   track, the hat-talks discipline; never two competing audio tracks).
3. The split line is a thin gold divider (PWC skin); each half keeps
   its own caption zone clear — captions sit in the chest band of the
   ACTIVE half only, never straddling the split.
4. Entrance: hard cut into the split ON the line it serves; hard cut
   out. No slide-in, no wipe.
5. Trigger: a two-take line (same line, two angles; before/after takes;
   a comparison of two deliveries).
