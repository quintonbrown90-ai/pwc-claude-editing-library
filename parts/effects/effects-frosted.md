# EFFECT — frosted [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @stocktonwalbeck / @coursecreator360 reel DfcnEEMgsNf (~26–28s,
effect 14) — spoken in the list run. The demo build is gated behind
EDITOR -> DM — NOT chased. Rebuild from visible spec only.

## The rule
On a social-proof moment, frosted-glass notification cards float over
the speaker — the proof arrives as a notification.

## Look (measured from frames)
Frosted-glass notification cards (blue "coursecreator360 — 8 effects",
purple card below) floating over him.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Notification cards: frosted glass (blur + white 15% fill + 1px light
   border, rounded corners), 2–3 cards stacked, in the empty space
   around the speaker, inside safe zones.
2. Card content is REAL social proof — a real handle + a real count or
   real comment (Q's real notifications), never invented. Blur per the
   complete blur law where other people getting paid appear.
3. Entrance: cards slide in staggered from the side (EASE OUT, 8
   frames) ON the proof line, with a notification-pop SFX per card;
   they float (slow EASE IN-OUT, ±6px) while the line lands.
4. PWC skin: cards tinted navy/teal at low opacity (Stockton's blue /
   purple cards map to teal / gold); handle text white, count in gold.
   NO RED, no purple — PWC palette only.
5. Trigger: a social-proof line ("8 effects", a follower count, a real
   comment, "people are asking"). No proof in the words = no cards.

## Numbering
Blue `NN` chip top-left + name in Stockton's original. PWC remap:
GOLD chip text on NAVY (teal border), never blue.

## Face-clearance check
Cards float in EMPTY space around the speaker — never over the face,
never over the mouth. If the take is tight, the cards stack in a side
zone instead of over him.

## Variants
- **David's glass widgets** (`parts/effects/effects-glass-widgets.md`):
  same frosted-glass family, different job — glass widgets are
  atmosphere (empty panels drifting); Frosted cards carry social-proof
  CONTENT (a handle + a count). Atmosphere vs notification.
- **Sky fake-UI notification cards:** same notification grammar in
  Sky's lane (Q-lane template V4) — Frosted is the glass-skinned
  version of that device.

## Worked example
Social-proof beat: two frosted cards float in beside Q as he says
"eight effects in one video" — top card his handle + the count in
gold, second card a real comment, then they lift away.
