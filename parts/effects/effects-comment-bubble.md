## The rule
A real comment pops as a rounded bubble beside the speaker — the proof
is social, not just numeric. The bubble lands ON the line it proves.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Bubble: rounded card (navy, gold border, white text) with a tail
   pointing at the speaker or the claim; 1–2 lines, REAL comment text
   only — never invented words.
2. Entrance: SPRING overshoot pop (0.25s) ON the trigger word + pop SFX;
   hold ~1.5s; exit EASE IN. One bubble per line, never two.
3. Placement: face-clear side zone, inside safe zones. If the comment
   names a person, blur per the complete blur law (face / profile pic /
   name blurred on hidden-method proofs; real creator comments shown
   as-is with @handle visible, never framed as endorsers).

## Trigger rule
A social-proof line: a follower's words, a real comment, "somebody
asked…". The bubble IS the comment. Never use it to carry the script's
own claims — only real audience words.

## Face-clearance check
Bubble never overlaps the face or the chest-band captions. Tail points
AT the speaker; the bubble itself stays in empty space.
