# EFFECT — the arena [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @stocktonwalbeck / @coursecreator360 reel DfcnEEMgsNf (~22s,
effect 11) — spoken in the list run. The demo build is gated behind
EDITOR -> DM — NOT chased. Rebuild from visible spec only.

## The rule
On a competition/lineup moment, tiles in an arena arc — an arched,
curved row of video tiles above the speaker, like a stadium.

## Look (measured from frames)
Arched/curved row of video tiles above him (stadium arc) on a navy grid.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. 5–9 tiles (REAL videos — Q's own or the real lineup being compared)
   on an upward arc above the speaker's head, in the face-clear zone.
2. Arc geometry: tiles follow a shallow stadium curve (centre tile
   highest); each tile upright, slight tilt toward the centre (<=8°).
3. Entrance: tiles rise into the arc staggered from the centre outward
   (SPRING, 0.25s each) with a rising chime SFX; the arc holds while
   the lineup line lands. Q points UP at the arc on the gesture — the
   point IS the cue.
4. PWC skin: navy grid bed, gold arc edge, the tile being spoken about
   gets a teal edge + a gold rank number.
5. Trigger: a competition/lineup line ("the lineup", "who's winning",
   "everyone in this space", a ranking). No contest in the words = no
   arena.

## Numbering
Blue `NN` chip top-left + name in Stockton's original. PWC remap:
GOLD chip text on NAVY (teal border), never blue.

## Face-clearance check
The arc sits ABOVE the head with >=40px clearance at its lowest tile.
No tile ever dips toward the face; the arc follows the tracked head
box if he moves.

## Variants
- **Halo** (`parts/effects/effects-halo.md`): same above-the-head arc
  grammar, different job — Halo showcases screens (a ring of
  phone cards); Arena ranks a lineup (stadium arc of video tiles).
  Halo = showcase, Arena = competition.
- **Sky D10 grid inserts:** flat grid version of the same lineup job;
  Arena is the curved, stadium-shaped version.

## Worked example
Lineup beat: 7 tiles arc above Q as he says "here's the whole lineup"
— the tile he names gets a teal edge + gold rank number, then the arc
lifts away.
