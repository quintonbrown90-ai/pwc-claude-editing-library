# EFFECT — digital zoom [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: Marc Cleroux / @tenfoldmarc reel Dew3PAQTMCs, canonical decode.
Effect 06 of 16 in his on-screen index. GATED pack — rebuild from
visible specs only.

## The rule
A digital punch-in WITH its own HUD: face-track brackets, a zoom scale
bar, and a big readout. The zoom is shown as a camera act, not hidden
as an edit.

## Look (MEASURED from frames)
- f_007: punched-in close-up with gold face-track corner brackets, "REC
  00:00:06:14" bottom-left, a zoom scale bar (1x–2x) with its marker,
  and "1.59×" big bottom-right. "06 digital zoom" highlighted in the
  index.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Punch in to 1.5–1.7× on the trigger word (0.3s EASE OUT), hold while
   the line lands, release on the next beat (hard cut back, never a
   slow push-out).
2. HUD: face-track corner brackets (gold, tracking the face box),
   zoom scale bar 1x–2x with the marker at the live value, big readout
   ("1.59×" style) bottom-right, REC timecode bottom-left.
3. The HUD is a DEVICE, not a fake camera claim — the timecode reads
   the real clip time; never fake a recording that didn't happen.
4. Sound: lens-whir tick on the punch, soft click on the release.

## Trigger rule
An emphasis line: zoom in on the point, the number, the face of the
claim. One punch per section, max — every line zoomed is no line zoomed.

## Face-clearance check
The brackets FRAME the face — they never cross it. The readout and
scale bar sit in the corners, inside safe zones, clear of the
chest-band captions.
