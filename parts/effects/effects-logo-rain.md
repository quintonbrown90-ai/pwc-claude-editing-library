## The rule
The brand falls from the sky: logos rain down around the speaker, face
clear, and the shower IS the brand moment. The rain is the brand — it falls
around him, never on him.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Emit logos from the top of the frame on the typed command: 8–12 logos,
   staggered starts, falling at varied speeds (slow fall, 2–3s per logo),
   slight drift left/right — the rain is gentle, not a storm.
2. Logos fall in the face-clear side zones and the empty space above the
   shoulders — none crosses the head box. Each logo fades out 0.3s before it
   reaches the bottom of the frame (it dissolves, it never lands on him).
3. Sound: soft patter per logo landing (faded out), never a downpour loop.

## Face-clearance check
Logos fall around the speaker, face clear. The head box is a no-fall zone —
any logo whose path crosses it is re-routed to a side zone. PASS in the
source video.
