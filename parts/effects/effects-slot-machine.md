## The rule
The cards spin like a slot machine, the combination lands, the final
combination flashes and grows. The landing is the biggest moment, not the spin.

## Executable steps
1. Build: 3 cards (or 3 columns) in a row, each cycling through options
   (effects, hooks, numbers) at staggered speeds — left stops first, right
   last.
2. Sequence: spin (0.6–0.9s, fast cycle) → columns lock one by one with a tick
   each → final combination holds → flash + size increase (+15–20% scale,
   0.15s) on the landed combination.
3. Trigger: the spoken line names the combination ("these three") — the cards
   land on the words, one column per word where possible.
4. Face rule: the machine sits in the lower two-thirds or beside Q, never over
   his face; the flash is a scale + brightness pop, not a full-screen white.
