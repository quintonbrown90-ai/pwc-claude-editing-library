## The rule
A dizzy sway: the frame rocks and the background breathes while the
speaker stays centred and sharp — the viewer feels the spin, the face
never blurs.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Face-track the head centre; lock the subject's face position.
2. Sway: rotate the background layer ±3° (slow sine, ~1s period) with a
   subtle dolly-zoom breathe (scale 1.00 -> 1.04 -> 1.00) — the room
   moves, the face does not.
3. Duration ~1s, EASE IN-OUT, on the trigger word; exit by settling
   dead-centre on the next line. Sound: low wobble / pitch-bent whoosh.
4. Cap the sway at 3° and the zoom at 1.04x — past that the face reads
   drunk, not dizzy.

## Trigger rule
A disorientation line: lost, spinning, stuck, "I had no idea which
way was up". One vertigo beat per video, max — twice reads seasick.

## Face-clearance check
The face stays centred, sharp and fully readable at every point of the
sway. If the track slips, the effect is cut — never ship a blurred face.

## Variants / cross-links
- **Camera whip zoom** (`parts/camera-moves/camera-whip-zoom.md`): fast
  directional zoom as a camera move; vertigo is the slow rotational
  sway, not a zoom.
