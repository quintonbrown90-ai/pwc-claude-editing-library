# EFFECT — reveal bar [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @stocktonwalbeck / @coursecreator360 reel DfcnEEMgsNf (~30–33s,
effect 16) — spoken in the list run. The demo build is gated behind
EDITOR -> DM — NOT chased. Rebuild from visible spec only.

## The rule
On a before/after moment, a white reveal bar wipes colour in — one
side grayscale, the other full colour, the bar at the seam.

## Look (measured from frames)
Vertical wipe: left ~85% grayscale, right strip full colour, white
reveal bar at the seam.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Split the frame vertically: left side desaturated (~10%), right side
   full colour. The seam is a vertical white bar (4–6px) with a soft
   glow.
2. Wipe motion: the bar travels left → right (EASE IN-OUT, ~1.0s) ON
   the "after" word, with a sweep SFX; the colour follows the bar. The
   bar pauses at the end for the payoff beat, then the full-colour
   frame holds.
3. The two sides are the SAME shot (before grade vs after grade) — a
   real grade change, never two different shots pretending to be one.
4. PWC skin: the bar carries a thin gold edge on its right side; the
   "after" side gets a small gold label (the after-state name) in the
   top corner, inside safe zones.
5. Trigger: a before/after line ("before it was… now it's…", "from this
   to this"). No before/after in the words = no bar.

## Numbering
Blue `NN` chip top-left + name in Stockton's original. PWC remap:
GOLD chip text on NAVY (teal border), never blue.

## Face-clearance check
The bar never crosses the face mid-wipe on a held frame — if Q is on
screen, the wipe runs on a full-frame B-roll/grade shot between beats,
or the bar travels in the empty space beside him. The face is never
half-gray / half-colour at a held moment.

## Variants
- **Sky D8 before/after pairs:** same job (old vs new) in Sky's
  side-by-side grammar — D8 is two panels; Reveal Bar is one frame
  with a travelling seam. Bar = motion, D8 = comparison.

## Worked example
Before/after beat: the gray left / colour right split sits on Q's
"before" line — the white bar wipes right as he says "now it's this"
and the full-colour frame holds into the next beat.
