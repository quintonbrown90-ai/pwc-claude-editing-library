# EFFECT — paper cut [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: Marc Cleroux / @tenfoldmarc reel Dew3PAQTMCs, canonical decode.
Effect 07 of 16 in his on-screen index. GATED pack — rebuild from
visible specs only.

## The rule
The word becomes cut paper: each letter on its own torn-paper tile,
tiles dropping in staggered, collage on a paper bed. Craft you can
feel — the opposite of a flat digital plate.

## Look (MEASURED from frames)
- f_008: cut-paper letters spelling "p a p e r / c u t" — each letter
  on its own torn-edge tile, slight rotations, collage over a warm
  paper-textured bed, yellow highlight tiles behind some letters.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Word split into letter tiles: torn edges (irregular, not straight
   cuts), subtle drop shadow, rotation ±4° per tile.
2. Tiles drop in staggered 2 frames apart, EASE OUT, with a paper-flick
   SFX per tile; the payoff letters land on gold tiles.
3. Bed: paper texture in navy (PWC) — his warm paper maps to navy;
   keep the torn-paper craft feel, never a flat colour plate.
4. Hold ~1s on the completed word, then hard cut out.

## Trigger rule
A craft / DIY / "cut" line, or the word itself is the thesis ("cut the
fluff", "cut pauses"). One paper-cut word per video — it is a loud
device; twice is wallpaper.

## Face-clearance check
The collage plays full-frame BETWEEN beats or in the top band — never
over Q's face. If he is on screen, the tiles sit above his head.

## Worked example (PWC)
"CUT the pauses" — the letters C-U-T drop in as torn gold tiles on
"cut", the rest of the word follows in white tiles; the edit then
actually cuts the pauses.

## PWC skin
Navy paper bed, gold tiles for payoff letters, white tiles for the
rest. Torn edges kept. NO RED.
