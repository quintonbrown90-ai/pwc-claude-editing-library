## The rule
A 6-second silent showcase: one static shot + staggered card pops. The cards
ARE the content — the viewer reads, the music holds attention, the full set
at the end is the payoff. The cheapest possible format for a "5 things" list.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Canvas: one static shot (Q working — laptop, desk, real setup), held the
   whole 6s. No cuts, no camera move — the pops are the only motion.
2. Headline lock: bold headline + subline pinned top-center the whole video,
   readable in a 1-second glance (the promise stays readable for the full 6s).
3. Cards: 5 cards, bottom-up stagger (card 1 ~1s, card 2 ~2s, cards 3–5 by
   ~4–5s). Each card pops in (spring overshoot 0.2s) + pop SFX, then holds.
   By the end all 5 tile the lower frame — the full set IS the payoff frame.
4. Card content: one skill/system per card — name + one-line job it does.
   PWC cards: navy field, gold border, white name, teal one-liner.

## Face-clearance check
Cards sit in the empty lower frame and never cover the face — the face is in
profile/small mid-frame, the cards tile below it. PWC version keeps cards
clear of Q's face the same way. PASS in the source video.
