# EFFECT — apple toggles [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @david_knowsai reel DfdAvbfRQY5 (26.7–34.9s) — his words: "adds
iPhone style switches that turn on underneath your face." Command:
`/effects apple toggles`. Skill gated, NOT public — rebuild from this
visible spec only.

## The rule
iPhone-style toggle switches appear UNDERNEATH the face (chest band) and
flip on one by one as he talks — each flip is a yes/no verdict on a
spoken item.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Toggle row: 2–4 iPhone-style switches (rounded track + circle knob),
   placed in the chest band UNDER the chin with ≥40px clearance, inside
   safe zones. Labels in white, left of each switch.
2. Flip motion: knob slides EASE OUT 6 frames + track fills teal (ON) or
   stays grey (OFF). Tick/pop SFX on each flip. Flips land ON the spoken
   item, never before it.
3. PWC skin: ON = teal track, knob white; OFF = grey track. Never green
   (Sky's verdict colour) and never red — Q remap binds.
4. One flip per spoken item; the row persists after flipping (the
   completed row is the payoff).

## Face-clearance check
Toggles go UNDERNEATH the face — chest band only, ≥40px below the chin,
never over the face, never over the mouth. If the take is too tight for a
chest band, the toggles go to a side zone instead.
