structures (cohort, 2 lives/week, waitlist nurture, proof packs).

## The rule
Followings are built on identity, inside language, rituals, retention
loops, plural proof, a shared story, and visible progression — never on
tips alone and never on manipulation.

## Executable steps
1. Identity first: every video's first line names who it is for
   ("creators under 10K"), never what it is about.
2. Inside language: keywords (WAITLIST, READY, MATH, CALENDAR) are badges
   — same words in videos, captions, DMs, and Skool.
3. Rituals: 2 lives/week (Mon 12 PM teaching, Fri 12 PM Lab/Hot Seats),
   same times, protected by record-ahead drops on low-energy weeks.
4. Retention loops: no video ends closed — last line points to the next
   video, the keyword, the waitlist, or the live.
5. Plural proof: the wall is OTHER creators' paid videos (P1 v9's 15
   real tiles), never Q and Kiya's own grid.
6. Visible progression: BUILD → CREATE → MONETIZE → SCALE, with Day 90
   / Graduation dates on the calendar.

## Worked example
A waitlister's journey: proof video → comment WAITLIST → 3-Wins PDF →
DM broadcasts + avatar warm-ups → Dec 7 join link in DM → Day 1 live
12 PM → Fri Lab hot seat. Every step names the next one.
