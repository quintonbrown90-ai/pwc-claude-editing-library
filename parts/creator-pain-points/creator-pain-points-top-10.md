# CREATOR PAIN POINTS — Coach Specialist (mastermind #15)

Source: `research/knowledge/CREATOR-PAIN-POINTS.md` — ranked by frequency
+ intensity, exact creator wording, public sources cited (Talentir Sept
2026 whitepaper; r/UGCcreators; Velena; laptopandcoffee).

## The rule
The top 10 pains are the content calendar and the curriculum. Every pain
feeds a topic (Content Strategy) and an objection line (Sales).

## The top 10 (ranked)
1. "What do I charge?" — rate negotiation is the #1 challenge (50% of
   creators call it their biggest challenge).
2. Getting paid late — or never (62% rank timely pay #1; 1/3–9/10 paid
   late; net-90 reads as a free loan).
3. "We'll gift you products instead of paying" (gifted-only = banned in
   r/UGCcreators; $150/video minimum).
4. Endless revisions — "one small edit" = a reshoot (31% top pain; two
   rounds is the norm; reshoot = new fee).
5. Usage-rights grabs — perpetuity is the most rejected clause (30–90
   days paid usage = +20–50% on base).
6. Pitches ignored — the portfolio kills the pitch (send 3 top videos,
   not 300; Canva portfolios read overpriced).
7. Conflicting advice — "one person says to do one thing, the other
   says the opposite" (month 4, worked hard, "I'm PISSED").
8. Posting for free with nothing to show (a 30s video is several hours
   of work; the pay is not).
9. Earning under a living wage (50.7% under $15K/yr; 56.6% of full-time
   under living wage).
10. Burnout — filming in the fifteen minutes before a shift (Q's origin
    is the mirror: 5 kids, no village, 2,000 miles on faith).

## Executable steps
1. Weekly re-sweep: r/UGCcreators, r/VideoEditing, IG captions, creator
   complaint threads. Log new pains verbatim + URL + date.
2. Re-rank on frequency × intensity. Hand the top 3 to Content Strategy
   (topics) and Sales (objections) each week.
3. Never invent a pain point. No public source + quote = not in the file.

## Worked example
Pain #1 → video: "Your rate is a system, not a guess" (hook: "You're
charging $150 because you guessed. Here's the math.") → keyword MATH →
flow delivers the rate sheet → waitlist.
