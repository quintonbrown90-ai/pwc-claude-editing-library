# PAIN-POINT → SOLUTION BANK (mastermind #15)

**Owner:** Creator Coach / Pain-Points Specialist (mastermind #15).
**Rule:** never invent a pain. Every entry carries its source — the exact
creator wording where it exists — and Q's answer. Entries 1–10 are the
ranked top 10 from `research/knowledge/CREATOR-PAIN-POINTS.md` (re-ranked
Nov 8, 2026). Entries 11–20 are NEW, mined from the 630 DECODE.md files
in `research/video-harvest/` this pass — each sourced to creator + decode.
**How to use it:** a pain is a topic (Content Strategy), an objection
line (Sales), and a hook (Hook Agent). The bank is the content calendar:
one pain per video, solution on screen, keyword at the close.

## The ranked ten (from CREATOR-PAIN-POINTS.md — full evidence there)

### P1. "What do I charge?" — rate negotiation is the #1 challenge
**Source:** Talentir Creator Relations White Paper, Sept 2026 (250,000+
creator payments): half of creators call rate negotiation their biggest
challenge. r/UGCcreators: portfolios that make creators "look overpriced".
**Solution (Q's answer):** rate-setting minimums + how usage/extra
deliverables change price — still owed from Q in his own words (Group 2
carousel block, Nov 6). **Content angle:** "your rate is a system, not a
guess." **Keyword:** MATH.

### P2. Getting paid late — or never
**Source:** Talentir, Sept 2026: 62% rank fair and timely payment #1;
between 1/3 and 9/10 creators have been paid late; net-90 reads as a free
loan. Creator wording (Velena): "your landlord does not accept skincare
as rent."
**Solution:** the hidden-method payouts + proof-led content; the community
teaches the payment terms to demand (net-30, 50/50, usage priced
separately). **Content angle:** "net-90 is a free loan — stop giving it."

### P3. "Every month starts at zero" — the one-off-deal trap [NEW #3, Nov 8]
**Source:** ugcroster.com: "If 70 percent or more of your income is
one-off, every month starts at zero." $4,000–$5,000/mo is a realistic
full-time floor; at $300/video that's 14 deliverables a month — "that
pace burns out fast"; a $300 video eating 6 hours = $50/hr before
expenses — "a gig, not a business."
**Solution:** MONETIZE phase — retainer math (Chad Lubinski: "retainer is
the gold standard", LMNT $1,500/mo origin). **Content angle:** "one-off
deals are a job with a monthly reset — retainers are the business."

### P4. "We'll gift you products instead of paying"
**Source:** Velena (2026): the #1 line creators hear. r/UGCcreators bans
gifted-only collabs; $150/video minimum.
**Solution:** Premium $97/mo promise — "posting for free to deal-ready in
90 days" means never working for product again. **Content angle:** "your
landlord doesn't accept skincare as rent."

### P5. Pitches ignored — the portfolio is killing the pitch
**Source:** r/UGCcreators (agency hirer, 30,000+ creators hired): "Linking
a massive portfolio, especially with the WRONG DATA, is killing your
pitches in 3 main ways: (1) makes you look overpriced, (2) you're not
showing the right data, (3) takes too much time." New signal (Nov 8):
"The pitching itself is a second job" — finding verified contacts,
writing personalized pitches, following up (ugcroster.com; clippingdeals,
Jun 19, 2026).
**Solution:** the pitch system (steps + 2–3 real pitch lines, names
removed) — still owed from Q in his own words. **Content angle:** "3
videos, not 300." **Keyword:** PITCH.

### P6. Endless revisions — "one small edit" that is really a reshoot
**Source:** Talentir: 31% name excessive revisions a top pain. Two rounds
is the norm. Velena: each "one small edit" "moves cost and risk onto the
creator."
**Solution:** contract language in the curriculum (revisions defined and
capped, reshoot = new fee). **Content angle:** "a new script is a reshoot
— a reshoot is a new fee."

### P7. Usage-rights grabs — perpetuity is the most rejected clause
**Source:** Talentir: 30–90 days paid usage is the norm at +20–50% on
base. "Perpetuity is the single most rejected clause in creator forums."
laptopandcoffee: "Don't assume paid-ad usage rights are included."
**Solution:** usage pricing as a core curriculum module. **Content
angle:** "perpetuity is the clause that costs you the most — it's also
the one you can price."

### P8. Agencies that take a cut but send no work [NEW #8, Nov 8]
**Source:** modliflex.com: "A creator who onboarded with several agencies
heard of no work afterwards." A manager takes ~20% but "most agencies
expect about 80% of a creator's business leads to come from brands
contacting the creator directly." r/UGCcreators: "I don't know of any UGC
creators who have an agency managing them!"
**Solution:** the pitch system + direct outreach (cold email + IG DM) as
the primary route; agencies as one source of briefs, never the plan.
**Content angle:** "an agency roster is not a pipeline."

### P9. Conflicting advice — "one person says to do one thing, the other says the opposite"
**Source:** r/UGCcreators, verbatim: "I'm in month 4 and have worked my
ass off. One person says to do one thing, the other says the opposite.
That's all" — posted as "I'm PISSED today."
**Solution:** ONE system (BUILD → CREATE → MONETIZE → SCALE), one voice,
one community. **Content angle:** "stop collecting advice, run one
system."

### P10. Posting for free with nothing to show — month 4, worked hard, no deal
**Source:** same r/UGCcreators thread. Velena: "a good thirty second video
is several hours of scripting, filming and editing, and the runtime is
not the size of the job" — the work is real, the pay is not.
**Solution:** the proof packs (P1–P35, RC1–RC35) — real creators'
receipts, and the 90-day path. **Content angle:** "posting for free is
the job — getting paid for it is the system." **Keyword:** WAITLIST.

**Live pains below the top 10 (kept in the file, Nov 8 re-rank):**

## New pains mined from the 630 decodes (Nov 8, 2026)

### P11. "Nobody shows you how" — the process is hidden behind the result
**Source:** @agi_point, "4-step AI video editor" (reel De5VcwvFTMK, Oct 24,
2026): "Everyone is editing their reels with Claude Code now, but nobody
shows you how." — 8K likes, 13K comments. Same pain, second voice:
@kelvin.martin1 (reel DeqFCTpO7bu): "My algorithm is full of videos like
this… AI made my video, Claude made my video… It's a little bit more that
goes into it."
**Solution:** show the whole process on screen (Format A: narrated
live-build — the build IS the content). Q's record-along (Oct 29 order:
record everything through the launch) is this pain answered. **Content
angle:** "everybody shows the result — here's the whole process."

### P12. "My videos all look the same" — the cheap-AI-slop fear
**Source:** @corbinsylk, "You've probably seen a thousand videos saying
Claude edited my content" (Modern Vox EP.06): the same effect built twice
— few-line prompt vs 27,000-char prompt — verdict on screen: "flat,
lifeless, amateur" vs "immersive, clean, professional." Q's own verdict
(Nov 7, SELF-LEARNING.md): Claude's edits were "cheap AI slop — same 12
components in the same rhythm across all 15 videos."
**Solution:** specificity is the quality lever (detailed skills over
one-liners); the human-made check (Q's @rpn bar) gates every render.
**Content angle:** "if every video looks the same, the prompt was the
problem — not the tool."

### P13. "Editing takes hours I don't have" — no time to edit
**Source:** @kingketo, "I'm ashamed to admit this, but I messed up a lot
when making this video": film a deliberately messy take, let Claude erase
it — the 5-minute turnaround claim as a pacing promise. @shedoesai,
ChatCut (reel Db5Z8cixSEJ, Aug 11, 2026 — 7K likes, 15K comments): "Keep
the good takes, tighten my pauses, add captions, and motion graphics
every 2-3 seconds." @minolee.mp4: dead-space >0.1s cut rule.
**Solution:** the through-edit standard (no pauses, no stumbles, no dead
air — FaceTime flow) + Claude/CapCut doing the cutting. **Content
angle:** "film it messy, ship it clean — the edit is the system."

### P14. "I'm basically guessing at my own Instagram"
**Source:** @liamjohnston.ai, "If you're making content without this,
you're basically guessing at your own Instagram" (reel De6xXw-Aldw):
170 likes / 480 comments — comments ≈ 2.8× likes, funnel-driven.
**Solution:** the hook bank + hookscore gate (26 formulas, 5 checks) and
the outlier system — decisions from measured posts, not guesses.
**Content angle:** "stop guessing — your own account already told you
what works."

### P15. "My hooks don't hold anyone" — views that never buy
**Source:** @kallawaymarketing, Desire Hook Method (reel Dfc59rPsETD):
"Here are the three best algorithm hacks for Instagram. This will get
views, but only from low quality viewers that are likely never buy."
@liamjohnston.ai, "Claude just learned to fix your hook" (reel
De8I3VcjtKq): 808 likes, 2,862 comments.
**Solution:** the Desire Hook (outcome + relatable character) over the
info hook; the 5 specific hook templates are the DM reward. **Content
angle:** "an info hook gets views — a desire hook gets buyers."

### P16. "I can't find viral ideas" — the blank-page problem
**Source:** @createcontent.club, "Claude can find viral ideas" (reel
DfKUfIqDdLM): 980 likes, 2,941 comments. @jayantcreates.ai: "Engineers
viral reel ideas from what's already blowing up in your niche."
**Solution:** idea generation from what's already blowing up (outlier
mining; Ava's 5X outlier feed; the 11 reference profiles behind
Authority-30). **Content angle:** "ideas don't come from scratch — they
come from what's already blowing up."

### P17. "One month of content takes a month" — batching is the bottleneck
**Source:** @nategold.ai, "Claude is editing 17 reels for me in the same
time" (reel DfU8iIAty_c): 1,626 likes, 5,669 comments. @kylewhitrow,
"Claude Code edited 53 videos at once" (reel DYxEl77Ef5M — batch CTA
swap). @jayantcreates.ai, "One month of reels with 5 Claude skills"
(reel DetOhAUjY71): 300 likes / 1,100 comments.
**Solution:** batch systems (one master → many outputs; Marc 1→10
repurposing; trial-reel factory). **Content angle:** "a month of content
in one sitting — the batch is the system."

### P18. "I waste money testing editors" — hiring is a gamble
**Source:** @forseth.ai, "Day One: recreating Kallaway's style" (reel
DbJ0mp1sbGL, Jul 23, 2026 — 8.6K likes, 18K comments): "stop wasting on
testing editors and testing and failing… Be the creative director
yourself, plug in to Claude Code, automate the editing."
**Solution:** the pilot gate (judge one pilot before the batch) + Claude
as the editor with Muse QC. **Content angle:** "stop auditioning editors
— direct the edit yourself."

### P19. "I like a style but I can't replicate it" — style has no system
**Source:** @forseth.ai (same reel): the SERIES mechanic itself — one
named editor style per day, style name as the funnel keyword. @kienobifilms
(Tier S): CLAUDE.md style memory + cut-list-first approval + storyboard
gate. @thelifeoflibs: CapCut screen-record narration as style transfer.
**Solution:** style-by-reference (style sheet from 3–5 reels, approved
before the build; /inspiration verb). **Content angle:** "like the style?
Steal the system, not the video."

### P20. "The algorithm buries me" — reach feels rigged
**Source:** @mr.paidsocial (reel Db4PVCotlC4): "So the algorithm just
optimizes towards people who will never buy from you and you pay for all
of it." (Spoken inside the brand ad he edited — his ad copy, not his
claim; filed as observed.) @kelvin.martin1: "My algorithm is full of
videos like this" — the feed feels saturated with the same claim.
**Solution:** the algorithm-mastery file (this library's companion part):
the system measures engagement per viewer, not follower count; the small
audience test (A3) means a small account is not gated out. **Content
angle:** "the algorithm doesn't hate small creators — it tests them.
Pass the test."

## Executable steps
1. Weekly re-sweep: r/UGCcreators, r/VideoEditing, IG captions, creator
   complaint threads. Log new pains verbatim + URL + date; re-rank on
   frequency × intensity.
2. Hand the top 3 to Content Strategy (topics) and Sales (objections)
   each week. One pain per video — never two pains in one hook.
3. Every pain entry needs a solution on screen in the same video (the
   solution is the value; the keyword CTA is the capture). A pain with no
   solution on screen is a complaint, not content.
4. Never invent a pain point. No public source + quote = not in the file.
