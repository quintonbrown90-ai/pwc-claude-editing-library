# STRATEGY — The 70 / 20 / 10 posting mix: every piece of content has a different job [INFERRED reconstruction]

Source: @scalewithdemi reel Dfeterxk3m3 "Get clients from your content with
the 70 / 20 / 10 split" — 91.1s, talking head at desk + title card + end
card. Funnel: follow CTA only. Rebuild from the visible spec only, labeled

## The rule
70% relatable top-of-funnel (what + why), 20% tactical middle (how), 10%
direct invite (bottom). Every piece of content has a different job — a post
that does only one job is a bug, not a style. Q's launch already runs a mix
(proof-led / V5 launch / avatar); the steal is the explicit ratio + the
one-job-per-post planning rule.

## The three stages
- **Top (70%) — the what + why.** "Casts a wider net... think about this
  as like meeting at a party and you're trying to entertain people and
  create content that is relatable." Relatable stuff that gets them to click
  and really stop — her example: "saboteur voices" (the viewer recognizes
  their own thought). The stop-test for Q's relatable posts: the viewer
  recognizes their own thought.
- **Middle (20%) — the how.** "It's basically like going for a coffee.
  This is more like the tactical next steps." The recipe device: "Think
  about it as like a recipe, a video that talks about a recipe. So like
  there's step one, step two, step three." Q's tactical/how posts should be
  built as recipes, not lectures.
- **Bottom (10%) — the invite.** "Just 10% of what you're going to be
  posting because what this is is usually a direct invite to anything that
  you own" — a direct invite to the newsletter / waitlist / community.
  Only 10% is the direct invite.

## The analogy (funnel-teaching device)
Party / coffee / invite — three everyday scenes carry the whole funnel in
one sentence. Q's funnel teaching (value → WAITLIST capture) can use the
same three-scene shape.
