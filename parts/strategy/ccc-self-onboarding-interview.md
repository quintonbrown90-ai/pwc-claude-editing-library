## The rule
Before Claude writes anything for Q, it interviews Q — two rounds,
niche first, then posting schedule and workflow. The interview IS the
intake: no strategy doc, no script, no format pick starts until both
rounds are answered. Round 1's 8 questions are on screen verbatim; they
are the most directly adoptable system in the Batch 9 pull, because they
are the intake step every Q strategy doc should start with.

## Round 1: Your niche (VERBATIM-FRAME from f_008 — their words, Q's pre-fills in brackets)

> "Welcome in, [Q]. Before we write anything, I need to understand who
> you are, who you're talking to, and how you publish. We'll go in two
> rounds: your **niche** now, then your **posting schedule and workflow**.
> Answer in whatever format is easiest. Bullet points and voice-note style
> rambling both work.
>
> **Round 1: Your niche**
> 1. **What do you do, in one sentence?** Describe it the way you'd say
> it to a stranger at a dinner, not the way it's written on your website.
> [PWC pre-fill: Q is a full-time UGC & content coach who runs Paid With
> Content with his wife Kiya — creators under 10K learn to get brand
> deals and get paid with content.]
> 2. **Who exactly is your viewer?** Give me their job or situation,
> rough age, and where they're stuck right now. If you could only help
> one type of person, who would it be? [PWC pre-fill: creators under
> 10K followers who want UGC / brand deals and are stuck posting for
> free with no deal system.]
> 3. **What do they want, and what's stopping them?** Name the result
> they're chasing and the 2 or 3 biggest problems or wrong beliefs in
> the way. [PWC pre-fill: deal-ready in 90 days; blockers — no proof
> system, no pitch system, "brands only pay big creators" belief.]
> 4. **What's your proof?** List specific results you've gotten for
> yourself or clients, with numbers if you have them: revenue, views,
> followers, time saved, before/after. [PWC pre-fill: Q + Kiya's own
> receipts only — 1.5M+ followers combined, high-six-figure earnings,
> brand deals with Walmart, ESPN, NBA, NFL, and more; P-pack / RC-pack
> proofs, real numbers only, never invented.]
> 5. **Do you have a named method, framework, or system?** Even a rough
> one, like "the 3-step X process." Also list any tools you always
> recommend. [PWC pre-fill: Paid With Content — Sky-lane teaching,
> proof-led, the 90-day deal-ready system; tools: CapCut-native edits,
> ManyChat keyword flows, Skool Premium community.]
> 6. **How do you talk on camera?** For example: direct and blunt, calm

> you say all the time, or words you'd never use? [PWC pre-fill: direct

> 7. **Which 2 or 3 creators in your space do you watch or admire?** And

## Executable steps
1. Run Round 1 before any strategy doc: ask the 8 questions, pre-filled
   with Q's known answers above; Q corrects or confirms each answer.
   A pre-fill Q has not confirmed is a draft, never a fact.
2. Run Round 2 (scope above) the same way — platforms, frequency,
   filming setup, weekly time — then hand both rounds to the strategy
   doc (`parts/creator-systems/ccc-content-strategy-skill.md`):
   positioning one-liner → 3 formats → ideas → first batch of 5.
3. The interview re-runs when the answers change (new offer, new
   proof, new cadence) — the doc is rebuilt from the new answers, never
   patched by hand.
4. The interview is the router intake's front end: it feeds the same
   12-question intake (`parts/prompts/prompts-router-intake.md`), it
   does not replace it — the intake still asks what the interview does
   not (proof object + second, keyword flow live, must-not-appear).
