# VIRAL STRUCTURE LIBRARY — structures that are working now (mastermind #16)

**Owner:** Trend / What-People-Like Analyst (mastermind #16).
**Rule:** a structure earns its row with ENGAGEMENT EVIDENCE — likes and
comments recorded at pull, from the decode — or it sits in the watch list
at the bottom. View counts from the yt-dlp android client are never
cited (unreliable). Every structure is written as HOOK → BODY → CTA so
Claude can lift it whole. Structures are adapted to Q's lanes (Sky Form /
Adilet B1 / proof-led), never copied with another creator's words.
**Evidence base:** 630 DECODE.md files, `research/video-harvest/`; 60
with engagement recorded at pull, 37 with comments > likes (funnel flag).
Last refresh: Nov 8, 2026.

## The structures

### S1. "Killed-it" 3-step setup — the highest-engagement structure on file
**Evidence:** @kylewhitrow, "Claude Code just killed video editors"
(reel DV8sEXGjYEN, Mar 16, 2026, 72.5s): **25K likes, 31K comments**
— the top engagement in the whole harvest.
**Hook (0–6s):** category-kill claim, verbatim: "Claude Code just killed
video editors, you can now edit any video using Claude Code and I'm going
to show you how to get this set up in three simple steps."
**Body:** three numbered steps, each SHOWN while narrated (copy-this →
paste-enter → it installs; film the video; run the skill with file path
+ edit brief). One screen action per step, no step without its screen.
**CTA:** "comment the word REMOTION down below and I'll send you the free
setup guide." (GATED — the guide is the DM reward; never chase it.)
**Steal for Q:** the shape, not the claim — a category claim ("X just
changed Y") + 3 numbered steps each with its screen + keyword CTA. Q's
version: "Brand deals just changed for small creators — here's the
3-step pitch system" → steps on screen → PITCH.

### S2. "Nobody shows you how" — the gap reveal
**Evidence:** @agi_point, "4-step AI video editor" (reel De5VcwvFTMK,
Oct 24, 2026, 71.8s): **8K likes, 13K comments.**
**Hook (0–9s):** "Everyone is editing their reels with Claude Code now,
but nobody shows you how. So here's every step, every prompt, and every
tool to build your own AI video editor. For free."
**Body:** setup → tools → style → film-and-edit. The style step is the
differentiator: "Give Claude three to five reels from a creator you love,
or from your own page… it studies the fonts, cuts, captions, and music,
and writes a style sheet for you to approve."
**CTA:** "Comment EDIT, and I'll send it to you." (GATED.)
**Steal for Q:** the gap ("everybody shows the result, nobody shows the
process") is itself the hook. Q's record-along is this structure.

### S3. Self-demo in 40 seconds — the video proves itself
**Evidence:** @shedoesai, "Claude edited this video" / ChatCut (reel
Db5Z8cixSEJ, Aug 11, 2026, 38.4s): **7K likes, 15K comments.**
**Hook (0–7s):** "Claude edited this video. Watch this. This is ChatCut.
It's a plugin that turns Claude into a full video editor."
**Body:** the one-line prompt VERBATIM ("Keep the good takes, tighten my
pauses, add captions, and motion graphics every 2-3 seconds") → watch
the edit happen → the save-as-skill twist ("so Claude edits your video
the same way next time") → one-line install.
**CTA:** "If you comment ChatCut on this video, I'll send it over."
(GATED.)
**Steal for Q:** the video IS the proof — no claim without the thing
playing. Q's avatar videos should carry the same self-proof ("the video
you're watching was made by the system it teaches").

### S4. Style-recreation series — one style per day
**Evidence:** @forseth.ai, "Day One: recreating Kallaway's style" (reel
DbJ0mp1sbGL, Jul 23, 2026, 65.5s): **8.6K likes, 18K comments.**
**Hook (0–14s):** "This entire video was edited by Claude Code, and I'm
starting a new content series. This is day one of recreating other
editor's style… Today's style is Kallaway."
**Body:** show the style ("my face and my screen, everything on the
bottom"), name why it's hard for a non-editor, then the promise ("I'm
going to showcase how you can create anything that you literally
imagine") + the anti-waste line ("stop wasting on testing editors…
Be the creative director yourself").
**CTA:** "Hit the follow button, comment KALLAWAY, and I'll send you the
whole breakdown." (GATED.) The style name IS the keyword.
**Steal for Q:** the series frame (Day One…) creates the return loop;
the style name as keyword makes every episode its own funnel. Q's
version: "Day One: recreating [creator]'s style" — directly matches Q's
"absorb everybody's style" goal.

Look at this." → proof → WAITLIST.

### S8. Desire hook — outcome + character beats info
**Evidence:** @kallawaymarketing, Desire Hook Method (reel Dfc59rPsETD).
No engagement on file for this reel — the evidence is the METHOD's
adoption (folded into parts/hooks + kallaway-desire-hook part) and the
Triple Hook carousel (1,712 likes / 709 comments).
**Hook:** contrast, verbatim: info hook — "Here are the three best
algorithm hacks for Instagram" ("will get views, but only from low
quality viewers that are likely never buy") — vs desire hook: "Here's how
to go from 200 to 20,000 followers in the next six weeks."
**Body:** Step 1 — lead with the desired outcome ("the outcome you want
is follower growth and the direct signaling to it makes you lean in
more"). Step 2 — tie the outcome to a relatable character ("here's how
Tony went from 220,000 followers in the last six weeks using this three
step system"). Test: "If that solution is not obvious, a lot of people
will want to buy it."
**CTA:** "Comment 'Desire' To Get The Video" (GATED — steps 02–05
blurred on screen; the five templates are the DM reward).
**Steal for Q:** Q's second-person framing ("you could be making this")
is the house rule for proof content — but for HOOKS, first-person "how
I" is the measured winner (Shannon McKinstrie: a student gained 30,000
followers from one hook edit switching "how to" → "how I"). Tension to
resolve, not a contradiction: hook in "I", payoff in "you."

### S11. Before / after split — the mess → clean arc
**Evidence:** @doctablademd, Reel Room: RAW vs FINAL split-screen

**CTA:** comment-keyword funnel (see decode §5).

## Executable steps
1. When a video idea arrives, pick its structure FIRST (this file), then
   its edit (EDIT-ROUTER.md). A video with no named structure is not
   ready to brief.
2. Structures are adapted to Q's lanes, never copied with another
   creator's words — the HOOK formula transfers, the SCRIPT does not.
3. Weekly: re-rank on fresh engagement (every post >2× the creator's
   median). A structure with no fresh winner in 4 weeks drops to the
   watch list.
4. The funnel keyword in every CTA must have a live ManyChat flow BEFORE
   the post (ENGINE Step-0 gate). Gated DM rewards are never chased,
   bought, or scraped — the structure is the steal, the file is not.
