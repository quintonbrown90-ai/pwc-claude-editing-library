## How to use this index
1. When Q sends a new creator, check this index FIRST — decoded beats
   profile-only, and a [GAP] row means "decode next", not "adopt".
2. Niche creators (Section A) are the IDEA source — pains, hooks, topics,
   proof shapes. Editing teachers (Section B) are the CRAFT source —
   devices, systems, pipelines. The mastermind crosses them: a niche pain
   (Section A) told through a proven structure (Section B).
3. Follower counts are never quoted in content without their pull date.
   Small creators' receipts are cited as THEIR claims, never as Q's.
4. Gated funnels are noted only — never chased, bought, or scraped.
   The keyword column records what the creator's funnel IS, not an
   invitation to enter it.
