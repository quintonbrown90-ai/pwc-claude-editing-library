# ALGORITHM MASTERY — Instagram First (mastermind #19)

**Owner:** Algorithm Mastery Specialist (NEW — ordered by Q, Nov 8, 2026).
**Rule:** every entry is DATED + SOURCED. Instagram publishes signals and
predictions, never weights or view thresholds — this file has none either.
Anything without a source is flagged [UNVERIFIED] or [MYTH], never stated
as fact. When Meta's own words and a tool blog disagree, Meta wins and the

## 0. The one sentence
Instagram does not run one algorithm. Feed, Reels, Stories, Explore and
search each run their own ranking system, and a reel is never ranked in
general — only against the ~100 other candidate reels for one particular
viewer at one particular moment. (Instagram ranking explainer, May 2023;
restated in Meta's Reels system card, updated Nov 11, 2025.)

## 1. Instagram Reels — what Meta has actually said

### A1. The top three Reels signals (OFFICIAL — Mosseri, Jan 2025)
"The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes
and sends." — Adam Mosseri, head of Instagram, Jan 21–22, 2025, reported
by Social Media Today. He told creators to read them as RATES in Insights:
**average watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach.** Likes are
slightly more important for CONNECTED content (reach among followers);
sends are slightly more important for UNCONNECTED content (reach among
non-followers). Instagram's creators blog (Feb 2025) names the same set
plus retention, shares, likes and comments. No weight has ever been
published for any of them.
- **What it means for Q:** the edit buys watch time first (hook ≤2s, visual
  event ≤2s, no dead air), then earns the send (a line worth forwarding to
  a creator friend), then the like. In that order.

### A2. Reels Chaining — the four-stage pipeline (OFFICIAL — Meta system card)
Meta's Transparency Center system card for Reels ("Reels Chaining", last
updated Nov 11, 2025) describes ranking in four stages for one viewer at a
time: source candidate reels → predict how that viewer will react to each
→ order by a relevance score → show the highest first. About 100 reels
are scored per viewer. The card lists NINE predictions the system makes,
with the signals behind each — including: watch less than 3 seconds
(signals: times watched ≥3s, dismissals, skips within 2s of opening,
views with sound on); watch more than 95% of viewers of same-length reels
(a reel is compared with reels OF ITS OWN LENGTH); reshare; watch all the
way through; like; go to the audio page. The card is a sample of how the
system works, not a full specification — treat it as the floor of what is
measured, not the ceiling.
- **What it means for Q:** the first 3 seconds decide whether the rest is
  ever scored well (skip rate in Insights is the visible trace). Length
  is judged relatively — a 40s reel competes with 40s reels, not with 15s
  reels — so never stretch a reel to fill time, and never cut a strong
  60s idea to 15s out of fear.

### A3. The small-audience test (OFFICIAL — Instagram creators blog, Feb 2025)
A new reel is "initially recommended and shown to a small audience of
people we think will enjoy it" — among non-followers — and widened if
they engage. Instagram describes this as "designed to level the playing
field by distributing content based on its engagement, not just the
creator's follower count." Follower count is NOT ignored, only not the
deciding factor.
- **What it means for Q:** a small account is not gated out of reach. The
  first audience is strangers who look like his viewers — so the hook must
  land cold, with no assumed context, and the avatar must be named in
  line 1 (creators under 10K who want brand deals).

### A4. Skip rate replaced view rate (2025, OFFICIAL-adjacent)
In 2025 Instagram replaced view rate with SKIP RATE — the percentage of
views from people who skipped the reel during the first 3 seconds — and
added a retention graph showing exactly where viewers leave, including
during the opening. (Reported in iimagined.ai's Oct 2026 check against
Meta's system card; monty.fast, Oct 10, 2026.)
- **What it means for Q:** the retention read already gates on this (hook
  ≤2s, face on screen). The skip-rate number in Insights is now the
  single most honest verdict on a hook.

### A5. Originality gate — Apr 30, 2026 (OFFICIAL, reported Apr 2026)
Since Apr 30, 2026, accounts that post MOSTLY unoriginal content (other
people's photos, carousels or reels) are no longer shown in places where
Instagram recommends content. Reposts, watermarks and mostly-unoriginal
accounts lose recommendations. (Instagram creator guidelines, Apr 2026
reporting; reeldrop.io, Oct 2026.)
- **What it means for Q:** Q's own takes, Q's own proofs, Q's own voice.
  Reaction/breakdown reels must add real commentary, never re-post the
  target bare — the target plays FULL with Q reacting (router A4), never
  as a re-upload.

### A6. Reels up to 3 minutes can be recommended (OFFICIAL, Jan 2025)
A reel must be 3 minutes or less to be recommended to non-followers. The
limit was raised from 90 seconds in Jan 2025. Longer reels are not
recommended through Explore / the Reels tab.
- **What it means for Q:** the 90s ceiling is gone, but nothing in the
  harvest says longer performs better — the length rule is relative
  (A2). Keep the length the idea earns.

### A7. "Your Algorithm" — viewer-side controls (OFFICIAL rollout)
Instagram launched "Your Algorithm" for Reels in the US on Dec 10, 2025,
extended it to Explore in Apr 2026, and made it live across Feed, Reels
and Explore globally in English on Jun 10, 2026. Viewers can review the
topics Instagram thinks they care about, add new ones, or down-rank ones
they don't want.
- **What it means for Q:** the viewer now steers their own feed. A video
  that is unmistakably ABOUT one topic (creator pay, brand deals, UGC
  rates) is easier for a viewer to keep in their algorithm than a video
  that is vaguely "creator tips". Topic clarity is distribution.

### A8. Shares are having a moment in 2026 (OFFICIAL direction, no weight)
Instagram is putting more weight on shares in 2026 because "sending a
post to a friend signals real connection" and the platform wants to
"inspire content that brings people together." Instagram's own creator
guidance (creators.instagram.com, Jul 2026) reads saves next to shares:
"If saves are high but shares are low, the content might be valuable but
not shareable enough." No weight published. (Hootsuite, 2026; creatorflow,
Oct 2026.)
- **What it means for Q:** every video needs one sendable line — a number,
  a rule, a sentence a creator would forward to another creator with "us".
  The keyword CTA already drives comments; the sendable line drives sends.
  They are different jobs and a video needs both.

### A9. Hashtags capped at 5; caption keywords do the discovery (Dec 2025)
Posts and Reels are capped at five hashtags since Dec 2025. Caption
keywords now handle the discovery work hashtags used to. Meta's Reels
ranking explainer lists NO hashtag signal. (gyre.pro, 2026; reeldrop.io,
Oct 2026.)
- **What it means for Q:** captions stay short, SEO-focused, keyword-rich
  (already the house rule) — the caption IS the discovery surface now.

### A10. Feed and Explore signals (OFFICIAL — ranking explainer)
Feed: the five interactions Instagram looks at most closely are how likely
you are to spend a few seconds on a post, comment on it, like it, share
it, and tap on the profile photo. Explore: the actions weighted most are
likes, saves and shares. (Instagram ranking explainer, May 2023.)
- **What it means for Q:** carousels live or die on saves (Explore's
  save-weight) — the framework carousel's save is its distribution. Reels
  live or die on watch time + sends.

### A11. AI-generated people without a label lose reach (REPORTED, Aug 31, 2026)
Since Aug 31, 2026, profiles that show an AI-generated person without
Instagram's label get limited reach. [REPORTED — creatorflow.so, Oct 2026;
not yet verified against a Meta primary source in this pass. Do not cite
as Meta's words until verified.]
- **What it means for Q:** the avatar lane is a disclosed, branded avatar
  (Q's own avatar, his own voice) — never a fake person presented as real.
  Label + disclosure is the safe side of this line.

### A12. Mosseri's Dec 31, 2025 memo — read it as psychology, not code
In a year-end memo (Dec 31, 2025, posted on Threads), Mosseri argued that
as AI makes polished images cheap, raw and imperfect content becomes proof
a real person made it, and listed as a 2026 priority: "Continue to improve
ranking for originality." Coverage has upgraded this into a confirmed
ranking boost for "raw" content. **That upgrade is wrong:** the memo is
about audience psychology, not code — Instagram has announced NO ranking
change that boosts raw content. (creatorflow.so; gyre.pro — both flag the
distortion, Oct 2026.)
- **What it means for Q:** the human-made bar (Q, Nov 3: "don't make it
  look ai made at all") is an AUDIENCE-trust rule, and it happens to align
  with the originality direction — but do not claim the algorithm rewards
  rawness. The claim to make is "viewers trust it more", which is true
  and evidenced.

## 4. TikTok — secondary (OFFICIAL: TikTok Newsroom)
For You ranks on three buckets, in TikTok's stated order: (1) user
interactions — likes, shares, comments, and ESPECIALLY watch time /
whether you finish a video; (2) video information — captions, sounds,
hashtags; (3) device and account settings (weakest). Strong-signal
example: finishing a longer video from beginning to end. Weak-signal
example: viewer and creator sharing a country. "Neither follower count
nor whether the account has had previous high-performing videos are
direct factors." Reused content without anything new is ineligible for
the feed. Sources: TikTok Newsroom "How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou"
+ Community Guidelines FYP eligibility, checked Oct 11, 2026 (KeyMo;
kompozy.io Platform Algorithm Index, verified May 29, 2026).
- **Q status:** TikTok posting for @yerrr__q is STOPPED by Q (Nov 4,
  2026) until he lifts the hold. This section is reference only — it does
  not reopen TikTok.

## 5. YouTube Shorts — secondary (OFFICIAL: YouTube Help)
Two official categories: Viewer Personalization (watch history, search
history, subscriptions, likes) and Content Performance (whether viewers
click, watch, positively engage). YouTube officially weights
satisfaction-survey feedback — not just raw watch time — so a video
people rate highly can outperform one with more passive minutes. For
Shorts specifically: swipe-through rate, loop/replay rate and shares
replace click-through-rate as the core signals — third-party analysis
(Hootsuite, Shortimize), NOT in YouTube's official Help text; the
Shorts-specific mechanics are not officially published. A view counts
when playback starts, since Aug 24, 2026. Reposting a TikTok-watermarked
video to Shorts measurably hurts reach — export clean, unwatermarked
files per destination. Sources: YouTube Help; kompozy.io (May 29, 2026);
KeyMo (Oct 11, 2026); CreatorForge (Sep 10, 2026).
- **Q status:** Shorts is a mirror surface for Q's reels, not a lane of
  its own. The Shorts difference that matters: Shorts keeps testing
  cohorts for weeks (slow burn; videos resurrect at day 20–45), while
  Reels/TikTok largely decide a video's fate inside 48 hours.

## 6. Executable rules (what Claude does with this file)
1. Before any edit, name the surface (Reels / Feed carousel / Stories)
   and apply THAT surface's signals — never a generic "algorithm" claim.
2. The hook is bought for watch time: claim/curiosity ≤2s, face on
   screen, proof at 0:00. The sendable line is written into the script
   (one line a creator forwards to another creator). The keyword CTA is
   the comment engine. All three in every video.
3. Never write a multiple ("3× a like"), a weight, or a threshold into a
   script, caption, or part — Meta publishes none.
4. Check Account Status / recommendation eligibility before diagnosing
   any reach drop as a shadowban.
5. Reels ≤3 min. Original content only (A5 gate). Caption keywords, not
   hashtags, for discovery (max 5 hashtags, usually 0).
6. Judge funnel posts on DM captures, reach posts on watch time + sends
   per reach — never judge a funnel post by its likes.
7. Weekly: re-check the system card + @creators + Mosseri for changes;
   log the check date here; drop stale entries.
