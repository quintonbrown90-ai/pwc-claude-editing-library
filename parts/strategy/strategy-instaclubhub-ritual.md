# STRATEGY — InstaClubHub ritual model: membership rituals + scoreboard (PUBLISHED)

Source: @brock11johnson — Brock Johnson, ~1.1M (IG milestone post Oct 7,
2026: 10K likes, 1.8K comments). V6-WIDENED-NET.md Tier S #1. Public material
only; InstaClubHub curriculum itself is GATED — noted only, never chased.

## The rule
Steal the RITUALS, not the curriculum: 50,000+ members off IG alone; a $7
trial door; weekly trending-audio drops as a membership ritual; a public
growth scoreboard ("1,000/day"). Rituals + a visible scoreboard are what make
a community feel alive between launches.

## Executable steps
1. Weekly ritual: one trending-audio drop per week inside PWC (teaching
   members which sound to use + the hook to pair it with).
2. Public scoreboard: waitlist count + member wins posted weekly (real
   numbers only — the waitlist target is 20,000; the scoreboard shows the
   true count, never a projection).
3. The $7-trial door is a CONTRAST CASE, not an adoption: Q's Dec 7 cohort
   is waitlist-only, Premium $97/mo, doors closed until Dec 7. [NEEDS Q'S
   WORD] before any trial-door experiment is even proposed to members.

## Worked example (PWC)
Monday: "This week's sound + the hook that rides it" drops in PWC; Friday:
scoreboard post — "Waitlist: X,XXX · Wins this week: [real member win]".
Same ritual shape as Brock's, Q's numbers, PWC routing (WAITLIST).

## PWC skin
Second-person framing; every ritual post ends on the WAITLIST or CALENDAR
CTA. No manufactured urgency — the Dec 13 close is the only urgency line.
