## The rule
Money first, then the machine. The dashboard's value is stated in dollars
before any feature. One metric per slide; no slide does two jobs. "Claude
built this" is the product claim — the dashboard itself is the proof the
skill works.

## The 9 slides (their structure, PWC re-skin)
1. Money first — cash collected $63,423, pending $39,596, new clients 12
   (Q's version: Stan revenue / waitlist value — REAL numbers only).
2. Connected channels (IG 444K followers / 1.2M reach + YouTube 5.4K subs
   → Q's: @yerrr__q + Skool + Stan).
3. IG performance (followers, views, growth chart, ranked top posts).
4. Business metrics (show & close rate by source, reply 29% / close 67%,
   average LTV).
5. Cash again ($63,423 vs prior $87,887) + daily cash graph — the money
   number opens AND repeats.
6. Sales team performance.
7. LTV per client $7,168.
8. Customization (date-range selectors, conversion panels).
9. CTA: FOLLOW + comment DASHBOARD → Q's: comment WAITLIST.

## Executable steps
1. Monthly (or at cohort milestones): Claude builds Q's dashboard from real
   numbers (Skool, Stan, IG insights) → 9-slide carousel, one metric per
   slide, money first and repeated → CTA WAITLIST.
2. Every figure traces to a real source or it doesn't go on the slide.
3. Sequencing lesson: the money number opens AND repeats — the dashboard's
   value is stated in dollars before any feature.

as reported in the search index — UNVERIFIED, never repeated as Q's numbers.
