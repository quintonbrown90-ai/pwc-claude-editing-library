# GAP-FILL — Color management [Q didn't ask, mastermind added]

Source: color-grade playbook (`research/knowledge/COLOR-GRADE.md`) +
BRAND.md (palette sampled from the logo) + Sky D-devices. Mark: GAP-FILL.

## The rule
The grade survives IG compression and stays the same across a series.
Skin tone is never sacrificed for a look.

## The practice
- Work in Rec.709, limited range (16–235) — the range IG expects. Full
  range (0–255) crushes blacks on upload.
- PWC palette is the grade's anchor: navy #00214E shadows, teal #14A886
  mids, gold #FEE238 highlights/accents. NO RED anywhere — a reference's
  red maps to gold.
- Skin-tone protection: skin sits in the same hue band in every video in
  a series. Check the face on the export frame, not in the editor — the
  editor's preview lies about compression.
- Consistent grade across a series: one grade preset per series (P-pack,
  RC-pack, avatar lane). A new grade per video is a tell — the series
  must look like one shoot.
- What survives IG compression: bold flat colors (teal/gold/navy),
  high-contrast text, large shapes. What dies: fine gradients, thin
  lines, subtle texture — never carry meaning in a subtle gradient.

## Executable steps
1. Grade the master, export, upload a private test, view on a phone.
   If the gold goes muddy or the navy crushes, fix the grade — not the
   phone.
2. Contrast-check every text element against its frame in a dark box
   (Herk contrast-check, folded into the Color Agent).
3. One preset per series, named and filed — the next video in the series
   loads it, never re-grades from scratch.

## Worked example
P1 v9 / P2 / P3 (Nov 7–8): same navy/teal/gold grade across all three,
same cover template, same badge. They read as one launch on the grid.
