# GAP-FILL — Accessibility [Q didn't ask, mastermind added]

Source: WCAG 2.1 contrast guidance (general knowledge) + Q's caption
standards (word-by-word karaoke, never clipped, safe zones) + the
designed-captions skill. Mark: GAP-FILL.

## The rule
A deaf viewer must get the whole video from the captions; a low-vision
viewer must read every word. Accessibility is a reach multiplier, not a
chore — a large share of IG watches muted.

## Captions
- Word-exact karaoke from the actual voiceover (never paraphrased).
  Every word on screen, timed to the word (Adilet +0.013s measured).
- Minimum caption size: 54px on the 1080x1920 canvas for body captions,
  72px+ for the hook line. Never smaller — small captions are unreadable
  on a phone at arm's length.
- Every text element on fully opaque backing in its own clear zone (Q's
  rule) — no text over busy footage without a plate.
- Contrast: white or gold text on navy, never teal-on-teal or gold-on-
  gold. Minimum contrast ratio 4.5:1 (WCAG AA for normal text).

## Beyond captions
- Never carry meaning in color alone (a red/green "wrong/right" with no
  label fails — and red is banned in PWC anyway; use icons + words).
- The keyword CTA is spoken AND shown — a viewer who can't hear must
  still see "COMMENT WAITLIST."
- Flashing: no more than 3 flashes per second (photosensitive epilepsy
  guidance) — the motion density rule (visual event ≤2s) must never
  become a strobe.

## Executable steps
1. Mute the export and watch it: if the story doesn't land from
   captions + visuals alone, the captions are incomplete.
2. Contrast-check every caption frame in the QC pass (Color Agent owns it).
3. Caption size is a QC gate — a caption under 54px fails the render.

## Worked example
DEMO A (designed captions): 72 caption events, every word timed, hero
words in Anton ~320px, front captions yield during hero windows — the
muted watch still carries the argument.
