# GAP-FILL — Creator business basics [Q didn't ask, mastermind added]

Source: CREATOR-PAIN-POINTS.md (Talentir Sept 2026; r/UGCcreators;
Velena; laptopandcoffee) + Q's positioning (deal-ready in 90 days).
Plain terms, no legal advice — the line "get it in writing" appears in
every section. Mark: GAP-FILL.

## The rule
The audience's business questions have plain answers. Every answer ends
in a document, a number, or a sentence to copy — never in vibes.

## Rates (the #1 pain — 50% call it their biggest challenge)
- Know the market: platform-sourced $50–$250/video; direct-sourced up
  to $400+; ad-package deals $200–$500+; expert-tier $500–$1,500+
  (laptopandcoffee 2026 ranges).
- Your rate = base + usage + extras. Usage is priced separately: 30–90
  days paid usage = +20–50% on base. Perpetuity is the most rejected
  clause — never accept it without a fee that reflects forever.
- Minimums: r/UGCcreators enforces $150/video as a floor for posted
  collabs. Under that, the brand is buying your time for free.

## Usage rights (the most expensive surprise)
- Paid-ad usage is NOT included in a quoted rate unless the contract
  says so. Negotiate it explicitly, in writing, before filming.
- Time-box it (30–90 days), price it (+20–50%), and never grant
  perpetuity by default.

## Pitch structure (the 3-video rule)
- Send 3 top videos relevant to the brand — never a massive portfolio,
  never the wrong data, never a number that makes you look overpriced
  (r/UGCcreators agency hirer, 30K+ hires).
- Pitch = one line proving you watched their work + the offer + the
  number (or range) up front. Personalised email converts ~3–5× a
  templated blast (Talentir).

## Contracts in plain terms
- Brief before agreement. Price for the work, not the seconds. Revisions
  defined and capped (two rounds is the norm; a new script/angle/setting
  is a reshoot = a new fee). Rights in writing. Payment on time (net-30
  or 50/50; net-90 is a brand using you as a free loan).
- Get it in writing. Every time. No exceptions.

## Executable steps

   document on screen — no invented rates.
2. The Coach specialist's pain-point file is the source; this part is

3. Q's frameworks (pitch steps, rate minimums, filming routine) come
