# GAP-FILL — Copyright + licensing safety [Q didn't ask, mastermind added]

Source: Q's standing rules (local licensed library ~1,250 files, Nov 3;
Epidemic Sound DECLINED, never purchase/re-offer; no copyrighted artist
tracks on IG; memes/sounds pulled online per Q's Nov 6 order). Mark:
GAP-FILL.

## The rule
Nothing gets claimed or taken down. If the license is unclear, the asset
doesn't ship.

## What is safe
- The local licensed library (~1,250 files) — the sound source for built
  videos. Cleared CC BY beats in `video/beats_cleared/`, mixed ~-28 dB
  under speech, CC BY credit at the end of captions.
- Q's own footage, photos, screen recordings. Real creator examples with
  @handle tagged (Hormozi, Jessica Carter) — real posts, attributed.
- Memes/sounds: Q's Nov 6 order — "u just use the sounds and memes" —
  pull them online and use them. No licensing gate for sounds/memes ONLY.

## What needs clearance (or is banned)
- Copyrighted artist tracks (Drake, Lil Baby, Gunna, Young Thug, Rod Wave
  family as STYLE reference only — cleared instrumentals in that style,
  never the actual tracks).
- Epidemic Sound: DECLINED Nov 3. Never purchase, never re-offer.
- Stock/AI photos for proof: banned (proof stays real, never fabricated).
  Generic stock/AI pics allowed ONLY for illustrative spoken-word pop-ups
  in the batch-2 edit lane (Nov 3 exception).
- The hidden app: never named in public content (hard rule, Oct 2).

## How to credit CC-BY material
Credit at the end of the caption: track title + artist + "CC BY" + link
where the platform allows. Credit lives in the caption, never burned
into the video (it would break safe zones).

## Executable steps
1. Every asset in a build gets a license tag (library / CC-BY / Q-owned /
   meme-exempt / real-creator-attributed).
2. Any asset with no tag is quarantined until tagged.
3. A takedown/claim on a posted video → pull the audio claim first
   (replace the bed, keep the voice), never delete the video before Q
   decides.

## Worked example
Hype beats under built videos: cleared CC BY instrumental, Rod
Wave/Gunna family feel, -28 dB under speech, credit line in the caption.
His uploaded reels stay original-audio-only.
