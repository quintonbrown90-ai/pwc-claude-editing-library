# SCRIPTWRITING — interview-style outline skill: brain dump → interview → scope guard → outline (Wes McDowell, Tier S)

Source: Wes McDowell — "Claude Just Changed Making YouTube Videos Forever"
(https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ywvAeaFojo). INFERRED reconstruction from
the visible workflow; no files downloaded. The vidIQ MCP is YouTube-specific
but the pattern (real search data → opportunity score) transfers. Knowledge
entries V8-91, V8-130, V8-131, V8-171, V8-177. Corroborated by V8-120 (the
skill-building prompt) and V8-04-adjacent style-screenshot reuse.

## The rule
The skill interviews you. Brain dump → skill asks about stance, opinions,
stories → scope guard → research pass → outline with 2 hook choices. The
interview step is the differentiator: "opinions and stories… none of your
competitors can ever copy." Your stories are the moat — the skill mines them
before it writes anything.

## Executable steps
1. **Interview first, outline second.** The skill asks questions; you answer.
   Guided skill flow: activate the skill, no prompt needed — Claude asks
   where you're starting from (have audio, or need ideas + script?), then
   duration, then generates ideas. The skill drives the conversation; the
   user answers questions.
2. **Voice mode for the interview.** Speak, don't type, to avoid
   self-censoring. The unfiltered answer is the material.
3. **Scope guard in the skill.** "Figure out the scope, then always stay
   inside that scope" — a line worth adding verbatim to Q's Claude skills to
   stop rabbit-holing. The guard is set before the research pass, not after.
4. **Master-prompt pipeline: niche → sub-niche → 10 topic ideas → pick →
   duration → full script.** One prompt drives the whole funnel from niche
   discovery to a complete script. The duration choice comes BEFORE the
   script (render time scales with length). 10 original story ideas, pick by
   number — the menu is the interface.
5. **Director mindset: name the style, don't say "cool".** "If you only say
   'just make a cool video for me' to Claude, they don't know what the cool
   means." Screenshot a style you like (two words at a time, font + emphasis
   in brand color) → "I like this style, but I want you to change this color
   into my brand kit color." Be specific: cinematic route? Animation at the
   beginning?
6. **Three steps: cut → animate → direct; codify into the skill.** One shot
   gets you 80–90%; directing is what makes it production-level.
   Rubric-editor feedback: click the video, comment with timestamp + point,
   copy all comments, send in one go. Then codify the session (design
   systems + all feedback) into the skill and keep updating it.
7. **Trigger phrase.** "Help me flesh this out using the outline creation
   skill" — the explicit phrase that makes a skill jump into action.
8. **Slide deck = teleprompter.** Animated slides left, presenter notes
   right; talking head composited over the notes side in edit. For Q's
   talking heads: the deck IS the visual plan + the prompt, and the edit is
   just sync + cut pauses. Style screenshot once, reuse forever — one
   design-reference screenshot becomes the reusable brand system.

## Worked example (PWC)
Q's proof-led script: brain dump ("creators under 10K, brand deals, the
$1,900 week") → voice-mode interview (stance: small creators get paid;
story: the 500-follower week) → scope guard (one proof, one CTA) → research
pass (the real payout screenshots) → outline with 2 hook choices → deck as
teleprompter → record. Keyword WAITLIST.

## Human-made check
The interview transcript is read before the outline is approved: the stories
are Q's real stories, the stance is Q's real stance, and nothing in the
outline is invented. An outline that invents a story is rejected outright.
