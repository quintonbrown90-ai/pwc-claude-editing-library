## The rule
"One Claude project. Your rules, your brand, your voice, your customer,
your corrections." "Once the files are in, you stop prompting from
scratch."

## Executable steps
1. **The project (3 parts):** open the Claude desktop app and make a
   project — every chat inside it starts with what you put there.
   INSTRUCTIONS (rules every chat reads first: banned words, format,
   what to read) / CONTEXT (brand voice, brand kit, character bible,
   customer, corrections) / FOLDER (same files on your computer — edit,
   re-upload, done). "You stop re-explaining yourself."
2. **The instructions — the rules that never bend:** "Short and
   absolute. What is where, what never happens, and what to read
   first. Every chat in the project loads them before anything else."
   His WINS list: "No emojis. / No ex dashes. / No markdown tables. /
   Never guess a brand name. Ask. / Read the brand kit and the
   character bible before writing. / Log every correction as a new
   file." "Rules go in the instructions. Everything else lives in a
   file." (His "no markdown tables" is HIS instruction for HIS project
   — the harvest's own tables are its format, not his.)
3. **The brand voice (brand-voice.md):** "How the company sounds, what
   it never says, how it is named, when it gets mentioned, and when it
   stays out. Every caption, deck, and page reads from it." The prompt:
   [size, offer, client emails] → "Write the brand voice file." —
   what it sounds like / what we never say / how the company is
   mentioned / when it stays out. "You are who the reader follows.
   The brand is where the work happens."
4. **The visual kit (brand-kit.md):** "Palette, fonts, accent rules,
   logo placement. Claude reads it before it designs a slide, a
   graphic, or a page, so every frame matches." "Every slide in this
   carousel was built from this file" — the carousel IS the proof.
5. **The character bible (character-bible.md):** "Feed it the emails,
   call transcripts, and proposals you already wrote. It returns a
   voice profile: your tone, your phrasing, your rules." His is 1,041
   words. The prompt: [emails, transcripts, proposals] I wrote →
   "Build my character bible from these." TONE (default tone; when I
   open and close; how I handle objections; tone by context) /
   STRUCTURE (sentence structure; how I open and close; how I handle
   objections) / RESULT (sentences we liked as examples; "Nothing I
   did not write"). "Every chat in the project reads it before writing
   a word."
6. **The customer (customer.md):** "Your ICP, in one file." "ICP is
   your ideal customer profile. It is more than demographics. It is
   what they build, what they want, and what they ignore." Template:
   [role] who builds with [tools] / HAS SHIPPED (one thing ×3) /
   WANTS (the process or proof they are after) / RESPONDS TO (the
   resource that maps a workflow) / WATCHES BECAUSE (type / effortless
   outcome / the anti-persona). "Every post picks one customer before
   a word is written."
7. **The corrections:** "When a draft misses, write the fix once. What
   went wrong, why, and how to apply it. Claude reads it before the
   next draft." His example file: no-superstar-clients.md — "Never use
   superstar metaphors. Keep the literal claim." ("rocked land made it
   into a script. I have never landed from a rock.") Rules: strip
   metaphors before writing; if you reach for one, write what it
   actually means; read it out loud before delivering. "Say it once.
   The file remembers it for every chat after." "Build it once. Every
   chat after starts from it."

## Worked example (PWC)
Q's project: INSTRUCTIONS = the standing rules (second person, never
name the hidden app, nothing over the face, captions center-lower,
hide likes/views, exact exported files, WAITLIST routing);
brand-voice.md = Q's voice (plain talk, faith-aligned, "do your thing"
means latitude); brand-kit.md = PWC palette teal #14A886 + gold
#FEE238 on navy #00214E, Anton headline, logo top-left; character-
bible.md = built from Q's own scripts and captions ("Nothing I did not
write"); customer.md = creators under 10K who want UGC/brand deals.
Corrections file example: no-denim-photo.md — "Never use the denim
photo (09C6) again." Keyword WAITLIST.

## Human-made check
"Correct it once" only works because a human writes the fix — the file
remembers it, but Q is the one who says what went wrong and why.
