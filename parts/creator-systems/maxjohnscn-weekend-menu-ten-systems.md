## The rule

## The ten systems (verbatim purpose + Setup)
1. **Morning brief agent** — "Wakes up before you, checks your calendar,
   inbox and the news, then gives you one page to start your day."
   Setup: Project + calendar + mail connectors.
2. **Meeting prep system** — "Before every call, it researches the
   company, contacts and latest news, then gives you 3 talking points +
   1 risk to watch." Setup: Project + web search + saved prep prompt.
3. **Inbox triage** — "Sorts what needs your attention, what needs a
   reply and what can be ignored — then drafts the replies for you."
   Setup: Mail connector + rules in Project instructions.
4. **Prospect research pipeline** — "Give it a name and get a full brief:
   company, role, angle and opener — ready to send." Setup: Project +
   web search + fixed output template.
5. **The decision council** — "Four sub-agents argue your decision from
   different angles, then give you the verdict." Setup: One Project +
   four role prompts.
6. **Your second brain** — "Keep your notes, docs and screenshots in one
   place and ask questions in plain English." Setup: Project + uploaded
   files.
7. **Content repurposing engine** — "Give it one long piece and get a
   week of posts written in your voice." Setup: Project + voice samples
   + format rules.
8. **The money system** — "Give it your income, spending and goals and
   get a monthly plan that updates with you." Setup: Project + your
   numbers + monthly review prompt.
9. **Weekly review loop** — "Every Friday, it tells you what moved, what
   stalled and what next week looks like." Setup: Saved prompt +
   recurring calendar block.
10. **Your voice clone** — "Feed it 5 things you've written and it learns
    to sound like you, not a chatbot." Setup: Custom Style + writing
    samples.
Close line (his): "Pick one and build it this weekend."
