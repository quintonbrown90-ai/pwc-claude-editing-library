## The technique (as pitched in the reel)
One Claude skill, "/lead finder": paste your website → a **Scraper** pulls profiles across social platforms ("thousands of profiles at a time") → a **Profile Builder** writes a per-lead card carrying their **PAIN / DREAM / OBJECTION in their exact words** → an **Email Finder** resolves a reachable address → a **Sender** sends outreach that quotes the lead's own words back ("You said orders keep getting lost in my DMs. We fix exactly that."). The reel is animated mock-ups, not a live run; its demo lead reads as staged and its scale numbers are marketing, not measurement.

## Q's adapted version (the doctrine that governs)
Q keeps the middle piece and rebuilds the ends — **the exact-words lead card is the asset; the mass-send machinery is not**:
1. **Finder = paced, not a harvester.** Small batches through the connectors Q already has (Instagram search/read), human pace, on the surfaces where his avatar already talks: commenters on creator-economy/UGC posts, his engagement-rotation communities, creators posting brand-deal struggles / ghosting / rate confusion / no-deal-yet milestones. Avatar: creators under ~10K who want UGC/brand deals (ICP-PWC.md).
2. **Profile builder = the core build.** One card per creator: handle, platform, follower band, niche + PAIN/DREAM/OBJECTION as **verbatim quotes with source links — never paraphrased, never invented; a quote that can't be sourced doesn't go on the card** (LEAD-CARD.md, with CSV/JSON schema). The bank triples as launch research: hooks, captions, objection content written off real avatar sentences.
3. **Email finder = enrichment only.** A publicly published brand-inquiry email goes on the card. Nothing inferred, guessed, or broker-bought — and **found emails are never a send list**. Consented channels only: ManyChat opt-ins (comment WAITLIST) and Stan buyer emails.
4. **Sender = drafts on rails.** The mirrored-words opener structure is copied (quote their words → PWC as the fix → waitlist invite → stop), but for Q **every outreach is a DRAFT for his approval — NO auto-sending, ever**; approved sends are paced, personal, a handful at a time (OUTREACH-TEMPLATES.md).
