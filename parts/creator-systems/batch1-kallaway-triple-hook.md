## The rule
Every hook has three steps. A hook with one step is a headline; a hook
with three is a trap.

## The three steps
1. **Context** — immediate; audio + visuals + text aligned on the same
   claim in the first second.
2. **The Lean** — open the curiosity loop (name the payoff, withhold it).
3. **Contrarian Snapback** — the fishing metaphor: pull one way, jerk
   back (say the expected thing, then contradict it).

## Executable steps
1. Write every Q hook as three lines: context line, lean line, snapback
   line. The router checks all three exist before the render.
2. "Say it. Show it. Write it." — every claim rides three channels at
   once (spoken + shown + written).
3. Carousel version: one idea per slide, step headers, one line diagram
   per metaphor, CTA last. PWC remap: black→navy #00214E, red→gold
   #FEE238 + teal (NO RED law — red is the one element that does not
   transfer).

## Worked example (PWC)
Hook: "You posted 4 videos, then you quit." (Context) / "The 5th one
was the one that pays." (Lean — withheld: which one?) / "Quitting is
the system working exactly as designed — for the brands." (Snapback).
Keyword READY.
