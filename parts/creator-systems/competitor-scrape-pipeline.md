## The rule
Don't hunt for ideas by hand — run a pipeline. Track a list of creators,
take the top 3 of the past 30 days only, analyze them on five fixed
dimensions, transfer the idea (same idea, new angle for YOUR creator), and
output scripts. The pipeline automates the icon method and runs continuously
instead of manually.

## Executable steps
1. **Icon method (the manual form, and the validation filter).** 100K+ views
   / ≤100K subs / ≥5:1 ratio: the idea did the work, not the channel. A
   video that clears all three is a proven idea; find it, make your version
   for your people.
2. **Competitor pipeline config.** 20 videos per creator, top 3, past 30
   days only. Recency is a filter, not a preference — six-month-old viral is
   a depleted trend. Analyze hook + retention mechanism + CTA; output 3
   scripts per idea, pick the best.
3. **Five analysis dimensions (every viral video, same five).** Concept,
   hook, retention mechanisms, reward/payoff, script structure. Fixed
   dimensions make outputs comparable across videos.
4. **Idea transfer, not copy.** The concept keeps the proven idea and
   re-angles it for the creator's niche ("Most wealthy people never saw the
   property before buying"). The transfer — not the copy — is the skill.
5. **Filmability constraints in the config.** Tell the system what you
   can/can't film (alone, one location, no car) so it generates only concepts
   you can actually replicate. Unconstrained ideas get filtered by hand and
   the system gets abandoned.
6. **Comment-gap mining + own-history analysis.** Analyze competitors'
   comments at scale for gaps and audience language; scrape your own videos
   to find what worked and what didn't — the audience pattern is in your own
   track record.
7. **Brand context as ICP input.** Website + LinkedIn + Instagram scraped
   into the system so concepts know the ICP, products, offer, and sales
   funnel — not just the niche.
8. **Config-driven runs.** Creators + category + analysis instructions +
   concept context saved as a config; run per config. Retarget by asking:
   "Scrape TikTok instead of Instagram" — the tool is re-skinnable by
   plain-English instruction. Build tools that take direction, not tools
   that need rebuilding.
9. **Presentation-as-deliverable.** 12 pages: best/underperformers, topic
   performance, hook patterns from transcripts, title formulas that win. The
   deliverable is the intel, not the raw scrape. A 5-email newsletter
   sequence can be extracted from a single top-performing video.
10. **Holy trifecta (idea → title + thumbnail mockup + intro options).**
    Title = thumbnail = intro, one message. The thumbnail must carry the
    idea alone (payoff in ~3 words); the title adds who + what you get; the
    intro repeats it.

## Worked example (PWC)
Q's week: pipeline tracks 20 creators → top 3 of the last 30 days → five
dimensions each → 3 scripts per idea, filmable solo in one location →
strongest script films as a proof-led reel, thumbnail carrying the idea in
~3 words, intro repeating it. Keyword WAITLIST.

## Human-made check
Every transferred idea is read by a human before it films: same idea, new
angle — never a copy of the other creator's words, hook, or thumbnail. The
pipeline proposes; Q disposes.
