## The rule
SCRAPE (last 50) → OUTLIERS (5× median, 30 days) → DECODE (spoken hook +
on-screen text + caption hook → [bracket] template) → REPORT (card per
outlier + template leaderboard). The pipeline turns a competitor's catalog
into a template library in one run.

## Executable steps
1. **SCRAPE:** the last 50 reels of 2–10 handles (his command: `/spy
   @handle1 @handle2 …`). Scraping runs through Apify (pay-per-use,
   fractions of a cent/profile; free tier works) — dependency + cost noted;
   Q's version uses the harvest's own decoded catalog first (333+ decodes
   already on disk) before any new scrape.
2. **OUTLIERS:** rank by outlier multiple (views ÷ the account's own recent
   median); keep the 5×+ outliers from the last 30 days. Same math as the
   Nate outlier-multiple part — the two parts share the multiple, this one
   adds the 5× / 30-day cut.
3. **DECODE:** for each outlier, capture the spoken hook + the on-screen
   text + the caption hook, then reduce it to a [bracket] template ("[X]
   went from [base] to [result] in [time]").
4. **REPORT:** one card per outlier + a template leaderboard (which bracket
   templates repeat across outliers — the repeating template is the steal).

## Worked example (PWC)
`/spy` on 3 UGC-coach accounts → 150 reels → 6 outliers at 5×+ → decode:
3 open with a Result hook, 2 with a debunk, 1 with a list → template
leaderboard: "[You] went from [posting for free] to [first $1,000 deal] in
[90 days]" repeats twice — that template becomes next week's hook.
