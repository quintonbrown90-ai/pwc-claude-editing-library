## The rule
One Claude skill = five pillars. The system is the product; the video
is the map of the system.

## The five pillars
1. **Connect** — IG numbers (the proof that the system works).
2. **Content Research** — find what already wins in the niche.
3. **Hook skill** — 10 openings, ranked best to worst.
4. **Humanizer** — before/after with waveform ("sounds like AI →
   sounds like you").
5. **Trial Reels** — 5 versions, winner only (the 5th pillar is "MOST
   EFFECTIVE" — the open loop of the video).

## Executable steps
1. Open with the count-up proof (432→38,000; views→8M) under the claim.
2. Draw the pillar-temple diagram: five pillars, the 5th tagged as the
   withheld payoff — name it, don't show it yet.
3. Rank the hooks on screen (best to worst) — the ranking IS the value.
4. Show the humanizer before/after with a waveform, not a claim.
5. Close on the trial-reel filter: A–E, losers fall away, winner stays.
