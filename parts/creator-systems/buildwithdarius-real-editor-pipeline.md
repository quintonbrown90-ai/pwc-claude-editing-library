## The rule
"How to edit your videos with Claude Code? It's so easy." One folder,
one skill, one command — and an agent whose only job is views.

## Executable steps (the 5-step core)
1. **Install Claude Code** — "The desktop app is the easiest."
2. **Drop the skill in** — "drop my real editor skill into your skills
   folder." (Skill file GATED — see header. The step shape is the
   fold: the skill lives in the skills folder, not in the chat.)
3. **Make a folder with your raw video + 2–3 reels you love** — "and it
   learns your style." (Same references-folder shape as the mac-setup
   and 4-step builds — one canonical shape.)
4. **Type /real-editor and wait 15–20 minutes** — "It cuts the mistakes
   [and] dead air, adds captions that nev[er cover your face]…" (his
   words, as spoken). The 7-step variant adds: references as LINKS
   ("Copy the links of a few videos you love, paste them in, and let
   Claude Code study how they're edited. You only do this the first
   time. Every video after that, it remembers your style.") — and
   "If your AI is blocked from social media… use the Apify API" (named
   only, not adopted).
5. **The 4-step pipeline shape (Claude + Cuttr AI):** download the
   Claude desktop app → make one folder "My Reels" (Claude makes three
   folders inside: raw for footage, style for videos you love, out for
   the finished edit) → pick the style → run. Person 5: Editor does
   the same job as a role: "Picks up your raw talking video and the
   scenes. It cuts every pause on the waveform with Cuttr AI's Reel
   Editor Pro, adds captions, push-ins and real visuals, and outputs
   a finished, publish-ready reel for you to review and post." CUTS
   "Every pause" / CAPTIONS "Word by word" / ZOOMS "Key lines."

## The views agent
"Someone built a Claude agent whose only job is to get you views. So
you just tell it who you are and what you sell and it goes hunting for
you." The loop, bakery example: "it reads the numbers on every post
you've made, finds what's actually working and picks your next post.
Then it writes the hook, the script, and the caption in your voice so
it never sounds like AI." "And get this, Claude and Cutter make the
[video]…" His on-screen number ("MY VIEWS, ONE WEEK 24,176") is his,
not verified. The agent is the audit + the pick + the write, in one
loop — the audit finds what works, the pick is the next post, the
write is in your voice.
