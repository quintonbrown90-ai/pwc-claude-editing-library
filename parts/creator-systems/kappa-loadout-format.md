## The rule
A persistent top bar names the set and unlocks it one slot at a time: each
slot lights up ON the spoken name of its item, the counter ticks with it,
and the completed bar IS the ending payoff. Same family as the persistent
index (Marc) and the to-do checklist (David) — this is the BAR form.

## Executable steps (rebuild)
1. Bar: one row, 5 equal slots + counter. Locked = "?" dim; unlocked =
   lit icon + short name. Slot 5 = gold fill + "LEGENDARY" tag.
2. Each slot unlocks ON its spoken name (never before): icon swap +
   counter tick + tick SFX, 0.3s EASE OUT. A two-item beat ("+2 MORE")
   unlocks both slots on the one spoken line.
3. The completed bar ("LOADOUT COMPLETE ✓ 5/5") holds into the CTA —
   it IS the payoff; end card lands ≤1s after the last word (dead-air
   gate). No separate outro device.
4. DM cards (hook only): 3–4 floating cards with REAL words (real
   comments/DMs; blur per the complete blur law where a proof shows
   another person). Cards drift in slowly, never over the face.
5. Placement: designed-frame videos (no face) may ride the true top;
   talking-head Q videos put the bar in the top band at y ≈ 300 (below
   the top-280 keep-out, clear of IG chrome) — never over Q's face.

## Trigger rule
Use for any "here's the stack / here's the system" video: tools, steps,
systems, frameworks with 3–6 named parts. One slot per named part; the
LEGENDARY slot is reserved for the one part that saves money or time
(the cost-saving rule, e.g. Blender = 0 credits).
