## The rule
The edits are buttons to toggle, not a prompt to write. Three steps, no timeline: drop the raw footage in, toggle the edits on, ask for revisions in plain words.

## The menu (visible on screen)
- **Remove bad takes** (toggle)
- **Cut silences** (toggle)
- **Animated captions** (toggle)
- **Sound effects** (toggle)

"Then click edit video and watch it cook." No prompt, no timeline — click and watch. "POV: you got tired of editing so you made Claude do it."

## Executable steps (PWC)
1. **Drop the raw footage in.** One take, everything included (same dump rule as kingketo step 5).
2. **Toggle the edits on:** bad-take removal, silence cutting (Q's through-edit standard: no pauses, no dead air — the toggle is the switch, the standard is Q's), animated captions (PWC designed captions, teal/gold on navy, never over the face), SFX (sound on every in/out).
3. **Watch it cook.** The render plays; the human watches the whole video (Q's self-watch rule — Muse watches every render before delivery).
4. **Ask for revision, updates in the video editor:** the revision is a plain ask ("make the intro faster"), the editor updates. Same revision loop as the rest of the library (one line, one change, at the timestamp).
