# CREATOR-SYSTEMS — Claude watched me work: record-a-skill by demonstration (DRAFT — v9 bulk-b)

Source: @imanoubou "Claude just watched me work and now it knows how to do my job for me" (Dc3vWC7RcMz). Tier S. INFERRED reconstruction of the doctrine from the visible transcript; the Cowork app itself is named only — NOT downloaded, NOT scanned, NOT folded in.

## The rule
The skill is learned by watching the work. Do the task once, out loud, like explaining it to a friend sitting next to you — and the recording IS the skill. "A reusable system that runs the exact same way every time you need it."

## Executable steps (PWC)
1. Pick one repeatable job (one pass of an edit, one caption write, one QC check — one job, not the whole pipeline).
2. Do the job once, start to finish, narrating every decision out loud: "Talk through it out loud like you're explaining it to a friend sitting next to you." The narration is the spec — silent doing teaches nothing.
3. The recording becomes the skill: the steps, the decisions, the order. Save it as a skill (see skill-doctrine-save-it-as-a-skill.md) — the skill file is the recording, written down.
4. Next time, invoke the skill instead of re-doing the job. Every correction after that becomes a rule in the skill file (see jlee-correction-rule-loop).

## What does NOT transfer
- **The Cowork app itself:** named only in the video. It is NOT in the harvest, NOT scanned, NOT installed. The doctrine transfers; the app does not. Any future Cowork use needs its own license check + SCAN PASS + Q's explicit word.
- **Screen-recording as the capture method:** the video's capture is the app's record button. Q's lane captures the same way with the tools already in the library (a narrated run-through + the written skill file).

## PWC skin
The demonstrated job is always a Q job (a P-pack edit pass, a caption write, a QC gate) with Q's rules in the narration (teal/gold on navy, nothing over the face, words never clipped). The skill that comes out is a PWC skill, not a copy of his.

## Gaps
- The app's actual recording format, storage, and permissions are NOT in the harvest — unknown. The part is the doctrine + the narrated-run-through method, not the app.
