## The rule
The code is the easy part now; picking one + finding ten paying users is
the hard part. Every idea is one line: a named pain (who loses what) →
the tool → the buyer. The pain→tool→buyer line is the spec shape for
every Q offer/system idea (who hurts, what it does, who pays).

## The ten ideas (verbatim)
1. **Google Review Replier** — "Local businesses have hundreds of
   reviews sitting there with no reply. Build a tool with Opus 5.5 that
   drafts a reply in their tone, and charge monthly."
2. **No Show Reducer** — "Salons and clinics lose real money every time
   someone forgets their appointment. Ship a simple reminder and
   rebooking tool, then sell it niche by niche."
3. **Invoice Chaser** — "Freelancers hate chasing late payments, so most
   of them just don't. Build something that sends polite reminders
   automatically until the invoice gets paid."
4. **Listing Writer For Resellers** — "Vinted, eBay and Etsy sellers
   spend ages writing every single listing. Let them upload a photo and
   Opus 5.5 writes the title, description and tags."
5. **Proposal Generator** — "Agencies and freelancers rewrite the same
   proposal from scratch every week. Feed it a call transcript and it
   turns that into a ready to send proposal."
6. **Meeting Notes To Tasks** — "Most teams take notes and then nothing
   actually happens with them. Build a tool that pulls out the action
   points and drops them straight into their project tool."
7. **Competitor Price Tracker** — "Shopify stores rarely know when a
   competitor changes their prices. Track it for them and send a weekly
   summary of what's moved."
8. **Contract Red Flag Checker** — "Freelancers and creators sign
   contracts they don't fully understand. Build something that highlights
   the clauses worth asking about before they sign."
9. **Content Repurposer** — "Creators film one long video and then
   struggle to get anything else out of it. Turn it into clips, captions
   and posts with one upload."
10. **Creator Rate Calculator** — "Most creators have no idea what to
    charge for a brand deal. Build a tool that suggests a rate based on
    their views and engagement."
