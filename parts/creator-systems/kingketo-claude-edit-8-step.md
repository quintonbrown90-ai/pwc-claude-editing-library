## The rule
One folder, three subfolders, one line. The whole system is setup + teach + one line + revise + save. "Eight steps. Zero editing apps." (His "zero editing apps" framing is HIS — Q's edits are CapCut-native; the transfer is the workflow, not the deletion claim.)

## The 8 steps (his words, PWC remap in brackets)
1. **Make one folder. Put 3 inside.** B-roll & photos / music / raw footage. "Everything for the edit lives here."
2. **Get the desktop app.** Mac or PC, free download. "This ain't gonna work on your phone, playboy." [Q's lane runs on desktop; trial reels stay app-only for Q's posting.]
3. **Install 3 free repos.** "One cuts the video. One makes the graphics. One cleans your sound." [Repo names/URLs are NOT in the harvest — GAP. Install only after license check + SCAN PASS per SAFETY.md §6–7. Never install unvetted.]
4. **Teach it your style.** Dump in reels you edited and love — or steal his. His style rules (visible on the slide): "My face full size, never covered" / "Captions 1–2 words, yellow on the word I say" / "Music from frame 1, under my voice." [PWC remap: face full size never covered = Q's standing rule; captions 1–2 words with the SPOKEN word in gold (his yellow → PWC gold #FEE238), teal on the rest; music bed under the voice per the cleared-instrumental rule.]
5. **Dump in your raw footage.** "Mistakes, messes, restarts, pauses. Everything." His "AS FILMED" device: one take, 4:24, silences counted.
6. **Type one line.** "Edit this video like the examples." Then: "Checked it: 11 of 11 passed" — Claude checks its own work before you see it. [Q's lane: the self-check is Claude's first pass; Muse QC's the render before Q sees it — the 11 checks are his count, not Q's gate list.]
7. **Go back & forth.** A few times, with specific notes on the exact reel — his example note: "Make a version where you don't cut off my girlfriend's head." One line, one change, at the timestamp.
8. **Save it as a skill.** "Next time, just say: 'Use this skill to edit it.'" / "Every note I give becomes a rule. The next edit needs fewer notes." [Same doctrine as skill-doctrine-save-it-as-a-skill.md — this is the system that produces the skill, that part is the doctrine.]

## Executable steps (PWC)
1. One folder per video (the one-stack: date/title/keyword) with 3 inside: b-roll & photos / music / raw footage.
2. Teach step first: drop in Q's own finished videos (P1 v9 / P2 / P3) as the style sources — never another creator's video as the style to copy — plus the PWC style rules (teal #14A886 / gold #FEE238 on navy #00214E, no red, nothing over the face, captions center-lower).
3. Dump the raw take, everything included. One line: "Edit this video like the examples."
4. Revise by plain-English notes, one line per change, at the timestamp ("at six seconds, make this tweak"). Version, never overwrite: "Done. v2 is ready. v1 kept."
5. Save the working setup as a skill. Every correction becomes a rule in the skill file (see jlee-correction-rule-loop).
