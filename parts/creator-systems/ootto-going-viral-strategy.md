# CREATOR SYSTEMS — Ootto Going Viral strategy layer (repo distill)

Source: Ootto-AI/claude-content-skills repo (MIT, SCAN PASS) —
`skills/going-viral/SKILL.md` in `uploads/QUARANTINE/ootto-content-skills/`.
Distilled from 160+ analysed viral reels + the research consensus (Berger &
Milkman: sharing is driven by high arousal, not positivity; ~68% of people
share to signal who they are; saves = a promise to future-self; open loops
must stay open past the 7–15s checkpoint).

## The rule
Virality is engineered, not lucky. Pick ONE goal → manufacture the emotion
that drives it → build the hook + funnel around it. The reel manufactures
the feeling; the caption/DM delivers the value. This is the WHY above the
tactical skills (viral-hook-writer writes the hook; this decides what to
make it about and why).

## Executable steps
1. Pick ONE goal before scripting — it dictates everything. SAVE: relief +
   fear-of-forgetting (finite numbered system + "future-you will need
   this"; list assembles on one frame → "save so you don't lose it"; depth
   in the caption). SHARE: awe / indignation / status (open on the finished
   result then reveal how; "send this to someone still doing it the hard
   way"). FOLLOW: FOMO + aspiration (anti-falling-behind framing +
   result-first proof + a consistent brand). LEAD: greed + terminal
   curiosity (a named free deliverable withheld behind a keyword).
2. The hook: frame 0 IS the hook — the biggest element is already on screen
   AND moving (count-up number, finished artifact, UI mid-action), owns
   ≥40% of the frame, legible on mute (70–85% watch sound-off). Order:
   motion → lock → claim. The claim lands ~1.2–1.6s — never at 0s (no payoff
   yet), never at 3s+ (too late). Open a gap, not a fact.
3. Hold them: every line gets a visual (never a plain text card for a
   how/demo line); nothing static (low-amplitude idle motion throughout —
   a frozen frame is where people scroll); re-hook at the dips (~4s / 9s /
   15s) with a micro-loop ("but here's the part nobody tells you…").
4. Curiosity gap — promise → withhold → escalate → pay off: tease the
   payoff in the hook but don't show it ("Day 6 broke everything", "the 5th
   one is the one that 10×'d it"); make it an escalating series with a
   visible finish line (numbered beats — a countdown is unscrollable); each
   beat out-does the last; save the best for last. Pay it off FOR REAL —
   tease, never lie: an unpaid loop kills saves + shares and trains the
   algorithm that you waste watch-time. Never resolve the loop early.
5. Convert with ONE CTA — Save / Share / Follow / Comment-keyword. Keyword
   in the first line of the caption (most people never tap "more"). The link
   goes in the DM, never the public comment (links in comments get
   reach-suppressed).
6. Rotate the angle every post (contrarian / result-first / meta-self-proving
   / POV / numbered). The meta "this was made by AI" self-proving angle is
   strongest when it is TRUE — the post proves its own claim. The "N things
   nobody uses" listicle fatigues fastest; don't default to it.

## Worked example (PWC)
Goal LEAD (WAITLIST): emotion = greed + terminal curiosity. Hook: the
waitlist count-up moving at frame 0, claim at 1.4s — "Here's how you go from
posting for free to your first $1,000 brand deal in 90 days." Open loop:
"the 3rd step is the one nobody does" — withheld to the end, paid off with
the pitch system, CTA: Comment WAITLIST. Keyword WAITLIST.

## Human-made check
Optimize for identity signal, not raw value — a share has to make the
sender look smart/early/right. High-arousal beats positive: awe, anger,
FOMO over calm tips. Every loop opened must close.

## PWC skin
Q's loops close on Q's real systems (the 90-day path, the pitch system);
the withheld deliverable is always the WAITLIST capture, never a fake
secret. Credit: Ootto (MIT).
