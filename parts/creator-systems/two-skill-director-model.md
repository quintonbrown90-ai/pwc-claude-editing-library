# CREATOR SYSTEMS — the two-skill model: one skill understands, one directs

## The rule
"HyperFrames lets AI edit video and understand what is happening in
your footage" + "the editing skill turns Claude into a full creative
director." One skill understands the footage; one skill directs the
edit. Two skills, not one prompt.

## Executable steps
1. **Skill 1 — understand the footage:** HyperFrames is what "actually
   lets AI edit video and understand what is happening in your
   footage." It reads the raw clip — what is on screen, what is being
   said, where the beats are — before anything is cut. (HyperFrames
   is already Q's skill; this part does not install it, it names its
   job in the pair.)
2. **Skill 2 — direct the edit:** "the editing skill… turns Claude
   into a full creative director." It takes the understanding and
   makes the calls: animation, motion graphics, sound effects, color,
   branding — "the whole thing, without opening a single editing app
   or hiring an editor."
3. **The handoff between them:** "tell it what videos [you want] and
   hand it [the] footage" → it researches → pulls the assets →
   builds the edit. The understanding skill's output (what is in the
   footage, where) is the director skill's input (what to do with it,
   when). Neither skill does the other's job — a director that hasn't
   watched the footage is guessing, and an understanding pass with no
   director is a transcript.
4. **The question it answers:** "Is it possible to take a plain boring
   video like this and turn it into a fully edited one like this, with
   animation, sound effect, the whole thing, without opening a single
   editing app or hiring an editor? Of course it is, you just need
   these two Claude skills."

## Worked example (PWC)
Q's talking head, plain take: skill 1 watches it (where he points,
where the proof line lands, where the pause sits) → skill 2 directs
(pop-up on the pointing beat, proof on the claim line, cut the pause,
SFX on every entrance, PWC brand on every frame). The director never
covers his face; the understanding pass is what tells it where the
face is. Keyword WAITLIST.

## Human-made check
The two-skill split IS the check: if the edit puts a device where
nothing was said or shown, the understanding pass failed — and the
fix is in the understanding, not in nudging the device. Q judges the
finished video, not the prompt.

## PWC skin
Director decisions follow Q's standing rules (nothing over the face,
teal/gold on navy, NO RED, sound on every entrance, proof on screen at
the moment of claim). The gated exact prompt stays gated (GAP).
