## The rule
"Claude reads every reel… it finds what works. A hook in three
seconds, a visual on every line, a reason to comment."

## Executable steps
1. **Read every reel:** "Step two, Claude reads every reel." Not a
   sample — the whole catalog. Q's version runs on the harvest's own
   decoded catalog (333+ decodes on disk) before any new pull, and on
   public data only — never logged into Instagram.
2. **Split the catalog in half and count:** "The first 132 posts, only
   three past 10,000 views, and next 130, 31 past 10,000. Nine past
   100,000." The split is the finding: the second half outperforms
   the first 10× on the 10K bar. The audit reports the split, not just
   the top 10.
3. **Find what works (three things):** "A hook in three seconds, a
   visual on every line, a reason to comment." For each outlier, capture
   the spoken hook + the on-screen text + the caption hook (same DECODE
   shape as the spy-pipeline part — one decode shape, this is its
   own-page instance), then name the repeating pattern.
4. **Rank and report (Bradford AUDIT variant):** "Read every video
   you've ever created. Break it down with the analytics and rank them
   from top to bottom." Prompt 1 ranks every reel by views, shares,
   and the rest; prompt 2 (function described, text gated) turns the
   ranking into the next moves. The prompts themselves are GAP — the
   ranking shape is the fold.

## Human-made check
The audit finds what works — it never decides what to post. Q picks
the idea; the audit only says which shapes already earned the views.
