## The rule
Teach Claude who you are, then teach it your taste, then never explain
again. The narrated screen-record IS the brand kit.

## The three prompts
1. **Who You Are** — write the identity file (voice, audience, lane).
2. **Brand Kit** — screen-record yourself editing in CapCut while
   narrating what you are doing, so the pacing becomes the kit.
3. **Make It A Skill** — compile both into a Claude Code skill. Then
   drop footage and never explain again.

## Executable steps
1. Write the identity file first (one page: voice, avatar, lane, hard
   rules). Q's version already exists: BRAND.md + the fingerprint F1–F6.
2. Record a narrated CapCut session: Q edits one video while talking
   through every choice — pacing, cuts, captions, devices. That
   recording is the brand kit.
3. Compile identity + kit into the skill. Every future video starts
   from the skill, not from a fresh brief.

"proof first, face in the hook, device every two seconds, gold on the
number." That 10-minute recording becomes the P-pack brand kit — Claude
reads it before every P-pack edit.
