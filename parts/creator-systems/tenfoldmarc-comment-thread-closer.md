# CREATOR SYSTEMS — Comment-thread mockup closer (@tenfoldmarc)

Source: @tenfoldmarc system-pitch template (Oct 18–20, 4 deliveries) +
RABBIT-HOLE-MANIFEST.md. Closer device: "you: KEYWORD → You're first in
line" — the DM payoff shown on screen. INFERRED from the visible frames;
the Studio funnel is GATED, never chased.

## The rule
Show the DM payoff on screen: the viewer comments the keyword, the DM
arrives, the payoff lands. The closer TEACHES the funnel — it shows the
viewer exactly what happens after they comment.

## Executable steps
1. Build: a 3-second mockup at the end of the CTA — the comment thread
   ("you: WAITLIST") → the DM arriving ("You're on the list — here's the
   3-wins PDF") → the payoff card ("You're first in line ✓").
2. The mockup is a teaching device, not a screenshot: it shows the funnel
   working, in PWC skin, with Q's real keyword and Q's real deliverable.
3. The mockup lands AFTER the spoken CTA, never instead of it — Q says the
   CTA, then the mockup shows it working.
4. The payoff shown must be real: the DM the viewer actually gets (the
   3-wins PDF + waitlist capture) — never a mocked-up deliverable that
   doesn't exist.

## Worked example (PWC)
P1's close: Q says "Comment WAITLIST" → mockup: "you: WAITLIST" → DM
arrives → "You're first in line ✓ — the 3-wins PDF is on its way".
Keyword WAITLIST.

## Human-made check
The mockup shows Q's real funnel (ManyChat value-first → WAITLIST capture)
— the DM copy in the mockup is the DM copy the viewer actually receives.

## PWC skin
Navy DM card, gold check, teal keyword. Same device family as the kappa
DM-delivery mock variant (folded onto the drop-one-file-install part).
