## The rule
"AI only does what you tell it." The director has three traits: conviction
(know what the video is FOR), experience (know the words — "ease in" +
"posterize time effect at 12 fps" as the example of experience = knowing the
vocabulary), taste (know what good looks like). This is the cleanest
articulation in the batch of WHY Q's taste matters more than the tool — Q's
launch positioning ("the director, not the editor") in his own words.

## Executable steps
1. **Conviction first:** before any edit, state the video's job in one
   sentence (what the viewer does next). No conviction, no edit.
2. **Experience = vocabulary:** the director's edge is knowing the exact
   words for the move ("ease in", "posterize time at 12 fps", "whip-pan",
   "punch-in"). Q's prompt vocabulary is a teachable asset — keep it in the
   skill files, not in anyone's head.
3. **Taste = the signature:** "Your machine can copy your output. It is not
   your reason." / "This is not a mistake. This is your signature." (Giotto:
   one circle, no compass — mastery reads instantly.) The imperfect human
   touch (a pause, a gesture, a hand-drawn feel) is the signature AI cannot
   copy, and "perfect" is the tell. Q's anti-slop positioning, same shape.
4. **Giant gold numeral:** the section marker is a giant gold numeral (1 / 2 /
   3) beside the speaker — the number IS the section marker, no lower-third
   needed. PWC: gold numerals on navy.
5. **Citation paper:** prove a psychology claim with the actual source — a
   real paper page (Taylor & Brown, 1988) shown full-frame. Q's proof-led
   videos already show real proof; this is the citation-grade version — show
   the source, not a paraphrase.
6. **Line diagrams over B-roll:** a metaphor drawn as minimal line art over
   the talk (salt pinch / garlic) beats stock footage. Minimal diagrams that
   illustrate a metaphor.
7. **The honest aside:** a 1.6s trust beat mid-video ("no, I don't sell any
   courses") that disarms the sales objection. Q's equivalent: the honest
   aside inside a pitch.
