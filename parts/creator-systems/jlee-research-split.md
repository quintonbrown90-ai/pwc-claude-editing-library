# CREATOR SYSTEMS — 4-job research split (@jlee.mov)

Source: @jlee.mov reel DemOzcbiJOm, 12.4–28.9s (MEASURED). "4 research
sub-agents" (VERBATIM): (1) repo-legit check, (2) Anthropic's own pages,
(3) business audit off real files, (4) real people with links.

## The rule
Research split four ways — each sub-agent checks one kind of source, so no
single check can be fooled. The split is by source type, not by topic:
legitimacy, the official source, the real files, the real people.

## Executable steps
1. The four research jobs (one per source type): (a) LEGIT — is this source
   real (the account, the repo, the page — not a fake or a copy)? (b)
   OFFICIAL — what does the official source say (Anthropic's pages,
   Skool's help, IG's rules)? (c) REAL FILES — what do Q's own files say
   (the proof library, the harvest, the stack)? (d) REAL PEOPLE — who
   actually did this, with links (the creator, the case, the receipt)?
2. Each job returns its findings with the source attached (a link, a file
   path, a frame number) — a finding without a source is not a finding.
3. The four outputs are combined by the script job (the page-per-job chain,
   job 4) — the script cites the source type for each claim (official /
   files / people / legit).

## Worked example (PWC)
Researching "the best hook for a WAITLIST reel" → LEGIT: the reel is from
a real account (not a repost farm); OFFICIAL: IG's safe-zone rules for the
caption layout; REAL FILES: Q's own hook leaderboard (top 10 mined hooks);
REAL PEOPLE: Kallaway's Desire Hook (with the reel link). The script's hook
is built from all four. Keyword WAITLIST.

## Human-made check
The four jobs check, they do not decide — a human (Muse) still judges which
findings make the script.

## PWC skin
SAFETY.md §4 is the LEGIT job's standing brief (account age, followers,
stars, README, LICENSE, last push, cross-check across TWO sources).
