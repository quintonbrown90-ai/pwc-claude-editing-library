# CAMERA — whip pan + zoom kick (MEASURED)

Source: Adilet Exhibit B (motion) — `skills/adilet-system/MOTION.md` +
`research/adilet-teardown/` frame logs; punch-in pattern (Tella/Clipify,
MIT).

## The rule
The camera move IS the transition. Cut on the pan's fastest frame; never
put a cross-fade on top of a whip pan. Zoom on the word that carries the
claim, not on every word.

## Executable steps
1. **Whip pan:** pan between two framings; cut exactly on the fastest
   frame of the pan. The blur hides the cut.
2. **Zoom kick:** punch-in on the payoff word, 8–12 frames EASE OUT, hold,
   ease back. Never a slow drift — a drift reads as a mistake.
3. **Punch-in on stressed words:** zoom on the claim word only. If every
   word gets a punch-in, none of them land.

## Worked example
Line: "Five emails. Ninety-two thousand dollars." The camera sits wide on
"Five emails", then ZOOM KICKS on "Ninety-two thousand" — the number lands
with the punch-in, and the proof (the 5-email wall) is on screen with it.
