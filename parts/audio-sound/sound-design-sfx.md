# SOUND — dense, organic sound design (PWC standard)

Source: Q's standing sound rules (dense sound, SFX on every entrance and
exit) + Adilet audio chain + local licensed library (~1,250 files).

## The rule
Sound on every imagery entrance AND exit. Layered landings on card rows.
Accents on statement lines and number landings. Low cleared instrumental
bed under untouched voice. Dense but varied and organic — never a single
loop repeating, never silence where a device lands.

## Executable steps
1. **SFX map:** in the plan, every device entrance AND exit gets a sound.
   Card rows land with layered sounds (one per row, staggered 4–6 frames).
2. **Bed:** low cleared instrumental under the voice (~−28 dB under
   speech for built videos; his uploaded reels stay original-audio-only).
   Real cleared produced instrumentals, hard hip hop/trap family — never
   in-house synthesized loops (Q rejected those).
3. **No added music** on his uploaded reels unless he explicitly asks for
   that specific post. No copyrighted artist tracks on Instagram.
4. **Voice consistency:** repaired segments reuse the original video's
   single voice — never switch voices mid-way.

## Worked example
A 4-layer teardown: layer 1 spreads EASE OUT + soft whoosh; layers 2–4
SPRING in staggered 5 frames apart, each with its own tick; the number
landing gets a deeper accent. Bed sits low throughout; voice untouched.
