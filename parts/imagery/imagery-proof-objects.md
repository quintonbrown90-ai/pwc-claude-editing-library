# IMAGERY — show the document / proof objects (MEASURED)

Source: Sky D-devices + Adilet PACING (every claim names its PROOF OBJECT
in the same beat) + Q's show-it rule.

## The rule
Wherever a video says something concrete but does not actually show it, or
shows it only generically, cut away via transition to it actually happening
— a real illustration or a real-life example — then come back. Every claim
gets proof on screen at the claim second.

## Executable steps
1. In the script, every claim beat names its PROOF OBJECT in the same beat
   (the subject file, diagram, stamp, or count-up that will show it).
2. The proof object is real: a screenshot, a screen recording, a photo, a
   diagram built from real numbers. Never a flat AI plate pretending to be
   proof.
3. If the proof object does not exist yet, the video is not ready to build
   — source it first, don't ship the claim bare.

## Worked example
Claim: "Five emails made $92,000." Proof object: the 5-email wall (real
emails, brands blurred per the blur law, amounts sharp). On screen at the
claim second, full-screen in the first 2 seconds for proof-led videos.
