# IMAGERY — titles-behind-head: the title passes BEHIND the speaker (PUBLISHED)

Source: @kienobifilms (Tier S, BULK-DECODE-LOG.md Batch 1) + Viashaan hero-
behind-head 35.66s (verified timeline). The kienobifilms cue is the
titles-behind-head variant: the title is occluded by the head — depth, not
a paste-on.

## The rule
A title that passes BEHIND the head reads as IN the scene. The head never
moves to make room for it — the word goes behind. Use on 1–4 words per video
(DEMO A used 4: TOGETHER / COMPLETION / RELATIONSHIP / PARASOCIAL). Front
captions yield during hero windows — no collision, no overlap, no ghosting.

## Executable steps
1. Pick the hero word (the thesis word, the payoff number — never a filler).
2. Build the matte (facetrack + foreground alpha) so the word occludes
   cleanly behind the head; verify with a 3-frame strip at the crossing
   point.
3. Front captions go silent during the hero window; the hero word is the
   only text on screen.
4. Cap at one hero word per section; never repeat the same hero treatment
   3 events in a row.

## Worked example (PWC)
Hook title "BRANDS ARE PAYING" — the word "PAYING" (gold, Anton ~320px)
passes behind Q's head at the hook beat, front captions yield, then the
stream resumes. Verified on the exported file, in motion.

## PWC skin
Palette teal/gold on navy; hero words never OVER the face — they pass
BEHIND it. If the matte fails, the word is cut, never pasted over him.
