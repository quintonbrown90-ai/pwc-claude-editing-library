# IMAGERY — B-roll on the exact word (PUBLISHED)

Source: Tella B-Roll Finder (Louise de Sadeleer, MIT) + Herk
one-visual-per-phrase + Zeeuiux one-scene-per-idea.

## The rule
B-roll lands on the exact word it illustrates — not a second early, not a
second late. One scene per idea, fired on its trigger word.

## Executable steps
1. Tag each spoken phrase with its trigger word (the word the visual
   illustrates).
2. Place the B-roll cut exactly on the trigger word's start frame.
3. Moving B-roll, not stills: the B-roll moves (pan, tracking, or the
   subject moves). A frozen B-roll still reads as a freeze.
4. Sound on every B-roll entrance AND exit.

## Worked example
Line: "I filmed this on my PHONE." The trigger word is "PHONE" — the cut
to the phone-in-hand B-roll lands on "PHONE", not on "filmed". The B-roll
is a moving shot (hand raising the phone), 1.1–1.4s, then back to Q.

## BODY-LANGUAGE SCANNER RULE (Q, Nov 8, 2026 — standing rule)

For EVERY video, scan body language, motion, and intention — not just the
spoken words — to catch where a pop-up example asset belongs: pointing,
moving, suggesting, looking, and similar indicators that something goes
there. Missing pop-ups are found and inserted naturally where those
indicators point. The trigger is the gesture, not only the word: if Q
points, looks, or moves toward something while talking, the example asset
for that thing goes on screen at that moment.
