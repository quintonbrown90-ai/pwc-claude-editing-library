## The rule
Step 1: write the script — that's it. The script is the whole input; the
graphics come from the script, not from a second brief. Claude gets the video
AND the timestamps of when things are said, so every motion graphic is cut to
the right length — the graphic lands on the word, not near it.

## Executable steps
1. **Script first:** write the script. Three steps, in order: (1) write the
   script, (2) record it, then cut every pause — no captions or graphics yet,
   just you talking to the camera, (3) upload the clean cut to Claude.
2. **Export the first version:** export the clean cut (no captions, no
   graphics) and hand THAT to Claude. The first version is the input, not the
   finished video. State the settings on upload (Opus 5.5, Extra effort) —
   don't leave them to default.
3. **The graphics prompt (rebuilt spec):** watch the edited video, transcribe
   it with word timestamps, then make a motion graphic for every line where
   something is shown or explained — 2160x2160, 60fps, ~10 seconds each, pure
   white background (PWC: navy background, teal/gold graphics — the white is
   the source's, not Q's).
4. **Reality rule:** "Use the real UI of every app I mention and my real
   names, products, numbers and photos, no placeholders." Real UI, real names,
   real numbers, real photos — never fabricated. Q's proof rule, same shape.
5. **Numbered delivery:** save them all to one folder, numbered in script
   order, plus a list of where each one goes on the timeline, down to the
   word. Each graphic is cut to the line it goes under. Delivery = the folder
   + the list; assembly = drag them in.
6. **QC:** "Check every clip frame by frame before you send it." The output
   log is the return receipt: transcribed with word timestamps, built from
   your apps, rendered, checked frame by frame — done, all saved to one
   folder. In about 30 minutes every graphic comes back.
7. **The filling board:** the proof board starts empty and the graphics appear
   one by one — the filling is the proof, not the finished board.
8. **Close:** the no-email close — "(I won't even ask for your email)": the
   comment is the only step. The DM-delivery proof: show the DM arriving with
   the prompt.
