## The rule
Research is commands, not browsing. Five codes, one job each — the output is
always a cited report, never a vibe. The sharpest framing in the sweep:
"learn to THINK in the style, not copy it" (from his /build-style reel
DexAiZthd4j — style-learning without cloning; Q's rule: mirror grammar,
never exact content).

## Executable steps
1. **/fact-check (the QC command):** plot every claim in a script, check
   against real sources, flag what can't be backed up. Output card on the
   document itself: claim → ✓ TRUE / ✗ No source found. Directly serves Q's
   claim-verification rule (Muse verifies claims before upload) and the P/RC
   proof packs (real payouts, real creators).
2. **/last-30-days (the hook-research command):** what real people said about
   a topic in the last 30 days (Reddit / X / YouTube) — source material for
   hooks that use the audience's own words.
3. **/deep-research (the report format):** "full report with every source
   cited" — the shape Q's research briefs take. No claim without a source
   line.
4. **Hook ladder (the re-cut lesson):** one script, four hooks — "5 secret
   research codes" (curiosity) → "world's most powerful ad researcher"
   (identity) → "Claude keeps making stuff up?" (pain question) → "Stop
   Claude from making stuff up" (pain imperative). The imperative is the
   punchiest — a command, not a question. Lead with the pain version for
   Q's audience (creators burned by AI slop / hallucination).
5. **Slop detector (the concept, ported to video):** a "62 signs" checklist
   that scans a cut for AI tells (flat captions, template rhythm, dead space,
   generic B-roll) and fixes them before Claude calls it done. Q's anti-slop
   rule ("don't make it look AI made at all") made into a checkable list —
   variant note on captions-ig-humanizer (the human-made check).

## Worked example (PWC)
P1 script: /fact-check plots "2.8M" → ✓ TRUE (Adley's Nashville video,
sourced) → the spoken "2.9 million" line → ✗ No source found → corrected to
2.8 in the audio. /last-30-days mines "creators under 10K can't get brand
deals" in the audience's own words → hook bank.

## Human-made check
A report with an uncited claim is a draft, not a report. The /fact-check card
is produced on the script before Q records.
