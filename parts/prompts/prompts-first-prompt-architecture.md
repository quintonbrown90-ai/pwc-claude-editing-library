# PROMPTS — first-prompt architecture: 13 items, one prompt carries the system (PUBLISHED)

Source: Grow with Alex — youtube.com/watch?v=03BNd8ygnq8 (PROMPT-PATTERNS.md
§12–15; CLAUDE-CODE-WORKFLOWS.md §14). His verbatim 13-item prompt text is
shown on screen only, not spoken — the skeleton below is INFERRED from the
spoken system, not his quoted prompt.

## The rule
The first prompt is the whole game: if it is right, everything after is
easier. It packs 13 things — one job per file, exact branding, reference
specificity, the 5 basics, self-check, working rules, and ask-for-more-than-
a-reel. It took the creator "a lot of trial and error" to derive.

## Executable steps (prompt skeleton — fill each bracket, paste as prompt #1)
"Edit this raw talking-head reel. Assets, one job each: [brand image =
branding], [reference reel 1 = what to take from it, specifically],
[reference reel 2 = what to take from it, specifically], [animation MP4 =
context]. Branding: [screenshot of colours + fonts]. Rules: (1) remove all
dead air, ums, pauses, retakes; (2) the first 2 seconds must hook — [hook
spec]; (3) text stays inside safe zones, never out of bounds on any
platform; (4) pace: something changes every 2–3 seconds, never
overstimulating; (5) colour grade: [grade spec]. Before you show me
anything, check your work against these rules and your own past mistakes.
Working rule: preview ONLY the part that changed — never re-render the full
reel for a fix. Deliver more than the reel: a list of every choice you made
without asking me, plus a Q-sheet (timestamp + what's on screen)."
Working rules that ride with it:
- "Preview only the part that changed. Don't send me the full reel because
  I'll waste credits. Send me only the parts that you've changed, so that I
  can quickly compare them." (verbatim from Alex — put in EVERY prompt.)
- Feedback format, every round: Verdict / Fixes (numbered, each with a
  timestamp) / Keep (what must not change) / Show me (stills of the fixed
  parts before any video render).
- Recovery prompt when a render goes messy: "Add rules, not effects."
  Verbatim rules: "Stop — this is going the wrong way. New hard rules: no
  cutouts, no film grain, no heavy glow. My face always looks natural. One
  thing moves at a time. Captions never cover the subject. Show me five
  stills before any video." Hard-rules list also: "Real footage in every
  frame, natural skin, captions never on the asset, steady framing,
  readable on the word."
- Second-chat receipt reader: paste Claude's full reply into a fresh chat —
  "Explain what was done here. Flag anything alarming, anything off-topic,
  anything that drifts from my brief, and any mistake in the plan."

## Worked example (PWC)
Prompt #1 for a P-pack proof video: brand image = PWC logo + palette card;
reference 1 = Sky D2 lockup (what to take: the hold + hard-pop exit);
reference 2 = Adilet beat pacing (1.5–3.5s); animation MP4 = the count-up
card. Rules carry Q's gates (face-clear, safe zones, ≤2s visual events,
-14 LUFS). Deliverable: the reel + the choice list + the Q-sheet.

## PWC skin
Palette teal/gold on navy, NO RED; nothing over Q's face; CapCut-native
motion; the 5 basics are Q's (brand, hook, proof, keyword, close).
