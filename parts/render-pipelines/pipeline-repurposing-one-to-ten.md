## 1. Q's constraints (these override Marc — non-negotiable)
- **Trial reels are app-posted by Q himself.** Trial reels are
  APP-ONLY for @yerrr__q (no Trial option on desktop/web). Muse
  SUPPLIES the files (video + cover + verbatim caption, 3/day);
  Q posts from the Instagram app. NEVER auto-posting.
- **Carousels follow the Sky carousel system** (EDIT-ROUTER.md C1–C3):
  silent slides (no baked music — Q adds a trending track in-app),
  Adley's voice only in phone slides where present, framework states
  as slides, hard slide cuts.
- **Scheduling = Muse supplies files + Q posts.** NEVER auto-DM, NEVER
  auto-post, NEVER auto-schedule a publish. Marc's "without opening a
  single app" is explicitly NOT adopted.
- **Keyword flows live before any post** (ENGINE Step-0 gate): every
  trial reel's keyword must have a live ManyChat flow before it posts.
- **Newest-version rule:** the newest master is the only one that
  repurposes; a re-cut master re-cuts its 10 reels.

## The rule
One finished master video is the raw material for a week of output:
cut pauses -> add effects -> export (the 3-step checklist, §3), then
auto-pull the 10 strongest moments as trial reels, twin the framework
as carousels, and supply the pack for Q to post. The master is edited
ONCE; the outputs are cuts of it, never re-shoots.

## Executable steps
1. **Master first.** Edit the master to the router's pick (EDIT-ROUTER.md
   §11). No repurposing starts before the master passes QC on the
   exported file.
2. **Sift the transcript.** Pull the 10 strongest standalone moments:
   each must have its own hook (claim/curiosity ≤2s), one payoff, and a
   keyword CTA. A moment that needs the master for context is cut from
   the list — trial reels play cold.
3. **Cut the 10 trial reels.** Each is a complete loop: hook + one
   payoff + keyword CTA, ≤35s. One Marc effect per reel, picked by its
   trigger line (transcript-aware selection, EDIT-ROUTER.md §10/§11).
   Each reel gets its own cover (locked template) + verbatim caption
   (≤500 chars, keyword CTA + dual-CTA profile line).
4. **Twin the carousels.** Framework reels get a Sky carousel twin
   (one device state per slide, summary slide last, CTA slide last);
   proof reels get a proof carousel (real screens per slide, blur law
   on hidden-method proofs). Silent slides, no baked music.
5. **Supply, don't post.** Muse delivers the pack as files (3 trial
   reels/day + carousel twins as they are ready) with covers + captions;
   Q posts from the app. 10 reels = ~3 days of supply at 3/day.
6. **QC every output.** Each reel gets the same gates as a master:
   freezedetect zero ≥0.35s, ±0.15s transition audits, last-voice ->
   end-card ≤1s, face clear, keyword flow live. A reel that fails is
   quarantined, never supplied.
