## The rule
The folder IS the pipeline. 1_Incoming / 2_Completed / 3_Originals / logs.
Upload from the phone → ~60 seconds → output = final video. The AI does the
cut; you do the finish (titles + overlays).

## Executable steps
1. **Folders:** 1_Incoming (drop the raw take here — phone upload is the
   trigger) / 2_Completed (finished cuts land here) / 3_Originals (masters
   kept untouched) / logs (every run logged with timestamps).
2. **The cut (automatic):** A-roll edited together — silent parts, stutters,
   mistakes removed. Q's through-edit standard, same shape (no pauses, no
   stumbles, no false starts surviving the cut).
3. **The finish (human):** "All you do is add your titles and your overlays."
   The AI does the cut, the human does the finish — covers + captions are the
   finish (Q's locked cover template + Sky captions).
4. **The handoff:** output in 2_Completed is the review copy — Muse QCs it on
   the exported file (freeze / transition / dead-air / lip-sync / retention)
   before it reaches Q. Nothing posts without the cover on disk.
5. **The log:** every run logged — input file, cut list, output file, QC
   result. The log is the receipt.

## Worked example (PWC)
Q films a talking-head take on his phone → drops it in 1_Incoming → Claude
cuts the A-roll → Muse QCs the export → Q adds the cover + keyword CTA in
the finish → post. Same as Q's existing Claude lane, with the folder as the
trigger instead of the chat.

## Human-made check
The 60-second claim is theirs, not Q's standard — Q's renders are judged on
the export, not the clock. The log must show the QC pass, not just the speed.
