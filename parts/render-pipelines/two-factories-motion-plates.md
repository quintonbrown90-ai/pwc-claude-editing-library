# RENDER-PIPELINES — two factories + motion plates (@aliabdaal, Tier S)

Source: Ali Abdaal — "I Tried Fully Automating My Video Editing With AI (RAW RESULTS)"
(https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C0jOKDJtK9s). INFERRED reconstruction from the
visible workflow; no files downloaded. Knowledge entries V8-15, V8-16, V8-17,
V8-19, V8-20, V8-21. Corroborated by B16 (transparent-background render →
CapCut overlay) and B40 (style-sheet step).

## The rule
Two factories, one composite. The A-cut of real footage and the motion plates
are separate workstreams; compositing is a third, small step. Never ask one
system to do both jobs at once.

## Executable steps
1. **Factory 1 — the A-cut.** Real footage only, cut to the script. No
   graphics, no plates, no music decisions here.
2. **Factory 2 — the motion plates.** Each plate is a 3–8s self-contained
   rendered movie: PNG sequence with transparency, 4K, frame-rate matched to
   the footage, its own file in one plates project. A plate is a movie, not
   a still.
3. **Composite last.** A-cut on V1, plates as B-roll/overlay, composite at
   the end. If a plate is wrong, only the plate re-renders — never the cut.
4. **Plan before build.** Storyboard / contact sheet first. Seconds to fix a
   plan, hours to fix a render.
5. **Film a short script for a short video.** Never ask AI (or a human) to
   mine 45s from 5 min of rambling. The script comes first; the A-cut
   follows the script.
6. **Treat the AI like a human editor.** Give it the task you'd give a human
   at the same instruction level — a 60s test from 10 min of footage, not
   "pick the best 45s from 10GB". The 60-second test is the pilot-gate shape.
7. **Learn the professional words.** "Motion plate", "contact sheet",
   "A-cut", "J-cuts / L-cuts" — naming the thing is what makes the prompt
   precise. Keep a glossary in the skill so Claude and Q share the same terms.
8. **Code-video = cheap mind-changing.** Rebrand / re-skin by swapping the
   logo + design tokens and re-rendering plates. The skin is data, not work.

## Worked example (PWC)
Q's proof video: A-cut of Q talking (Factory 1) + payout-count-up plate and
proof-wall plate (Factory 2, teal/gold on navy, PNG + alpha) → composite in
CapCut. The $28,800 plate re-renders alone when the number changes; the
A-cut never re-opens. Keyword WAITLIST.

## Human-made check
The composite is watched whole before delivery — plates land on the spoken
line, nothing over Q's face, and the A-cut still reads as one continuous
human take.
