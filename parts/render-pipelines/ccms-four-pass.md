# RENDER-PIPELINES — CCMS: Cut, Color, Motion, Sound — four passes, in order (Content Creators, Tier S)

Source: Content Creators — "How to Edit Videos With ChatGPT + Claude (Start
to Finish)" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=guFwFzQplJI). INFERRED
reconstruction from the visible workflow; no files downloaded. Knowledge
entries V8-132, V8-133, V8-134, V8-135, V8-136.

## The rule
The edit is a sequence of four named passes, not one blob: Cut → Color →
Motion → Sound, in that order. A pass never starts before the previous pass
is locked. If a pass is skipped, the edit is unfinished, not "done faster".

## Executable steps
1. **Cut.** The rough cut first: silences, retakes, false starts out. Two
   frames of dead air before the person starts talking, no more. Audio
   prompt for the pass: "adjust the balance and audio levels of all clips.
   Make sure the voice is clear, consistent, and loud enough for YouTube
   without any clipping or distorting."
2. **Color.** Grade after the cut, never before — grading footage that gets
   cut is wasted work. One house look (PWC teal/gold/navy) applied as a
   pass, not per clip.
3. **Motion.** Motion graphics timed with speech, smooth in/out, brand
   colors: "Whenever I'm describing something important, I want motion
   graphics to appear on screen to help visually explain what I'm talking
   about… timed with what I'm saying and have smooth animations as they
   appear and disappear." The graphic appears on the described thing.
4. **Sound.** SFX + music last, after the picture is locked. Music bed and
   SFX placed on the picture, never before it exists.
5. **Captions on a video track, not a caption track.** Individual text
   blocks, adjustable timing/text; separate colors into separate layers /
   tracks (blue on track 4, white on track 3). The track structure IS the
   customization.
6. **Turn the finished edit into a skill.** "This video is great, please
   turn it into a skill where I can say edit this video and it will go
   through the whole editing process, start to finish." Fully automatic vs
   check-ins is a setting, not a rebuild — and TEST RUN FIRST: the test run
   found "a ton of errors". Then: drag footage, say "edit video", done.
7. **Reference video for captions.** Hand Claude a reference video for the
   caption style; the style is shown, not described.

## Worked example (PWC)
Q's talking-head take: Cut (through-edit, ≤2 frames dead air at the open) →
Color (house LUT at 50%) → Motion (proof plates + pop-ups on the spoken
thing, teal/gold/navy) → Sound (SFX on every in/out, bed under speech).
Captions on video tracks (gold keyword track, white body track). The finished
edit becomes the skill for the next take. Keyword WAITLIST.

## Human-made check
Each pass is watched before the next starts. A motion pass that hides a cut
problem is sent back to Cut — never papered over with a graphic.

## PWC skin
Teal #14A886 + gold #FEE238 on navy #00214E across all four passes. NO RED.
Caption tracks: gold for the keyword, white for the body — never red.
